# C04-M08 — Assessment and delayed check

**Question:** Can you transfer what you learned to a new inferential question and retain it later? **Prerequisites:** M01–M07 artifacts, actual source access, assistance/exposure logs. **Outcomes:** independently reconstruct an existence argument, interpret a time/soul passage outside the paper's focus, compare reason/revelation/predication with context, and reconstruct a key inference after delay.

## Readings and evidence

Use the exact editions and ranges in M01–M06; no new author or unassigned doctrine is tested. The assessor checks [outcomes](../outcomes.md), selects one unexposed [assessment form](../assessment.md), and locks the source locations before the attempt. For al-Ghazali this requires the actual Khalidi mathematics or logic passage within pp. 59–98. A contents record is not a substitute for that passage. An unavailable assigned source leaves the affected source criterion unresolved.

## Teaching explanation and procedural example

An essay gives sustained evidence on one problem. It cannot by itself show that you can reconstruct another method or distinguish another author's question. A new task tests transfer by changing what must be inferred, not by requiring an unread book. Revising after feedback establishes improvement on that attempt; a different uncoached item is needed to establish independent competence after a consequential failure.

**Invented evidence example:** Learner R has an excellent Gaunilo essay and a corrected Augustine exercise, but no independent time interpretation. The essay supports existence-argument work; it does not fill the time/soul row. The corrected exercise is supported practice. R still needs the designated fresh time task. A total score averaging all three would conceal that gap. This example teaches record keeping and supplies no answer to the exam.

**P8:** Fill the evidence grid with actual filenames, exact passages, assistance, status, and one next action per unresolved row. Check five citations: one from each of M02–M06. If the edition is different, state it; if a source was never read, remove the attribution and mark the task pending. This is preparatory practice, not a rehearsal of assessment answers.

## Independent assessment and continuation

Complete three 25-minute fresh samples, one per essential group, followed by a 20-minute dialogue or typed equivalent. Instructions and neutral criteria are in the released form. Use assigned primary texts and a citation sheet; no AI content help, hints, examiner notes, prior essays, or prewritten responses. Time allocations are planning estimates, not speed grades. Separate source location preparation from intellectual help. If a prompt or diagnostic answer was seen, use the unexposed alternate or an equivalent assessor-written task recorded before the attempt.

Feedback names the criterion, evidence, checked source, and next action. Repair a weak group using its source module, then retest with a changed question. Do not pass a row merely because the rest of the portfolio is strong. A source unavailable to the assessor must remain marked provisional, even when the prose sounds plausible.

After **four to six weeks or two later modules**, complete the 30-minute delayed task for the least secure group, including the short essay-inference recall. Its 30 minutes of feedback/repair are also reserved here. Record both against Course 4, even if undertaken during a later course. If retrieval fails, return to the identified inference and use another uncoached question after repair; log excess time separately.

## Six-hour budget

Portfolio/reading/citation audit 45m; fresh forms 75m; dialogue 20m; feedback 60m; targeted repair/retest 60m; final revision and record 40m; delayed task 30m; delayed feedback/repair 30m = **360m / 6h**. The 75-minute source-specific grid overrides a generic ninety-minute suggestion. All prior module independent checks and the PC-30 changed case have their own existing allocations and are not charged here again.

Otherwise name the exact unresolved row/source/repair. Finishing these authored materials is not a learner completion claim or external validation.
