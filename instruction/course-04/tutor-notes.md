Use the active [tutor rules](../tutor.md) and [calibration set](../tutor-calibration.md). Run the calibration against the actual tutor/model after material changes before relying on assessment.

## Source-bound preparation

Ask for the learner's exact module/task, edition, range, attempt, time available, and desired help. Review the source ledger before giving a textual judgment. Authoring checked Pusey, Deane, Dominican Aquinas, selected older Maimonides chapters, Cambridge metadata, and a bounded alternate al-Ghazali passage; it did not inspect Chadwick, Williams, Pines, or the Khalidi assigned excerpts. Do not inherit confidence from the source catalogue's existence. Record which passage you can actually see.

For M04, confirm whether the assigned Book of Salvation excerpt contains the required flying-person formulation. Inspect before locating, rather than attaching the most familiar version from another work. If absent, tell the coordinator exactly what was inspected and what criterion remains blocked. For M05/E3, record anthology pages of the mathematics/logic passages and introductions. A lawful short excerpt or checked page image can support a focused judgment; keep it with the private learner record, not in the distributable teaching files. No full text is needed in the repository.

## Coaching sequence

Start with the attempt. Name one or two consequential issues, give the relevant criterion and actual passage, and ask for a revision. A textual error (“this is an objection, not Aquinas's answer”) differs from an interpretive dispute (“the necessity premise may require more argument”). Explain that difference. Model a different example from the task; do not rewrite the learner's assessed paragraph. Fade a scaffold from labelled premises to an unlabelled map, then to the independent changed case.

Use these diagnoses to choose remediation:

| Pattern | Repair before a fresh task |
| --- | --- |
| Faith/reason binary replaces author-specific analysis | Rebuild M01 ledger with speaker, method, authority and scope in separate cells |
| Present memory is treated as the past event existing now | Draw the event/act distinction at one instant; reread XI.20 and XI.27–28 |
| Gaunilo's island is dismissed without locating the targeted premise | Compare P3 maps line by line; identify the special description and modality |
| Unawareness of the body is treated as immediate proof of separability | Separate epistemic and metaphysical claims; find or identify the missing bridge |
| Al-Ghazali's criticism becomes rejection of every discipline | Return to one actual branch and distinguish acceptance of method from its application |
| Negative theology becomes silence about everything | Identify an exclusion, its warrant, and what it does not describe; inspect I.58–60 |
| Five ways become one “everything has a cause” proof | Reconstruct one chosen way using its own starting point and dependence premise |
| Evil reply is treated as an explanation of every suffering | Mark possibility, actuality and explanatory warrant separately |

## Feedback and record

Write `criterion → evidence in attempt → source support/uncertainty → next action`. If challenged, revisit the exact passage and inference. Correct an unsupported judgment. Agreement among tutors or models is not verification. A learner may reject or defend an argument; assess understanding and reasoning, not profession of belief. Do not attribute a clinical, scientific or historical claim to an invented instructional case.

Check the three essential rows separately. Keep the M04 source requirement visible even though the final E2 forms use Augustine to avoid overlap with the essay. A final sample is independent only if answer keys, content hints and rehearsed responses were excluded. Mark a source-bound judgment provisional if the source was unavailable to the assessor; do not convert the status through a confident style score.

## Access, audio and pacing

The timed allocations plan workload, not speed or fluency. Allow breaks, enlarged text, screen readers, speech-to-text, or an equivalent typed dialogue. During independent work, an access assistant can read text or operate equipment but cannot select premises, explain a term for that item, or edit the argument. Agree aids before the attempt and record them. A text-to-speech session must preserve article/objection distinctions and negations; verify citations against text afterward. A written equivalent is always available.

Use only Module 8's 75m final samples and 20m dialogue once. The delayed 30m attempt and 30m feedback belong to Course 4 even if performed during Course 5. M06's two-hour PC-30 packet is already included. Record actual excess time rather than silently expanding the baseline. Close the record with competent/pending/repair for each criterion and a named next step.
