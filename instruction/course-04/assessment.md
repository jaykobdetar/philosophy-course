# Course 4 — Independent forms

**Assessor releases one unexposed form.** This public file is not secure exam storage. Reading a prompt, rehearsing its case, or reading its diagnosis is exposure; record it and use the alternate form or a new equivalent from the passage bank. Do not use an item as coached practice and later call it fresh. Answers and diagnostic notes are kept separately in [examiner notes](examiner-notes.md).

## Conditions and source preparation

Each form has three **25-minute** samples: existence arguments; time/soul; reason/revelation/predication. Aim for 200–350 words per sample or an equivalently structured accessible response. The 75 minutes follow the exact [Course 4 grid](../../current/assessment.md). Permit the assigned primary texts and a citation sheet naming edition and locators. Prohibit AI content help, task-specific hints, examiner guidance, prepared paragraphs and prior essays during the attempt. An assistant may operate access technology without supplying content. Save the original attempt before feedback.

Prepare the source card before releasing the prompts. Record `edition | exact pages/sections | paragraph beginning and end | inspected by/date | exposure`. The Aquinas and Augustine section locators below are fixed. For al-Ghazali, the assigned range is **Khalidi pp. 59–98**, but the internal pages of the mathematics and logic discussions are **not verified in authoring**. The assessor must locate them in the actual copy and add the precise page and brief paragraph boundaries to the card. Check that the mathematics selection includes both the transfer-of-prestige issue and the rejection-of-demonstration issue; for logic include the account of method and its application. If absent, stop the affected item and seek a bounded source correction. Never use an arbitrary excerpt or the publisher preview as a substitute. Maimonides is **Pines, Guide I.58 and I.60**, with actual pages added from the copy. If either assigned translation is unavailable, E3 remains unresolved; a logically plausible answer cannot count as a verified textual interpretation.

Use a passage outside the paper's central focus for E2. All permitted papers concern Avicenna, Anselm/Gaunilo, or negative theology, so the Augustine items satisfy this rule. Do not replace them with the paper's already rehearsed Avicenna passage. Questions assess only assigned texts; detailed accounts of occasionalism, later scholastic analogy, or unassigned modal systems are not required.

## Form A

**A1 — Existence, 25m.** Source: Aquinas I q.2 a.3, main answer, second way; I q.2 a.2 reply 3 for the stated limit. An invented critic proposes an endless series of causes, each receiving its causal power from another, and says: “Since every member has a cause, the series needs no further explanation.” Reconstruct Aquinas's response to an ordered series, identify the precise premise the critic disputes, and state one further claim about God that this short argument alone has not established. Do not substitute “everything has a cause.”

**A2 — Time/soul, 25m.** Source: Augustine XI.27–28, sound/impression and recitation discussion. Invented case: a reader silently rehearses the rest of a poem after an audio recording abruptly stops. A commentator says, “Because she still expects the ending, that future sound already exists.” Reconstruct the relevant temporal distinction and explain whether the inference follows. State what your interpretation establishes about experience and what it leaves open about temporal reality.

**A3 — Reason/revelation/predication, 25m.** Sources: the locked Khalidi mathematics selection within pp. 59–98; Maimonides Guide I.58. Invented claim: “Al-Ghazali's criticism of philosophers and Maimonides' rejection of positive essential attributes are both blanket refusals to learn by reasoning.” Use a precise claim from each passage to assess this assimilation. Identify their different immediate questions, one limit to the comparison, and one contextual caution grounded in the assigned introductions or the text's stated argumentative setting. Do not supply a general history of Islam and Judaism.

## Form B — reserve or targeted retest

**B1 — Existence, 25m.** Source: Proslogion 3–4 and Anselm's reply 1 and 3, Williams. Invented claim: “If we add ‘cannot be thought not to exist’ to a fictional character's description, the character must exist.” Reconstruct the relevant Anselm inference, explain which additional premise or understanding of the description matters, and assess whether the invented claim preserves that reasoning. A reasoned objection or defense can both meet the criteria.

**B2 — Time/soul, 25m.** Source: Augustine XI.20 and XI.27–28. Invented case: two readers expect the same recitation to take different lengths of time; one later says, “Because their expectations differed, the event must have had two different completed durations.” Reconstruct what the present expectation is a present state of, and explain whether the conclusion follows. State one distinction between a measure, the mental act of measuring, and what is measured. Avoid assuming an unassigned modern theory of time.

**B3 — Reason/revelation/predication, 25m.** Sources: locked Khalidi logic selection within pp. 59–98; Maimonides Guide I.60. Invented claim: “Knowing the rules of proof guarantees a metaphysical conclusion, while any negative statement is automatically an advance in knowledge.” Explain how each assigned passage bears on one half of the claim. Identify what would count as an adequate warrant, distinguish the two projects, and provide one source-grounded contextual limit.

## Neutral criteria for every form

Identify the actual argument or distinction, its speaker and conclusion; attach traceable locators; expose a necessary inferential step; answer the specific new question; qualify the conclusion where the passage does. A source-grounded rival interpretation is acceptable. Source fabrication, conflation of voices, or importing an unassigned doctrine to avoid the question requires repair. A polished response with the wrong target is not competent. Timing is adjustable for access; correctness, reasoning and source integrity remain the criteria.

## Twenty-minute conversation or typed equivalent

After saving the three responses, the assessor selects one inference to challenge and asks: “Which premise would you revise if this objection succeeded?” Then ask the learner to explain one translation-sensitive or source-scope decision from the essay, with the passage available. Clarify the attempt rather than introducing an untaught doctrine. A typed exchange or paced recorded response to sequential prompts has the same intellectual requirements. Record the actual prompts and responses. Content coaching during this exchange cannot retroactively make a weak written sample independent.

## Delayed forms — release after four to six weeks or two later modules

Use the least secure essential group; allow **30m total**. First, for 10m without essay or notes, state the essay's key inference and one vulnerability, then use the primary text to check one locator. For the remaining 20m, allow primary texts/citation sheet and release one of the following changed tasks. No live content help. The original Course 4 budget also reserves 30m feedback/repair.

**D1, existence:** Summa I q.2 a.1 reply 2 and a.2 main answer. An invented reader says, “Understanding what a proposed cause would be and observing an effect provide exactly the same evidence for that cause's existence.” Distinguish the routes and identify the burden each leaves. Do not recycle the endless-series response.

**D2, time/soul:** Augustine XI.23–24 and XI.28. An invented conductor imagines a familiar melody while every instrument is silent and stationary. A critic says that no comparison of durations can occur. Explain the relevance and limits of Augustine's arguments about rest and attention, without claiming an empirical experiment proves his account.

**D3, reason/revelation/predication:** locked Khalidi mathematics selection and Guide I.60. An invented reader learns that one claim by an authority was false and therefore rejects every demonstrated exclusion that authority made. Explain whether the rejection is justified, what must be checked individually, and why the same answer does not make al-Ghazali's and Maimonides' projects identical.

If all delayed cases were exposed, the assessor uses the same exact passage bank with a new inference and records the new prompt before release. A different label on the same coached case is insufficient. Record pass/repair/pending per criterion and actual minutes to Course 4; any work beyond the six-hour estimate is additional recorded time.
