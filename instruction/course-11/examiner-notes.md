# Course 11 examiner guidance — open after independent submission

**Contains diagnostic anchors.** Learners should not consult this file during independent tasks. The cases are original; source-specific anchors are interpretive expectations to check against the actual editions. They do not replace the texts. A materially different reading can be competent if it explains the relevant evidence and survives the objection. Missing primary access means `source-dependent`, not a fabricated textual verdict.

Use [assessment](assessment.md) and [outcomes](outcomes.md). Record form, exposure, assistance, edition/locator, actual minutes, each criterion and next repair. Fresh tasks are source-open, without AI or hints. Dialogue may clarify the learner's reasoning, but cannot retroactively turn an unsupported written answer into evidence of independent performance. Make the added evidence explicit. Retest with a different case after feedback.

## Final 11A fresh and alternate anchors

**A8-F1/X1 — concern/cultivation.** A competent response distinguishes family bonds, role obligations, exclusion from concern and distributive treatment. It explains an Analects-grounded reason and a Mozi16-grounded challenge with locators. F1's unsafe supply creates a public-role/harm problem; X1's tailored escorts may serve every child's needs. Neither “family” nor “impartial” yields a verdict alone. Strong work identifies whether the purported dispute survives a distinction between inclusive concern and equally intense affection. Do not credit an unsupported claim that Mozi eliminates every relationship. Do not decide the chapter's exact requirement from the title “Universal Love.” Repair: reconstruct the premise linking the norm to the disputed allocation, then retest with an unused case.

**A8-F2/X2 — nature/effort.** In 6A1, verify who advances the willow/cup analogy and how Mengzi's reply challenges the mapping; confusing the opponent's analogy with Mengzi's endorsed theory is consequential. X2 requires the direction/forcing distinction in 6A2, not a repeated 6A1 answer. Compare Xunzi23's actual definitions and explanation; shared need for cultivation does not prove agreement, and different English headlines do not prove total opposition. Translation criterion: name both renderings and exact locus or the translator's explicit commentary; explain the philosophical consequence; limit the claim to the evidence available. A fluent hypothetical contrast without verified sources leaves the row incomplete. Strong work separates term scope from substantive developmental psychology and identifies what evidence would distinguish readings.

**A8-F3/X3 — perspective/comparison.** F3: the transition between butterfly and Zhuang Zhou supports a question about standpoint and transformation; it does not automatically establish the nonexistence of an external world or the equality of every belief. X3: the gourd's alleged uselessness depends on a customary use; a changed use challenges that assessment without abolishing every standard. Require a text-sensitive account rather than these formulations verbatim. A productive cross-tradition comparison identifies a specific question and an already studied source; a limit explains a difference in target, method or argumentative role. “Both are skeptical” is too thin. A learner may challenge a skeptical reading with evidence. If the selected episode was central to the essay, use the alternate or record another complete assigned episode; do not relabel it fresh.

## Final 11B fresh and alternate anchors

**B8-F1/X1 — racialized experience.** F1: the desired resolution does not simply erase either identification; verify the actual paragraph. A competent answer situates divided self-relation in racialized evaluation and identifies aspiration, description and historical framing without pretending the passage supplies an independent historical proof. X1: the examiner must document the Philcox passage and its surrounding context before grading. Separate narrated/reported voices from endorsement; identify the philosophical inference and the empirical claim the passage cannot alone establish. A statement that experience is “subjective, therefore worthless” misses the task. Strong work identifies how form and voice contribute to the argument and a real interpretive ambiguity.

**B8-F2/X2 — person/community.** F2 holds membership, recognition and good faith fixed while introducing two incompatible legitimate obligations. Explaining a person’s development through community does not by itself rank communities or confer unconditional authority on one; rejecting that authority inference does not by itself refute communal formation. Require the connecting premise for each elder’s conclusion and check the reconstruction of Menkiti and Gyekye against actual loci. A competent answer may identify an unresolved priority question instead of inventing either author’s rule for emergencies. Strong work explains whether the two accounts leave different room for individual judgment without equating judgment with social independence. X2 instead separates public honor from actual conduct and motive; check the distinct achievement/recognition inference. Unsupported claims that Menkiti endorses every communal command or Gyekye rejects all communal formation require repair.

**B8-F3/X3 — method/institutions.** The relevant Wiredu reasoning must support the diagnosis of asymmetric evidence or illicit comparison; a namedrop is insufficient. F3's individual benevolence need not refute a chapter2 institutional mechanism. X3's repeal matters only to the extent it changes the mechanism; other claims may survive or require new evidence. Require one actual Mills claim, its textual locus and a clearly stated empirical dependency. Strong work names what would genuinely weaken the account. Do not attribute a shared doctrine to all Africana thinkers or infer every historical mechanism from the invented case.

## Paper, dialogue and readiness

For A7, require a genuine specialist interpretation, checked primary support and a translation note with identified evidence. For B7, require a checked specialist source and a method note that changes scope, comparison or inference; Gyekye's later SEP writing is a participant's further interpretation, not neutral independent confirmation. McCarthy's review remains unread until access is secured; do not invent its objection. Papers may reject the teaching hypotheses when supported by the actual texts.

For the dialogue, select an objection to a concrete premise in the submitted paper and record it. Competent performance identifies the question, answers with reasons and can narrow a claim; repeated prepared phrases are insufficient. Accessible typed exchanges preserve philosophical demand. For the revision memo, distinguish cosmetic changes from a changed premise, interpretation, evidence choice or scope. An independently weak row cannot be cleared solely by a polished revision.

Completion requires all essential rows, revised paper, source integrity and a delayed sample. If the delayed sample reveals a failure, reopen the affected group. Time beyond the eight-hour assessment reserve is additional and logged. This is a local educational judgment, not accredited or empirical certification. Learner pilot, specialist review, tutor calibration and rendered accessibility remain pending unless separately evidenced.
