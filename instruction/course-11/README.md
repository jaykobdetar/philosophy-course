# Course 11 — sustained study of a global tradition

Choose **one complete 140-hour track**: [11A Classical Chinese philosophy](a/README.md), after Courses 1 and 6; or [11B Africana philosophy](b/README.md), after Courses 1 and 8. Both tracks are fully authored here. Taking both is 280 hours; shared procedural guidance carries no additional hours. The accepted [course selector](../../current/courses/11.md) remains authoritative.

This packet teaches close interpretation, disagreement within a tradition, and responsible comparison. It does not certify Chinese-language competence, knowledge of every African society, or mastery of traditions outside the assigned texts. The learner works through explanations, original worked practice, independent changed cases, feedback and substantive revision. The culminating paper is 2,000–2,400 words. Each essential outcome also needs independently competent fresh evidence.

## Start here

1. Read the selected track's README and [source audit](source-audit.md). Secure the exact editions one module at a time. A record in the catalogue is not proof that the text is available.
2. Create a private portfolio with `reading-log`, `glossary`, `practice`, `independent`, `revisions`, and `evidence-grid` folders. Keep copyrighted excerpts and personal drafts there, outside the distributable course.
3. Begin Module 1. Each module provides its own prerequisites, source boundary, time budget and next step. Record actual time, assistance and any previous exposure to test prompts.
4. Use [tutor guidance](tutor-notes.md) for supported work. Keep [examiner guidance](examiner-notes.md) closed until submission of the relevant independent task. A tutor may explain a different example, but must not compose the learner's assessed paragraph.
5. Complete the [outcome map](outcomes.md), [assessment](assessment.md), and delayed check. A row with missing source access or unsupported interpretation remains unresolved.

## Audio and access

Use lawful text-to-speech or an audiobook of the exact assigned translation if available. Listen in 15–25-minute segments, stop to state the argument aloud, then mark the book/chapter or page in a written source log. Audio time replaces the corresponding reading time; it is not extra required listening. Every spoken task may be submitted as a written or typed exchange with the same questions. Before citing, check spelling, wording and locator in an accessible text; timestamps alone are insufficient. No specified audiobook has been verified. A recording in the historical audio collection is not automatically the required reading.
