# C11B-M08 — Independent assessment and delayed transfer

**8 hours. Guiding question:** Can you identify and repair an inferential mistake on a fresh case while preserving the actual text? Prerequisites: B1–7 and access to all assessed passages.

## Outcomes and assigned work

Demonstrate the [three essential groups](../../outcomes.md): racialized experience, person/community, and method/institutions. The paper supplies depth but cannot replace independent understanding of the other core debate. Sources remain the exact editions and bounded ranges from Modules 1–6. No new whole work is assigned. An assessor without the relevant primary passage must label textual judgments provisional.

Use [assessment](../../assessment.md): three thirty-minute written fresh tasks, a twenty-minute dialogue or equivalent typed exchange, targeted repair/retest when needed, and a twenty-five-minute delayed task four to six weeks later or after two later modules. Declare any earlier exposure to the prompts. The examiner uses an unexposed form and keeps [anchors](../../examiner-notes.md) unavailable during the attempt. Text access is allowed; AI content help and prepared responses are not.

## Teaching: evaluate the inference, preserve the disagreement

A fresh response can reach a different conclusion from the tutor and still be competent. What matters is accurate interpretation, a defensible inference and an honest account of the evidence. Conversely, agreeing with the tutor does not rescue a response that collapses moral achievement into basic standing or generalizes one author to a continent. The assessment asks you to show the reasoning, not display approved political vocabulary.

Build a row-by-row evidence record. Identify a supported module artifact, the new independent sample, a precise unresolved criterion and the next repair. If a source is missing, write its title and range; “more research needed” is not actionable. If an independent answer confuses historical explanation with phenomenological description, the repair should target that transition. A generic reread of every source would not tell you whether the problem was resolved.

## Worked practice — original evidence audit

A fictional learner's B4 table separates four senses of personhood. On a fresh case the learner infers that an infant lacks rights solely because the infant has not achieved moral maturity. The table shows supported learning; the new inference shows a remaining gap. The repair identifies and tests the extra premise linking achievement to protection. An alternate case involving an adult dissenter can then test transfer. Copying the corrected infant answer is not an independent retest.

Before starting, spend twenty minutes checking that your grid contains B2/B3, B4 and B1/B5/B6 evidence. Check one quotation or paraphrase locator from each group. Identify which Du Bois or Fanon passage was central to your paper, so the examiner can use a different one.

## Independent and delayed tasks

B8-F1 interprets a fresh assigned Du Bois/Fanon passage; B8-F2 tests the person/community debate on a new case; B8-F3 diagnoses a comparison and explains a Mills claim. Full prompts and alternates are in [assessment](../../assessment.md). The dialogue asks you to defend one source choice and respond to a new objection to your paper. Use B8-D for the least secure group after the stated delay. Record it against this course once, even if completed during Course 12. Earlier module retrieval does not replace it.

## Budget, repair and completion

Evidence/access audit 0.75h; fresh writing 1.5h; dialogue 1/3h; feedback 1h; targeted repair 1.5h; alternate fresh retest reserve 1h; delayed sample 5/12h; final revision/citation/readiness audit 1.5h = **8h**. The reserve is not a second mandatory full exam. If repairs exceed it, record extra time rather than squeezing an incomplete task into the ledger.

Every row needs satisfactory practice and independently competent fresh evidence, plus the revised paper and source integrity. Fabricated references require correction before assessment can proceed. A failed delayed check reopens its group for targeted work. Next: select a compatible Course 12 option using the current route map; retain B4–7 for later social-knowledge study. Completion does not imply coverage of all African languages, African feminist philosophy or decolonial thought.
