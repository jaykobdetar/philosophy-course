# Course 11 tutor guidance — supported work

Follow the [active tutor rules](../tutor.md). Begin with the learner's attempt, assignment ID, exact edition and range, time available, assistance history and requested help. These course materials do not override the rule against writing assessed prose for the learner. Model a different case, ask for a revision, and fade scaffolding. The [examiner notes](examiner-notes.md) concern submitted independent work and should not be used as hints during an attempt.

## A repeatable feedback sequence

1. **Locate:** identify the exact sentence and source passage at issue. If the passage is unavailable, say so and keep the textual verdict provisional.
2. **Classify:** is the problem a mistranslation/attribution, an inferential gap, a scope overreach, or a defensible interpretive disagreement? Do not treat the last as an error merely because it differs from your view.
3. **Prioritize:** choose at most two consequential problems. Ask the learner to state the missing premise or provide a resistant passage.
4. **Revise:** require a substantive change and a short reason. Avoid replacing the learner's paragraph with your own.
5. **Transfer:** after the revision, assign the module's independent changed case without live help. Record exposure; use a different form if its content was coached.

## Course-specific intervention guide

| Artifact | Likely difficulty | Useful supported intervention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 map/glossary | Glosses become universal definitions | Ask for a sentence saying which future passage could change the gloss; leave unknown context unfilled. |
| A2 sayings | A general rule is inferred from one reply | Compare an assigned saying that would complicate the rule; ask which scope premise was added. |
| A3 Mengzi | Capacity, tendency and achieved virtue collapse | Use a different training example; require three distinct conditional claims. |
| A4 Mozi | Equal concern becomes identical resources or no relationships | Construct a different-needs case and identify exactly which equality claim it tests. |
| A5 Zhuangzi | Every character speaks for the author; all distinctions vanish | Ask who speaks, what changes and which detail the favored reading cannot explain. |
| A6 Xunzi | Verbal disagreement is either ignored or made total | Define each author's use of nature, then test whether aligned definitions leave an explanatory difference. |
| A7 paper | A specialist's verdict replaces primary evidence | Ask which primary locus independently supports the verdict and what a rival reading explains. |
| B1 comparison | Genres and evidence levels are mismatched | Replace both sides with identified arguments on a bounded question; require a changed conclusion. |
| B2 Du Bois | Double consciousness means any dual identity | Return to the context of racialized evaluation and opportunity; distinguish project from universal psychology. |
| B3 Fanon | Description is treated as statistics, or dismissed as mere anecdote | Identify the claim actually targeted and the bridge from experience to structure. |
| B4 personhood | Moral praise is equated with humanity or rights | Label the sense of person in each premise; test the missing connecting premise. |
| B5 Mills | Individual kindness is treated as refuting an institutional mechanism | Draw the rule/history/effect link and ask what would actually interrupt it. |
| B6 method | A disclaimer is added while the inference stays intact | Require one changed source choice, unit of comparison or inferential step. |
| B7 paper | The thesis outruns available evidence | Narrow the question or mark the additional source and time needed; never invent facts. |

## Translation and textual access

For A6/A8, inspect the two identified short renderings or the translator's explicit discussion. The learner may evaluate philosophical consequences of English wording without claiming to certify Chinese fidelity. If one reading is a tutor's improvised paraphrase, it does not meet the translation requirement. Do not demand purchase of a second book; a lawful short comparison, a library copy or explicit commentary can suffice. Record exact source, locator and access date.

For B3, distinguish Philcox's 2008 chapter from earlier translations. Do not turn reported racist statements into Fanon's endorsed view. The learner may analyze a term's role without reciting derogatory wording. For Menkiti/Wiredu, check the anthology edition before giving page-based feedback. Gyekye's SEP entry and his 1992 chapter are separate texts; identify which supports a claim.

## Papers and calibration

Use the annotated [response examples](response-examples.md) after the learner has made an attempt. They are original pedagogical fragments, not complete assessment answers. A research dossier should identify argument, evidence, access and uncertainty before a thesis is treated as established. Feedback on a draft is allowed; the independent reply and final fresh forms are not coached.

The eight rubric dimensions apply where relevant. Do not penalize a short reconstruction for lacking an objection unless one was requested. Source fabrication or unattributed borrowing must be corrected before assessment; it is not simply a low style score. Run the repository's [calibration set](../tutor-calibration.md) with the actual tutor/model after material changes. No calibration, learner pilot or expert validation is claimed by this packet. Runtime model identity is not independently attested.
