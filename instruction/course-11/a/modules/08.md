# C11A-M08 — Independent assessment and delayed transfer

**8 hours. Guiding question:** Can you interpret, compare and qualify a new argument without reproducing coached work? Prerequisites: A1–7, lawful access to the assessed passages and an exposure log.

## Outcomes, sources and procedure

Demonstrate all three [essential groups](../../outcomes.md): concern/cultivation, nature/effort with translation sensitivity, and perspective/comparison method. Assigned sources are the exact editions and ranges in Modules 2–6; there is no new book. Source-dependent judgments remain pending wherever the examiner cannot inspect the relevant passage. The final task samples a fresh question from an already read text; “fresh” does not mean you should read an unassigned chapter.

Read the common [assessment rules and A forms](../../assessment.md). Submit A8-F1, F2 and F3 at thirty minutes each, then a twenty-minute dialogue or equivalent typed exchange. The examiner selects a form not previously rehearsed and keeps the [anchors](../../examiner-notes.md) closed during the attempt. If the chosen Zhuangzi episode was central to A5 or the paper, use the alternate episode. Source-open work permits primary texts and the short checked translation comparison, not prior answers or tutor notes.

## Teaching: knowing what your evidence shows

A portfolio and a fresh task provide different evidence. Revision may show that you can use feedback intelligently while leaving open whether you can recognize the same problem in a new setting. A fresh task tests that recognition. Conversely, one hurried error need not erase weeks of good interpretation: diagnose the error, teach the missing distinction and retest it with a new prompt. Record these occasions separately.

Use an evidence row with five fields: supported artifact; independent sample; precise remaining issue; repair; fresh retest. “Good essay” is not a locator. Write, for example, “A6 paragraphs 3–5 reconstruct the nature distinction; A8-F2 correctly identifies the translation inference but omits a rival reading.” This identifies what learning needs to occur. A total mark cannot compensate for an empty row.

## Worked practice — original record audit

A fictional learner's A4 essay distinguishes inclusive care from equal parcels. On a fresh task the learner says all relationships must end merely because the chapter title contains “universal.” The practice artifact is satisfactory, but the independent concern row is unresolved: the title has replaced an argument. Repair the scope inference, then use a new case with different needs. Do not mark the original fresh task competent because the revised answer is good.

Before assessment, audit your own grid in twenty minutes: check one actual artifact for each row, confirm source locators, and mark any previously seen fresh question. This is procedural preparation, not coached rehearsal of the prompts.

## Independent and delayed tasks

The full changed-case prompts, alternate forms and neutral criteria are in [assessment](../../assessment.md). The A8 dialogue asks you to identify the strongest unresolved objection to your paper and explain what evidence could change your mind. Four to six weeks later **or after two later modules**, complete the unused A8-D form for the least secure group in twenty-five minutes. Do not reread an answer key immediately before it. The examiner can allow the assigned source and citation sheet; no content help. Report actual timing against this course, once.

## Budget, remediation and completion

Evidence/access audit 0.75h; fresh written forms 1.5h; dialogue 1/3h; feedback 1h; targeted repair 1.5h; alternate fresh retest reserve 1h; delayed independent sample 5/12h; final revision/citation/readiness audit 1.5h = **8h**. Unused retest time supports checking citations and repairing weak reasoning, not another mandatory examination. If repairs exceed the reserve, log extra hours.

Completion requires independently competent evidence in every row, the revised paper and honest source integrity. If you cannot substantiate a translation claim, obtain the bounded comparison and retest that criterion; do not replace it with an invented English contrast. Next: select a compatible Course 12 option using the current route map; for comparative moral cultivation retain A3, A6, the translation record and the revised paper. This course does not grant coverage of Laozi, Han Feizi or later Chinese philosophy.
