# C11A-M03 — Mengzi: beginnings, extension and development

**20 hours. Guiding question:** What would make a spontaneous response evidence about human nature? Prerequisites: A2 reconstruction and Course 6 ethics; no prior Chinese competence assumed.

## Outcomes and assigned reading

Distinguish an incipient disposition from completed virtue; reconstruct one extension argument; explain a textual difficulty without inventing a solution; evaluate a translation-sensitive inference conditionally.

## Teaching

A developmental claim lies between two extremes. If goodness were already a finished achievement, cultivation would be unnecessary. If there were no relevant initial resources, talk of nurturing beginnings would lose its explanatory role. Mengzi's sprout imagery invites the reader to distinguish having a basis for development from completing that development. A disposition can be interrupted, misdirected or neglected; observing failure is therefore not by itself a refutation of the claim that the relevant beginnings are present.

Yet the argument still faces a burden. A vivid case of concern cannot establish a universal thesis simply because it feels compelling. Reconstruct 2A6 in stages: the imagined situation, the response attributed to people, the excluded ulterior explanations, and the conclusion about the basis of ethical life. Ask whether the inference concerns a universal capacity, an ordinary tendency or a morally authoritative starting point. Those readings require different evidence. Mark what the passage says and what you are supplying.

The movement in 1A7 also requires a bridge. Concern already displayed in one situation can be brought to bear on another. An argument from extension must identify a relevant similarity and explain why attention or action has not already followed it. “You once cared, so you always care equally” is too strong. Nor does a failure of extension show that the first concern was insincere. There may be a gap between noticing a case, seeing its relevance and organizing conduct accordingly.

Use 6A1–10 to test the explanatory role of nature and environment. Treat analogies as arguments with a mapping: what corresponds to growth, damage, direction and intervention? A water or plant comparison does not establish its own applicability. In 4A17, ask how judgment works when a rule's ordinary application confronts an urgent case. Read 6B2 and 7B24 before treating every demand as an uncomplicated inference from “nature.” Do not import an Aristotelian account until your Mengzi reconstruction can stand alone.

## Worked practice — original case

A pupil notices that one classmate has missed lunch and offers food, but ignores a distant group's equally serious hunger. This supports the limited claim that the pupil can respond to another's need. It does not establish stable impartial motivation. A proposed extension asks the pupil to notice that distance does not remove hunger's moral relevance; the proposal needs an argument about relevant similarity. Training could develop this response without pretending that mature virtue was already present. The case illustrates a distinction; it is not a quotation or an empirical test of Mengzi.

Produce A3: a **700-word reconstruction** of 2A6 or 1A7 with one difficulty supported by another assigned passage. Attach an analogy map and a glossary revision for `xing` or `ren`. For translation practice, use Van Norden's explicit attached discussion of one term if available; record the exact note and distinguish the translator's claim from yours. If no explicit discussion is present, reserve the translation claim for A6's documented two-rendering route.

## Independent changed-case A3-I

An invented apprentice feels shame after taking credit for a colleague's work, yet repeats the act after competitive rewards are introduced. A critic says this disproves all good beginnings. In 350–450 words, use 2A6 and one of 6A1–10 to assess the inference, identify a different kind of evidence that would challenge your interpretation, and state one limit of the analogy. Thirty-five minutes, assigned sources and citation sheet only; no AI/hints/notes from the worked case. Criteria: separate capacity, tendency and achievement; use the text; take the critic seriously.
