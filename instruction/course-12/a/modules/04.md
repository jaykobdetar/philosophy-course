# C12A-M04 — Intelligence and understanding

Baseline allocation: **20 hours**. Prerequisites: Courses 1, 2 first-order language/semantics, 5 and 7; prior modules in this option (except M01, also usable as the PC-28 foundation).

## Guiding question and outcomes

What can success at a linguistic task establish about understanding?

By the end: Distinguish a test from a theory; reconstruct the Chinese Room; assess the systems reply and Searle’s reply.

## Teaching explanation

Turing replaces an unstable verbal question about machines thinking with a specified performance comparison. The replacement directs attention toward evidence and test conditions. Passing an operational test may warrant an attribution under a proposed standard; it does not deductively settle every metaphysical question about consciousness. Reading the actual objections and replies prevents reducing the paper to a prediction about chat software.

Searle targets the claim that implementing a formal program is sufficient for understanding. In the room, a person follows rules manipulating symbols without understanding the language. The intended inference reaches beyond the person to a claim about computation and meaning, and that is where the systems reply presses: the whole organized system might possess something the operator lacks. Searle considers internalizing the system and argues that the understanding would still be absent. The dispute therefore concerns the right bearer of understanding and whether the intuition about the internalized case settles it. Neither a confident impression of understanding nor a confident impression of its absence eliminates the need to state a sufficiency premise. Keep syntax, semantics, behavioral evidence and subjective experience distinct.

## Worked practice example

Invented example: a volunteer uses an enormous card index to answer gardening questions in an unfamiliar language. “The volunteer cannot translate the questions” is evidence about the volunteer. “No larger arrangement can understand them” needs another premise about how properties of a system depend on its parts. The systems reply challenges that step without proving that this particular card index understands.

## Independent changed case

I04: A maintenance network passes an unrestricted text interview, grounds symbols in sensors and can explain its own failed predictions. A critic says that each individual processor lacks understanding, so the network must lack it. Evaluate using functionalism and Searle, including one relevant limitation. 450 words.

Use assigned sources and ordinary access aids, but no live content help, model answers or examiner notes. Declare prior exposure. Neutral criteria: faithful use of the assigned concepts; a justified inference; an identified limitation; traceable attribution. Freeze the response before feedback. If exposed, use the relevant alternate in assessment.md, or the examiner's unused changed-case procedure for a nonessential subskill.
