# C12A-M07 — Essay and revision

Baseline allocation: **26 hours**. Prerequisites: Courses 1, 2 first-order language/semantics, 5 and 7; prior modules in this option (except M01, also usable as the PC-28 foundation).

## Guiding question and outcomes

Can you defend one bounded claim while fairly reconstructing the strongest objection?

By the end: Produce a 2,000–2,400-word paper, a source dossier, a substantive revision and a changed-case response.

## Assigned reading and access

Revisit the exact ranges in earlier modules. Select one verified secondary source relevant to the question; record author, title, publication, edition/URL, exact section and why it bears on the disputed premise. No new full book is required. If access fails, narrow the claim or record a source dependency; never invent supporting literature.

Consult [source audit](../source-audit.md) before making textual judgments. Reading access is distinct from bibliographic identification; original teaching below is not a substitute for the primary assignment. Audio reading is welcome, but retain a written edition for exact locators. Follow [study and access procedures](../../study-protocol.md).

## Teaching explanation

Choose one of these bounded questions, or a demonstrably equivalent question approved through the same scope test: (1) Does the systems reply identify a gap in the Chinese Room inference? (2) Can the ability hypothesis explain the specified experiential gain without adding nonphysical facts? A viable question names a disputed inference, identifies assigned passages that bear on it and permits a serious rival answer. Reject a topic whose answer would require unassigned books or an empirical study you cannot conduct. For aesthetics, name an actual work, checked maker/date/medium and the limits of your reproduction; invented works cannot replace the final case essay. For comparative inquiry, give each author’s question before imposing a common criterion.

Build the paper in stages. Write a 100-word claim with one explicit limitation; reconstruct the relevant argument in 400 words; locate the strongest objection to its pivotal premise; answer it by defending, narrowing or abandoning a part of your claim. An interpretation answers what the text supports, whereas an improvement proposes what the author could say. Mark them separately. The new example tests a premise; it does not prove a general theory merely because a verdict seems intuitive.

Revision is a reasoned change to the argument. Use a ledger with criticism, affected passage, decision, reason and resulting change. A rejected comment needs a reason too. Check every quotation against the actual edition and distinguish your paraphrase from borrowed language. A final conclusion should state what the argument establishes and which uncertainty remains, without inflating the scope to the whole field.

## Worked practice example

Invented planning example: “Theory T is false” is too broad. “T’s inference from feature F to verdict V fails when condition C holds” names a testable target. A revision from “the counterexample refutes T” to “it defeats this sufficient-condition reading unless T adds a historically supported restriction” changes the argument’s scope, not merely its style.

## Time plan

Planning budget: 2 h question/scope; 9 h rereading and source dossier; 6 h drafting; 2 h feedback; 4 h substantive revision; 1 h independent changed case; 2 h final citation and revision audit (including twenty-minute early retrieval). Total 26 h.

## Supported practice artifact

P07: Submit a question card, a source dossier with at least two primary locators and one checked secondary source, a 2,000–2,400-word draft, a reverse outline and a 400-word revision memo. Use the [comparison progression](../../comparison-progression.md). Preserve the draft and final versions. The essay can reuse the workshop/case dossier; it is not a second unrelated paper.

Criteria: answer the stated question, make the key distinction explicit, connect reasons to the conclusion, and identify the actual source of attributed claims. Label invented examples. Keep your first attempt and help log.

## Independent changed case

I07: Choose the branch matching the frozen essay question, within the existing one-hour independent-work allowance; write 350 words without live content help. **Systems-reply branch:** stipulate that the system in your essay is discovered to contain a concealed link to a human language expert who supplies interpretations whenever its formal rules run out. The rest of its organization and observed performance are unchanged. Explain how including that expert within the “whole system” affects a systems reply and what, if anything, success now shows about a program’s sufficiency. **Ability-hypothesis branch:** stipulate that the subject’s full physical dossier describes every event non-indexically, but on first experience the subject also learns “I am undergoing this experience now.” Assess whether that self-locating gain by itself supplies the new qualitative/nonphysical fact your argument requires; distinguish it from recognitional ability and state a limit.

Use assigned sources and ordinary access aids, but no live content help, model answers or examiner notes. Declare prior exposure. Neutral criteria: faithful use of the assigned concepts; a justified inference; an identified limitation; traceable attribution. Freeze the response before feedback. If exposed, use the relevant alternate in assessment.md, or the examiner's unused changed-case procedure for a nonessential subskill.

## Selecting an equivalent question’s branch

An equivalent approved question must remain within one of the stated essay subjects and the assigned source range. Before drafting, label its subject and the existing challenge branch it will receive; for C, distinguish James, Husserl and freedom explicitly. The tutor checks that the branch changes a premise the planned essay actually uses. If it does not, narrow the question until it does; this packet does not silently approve a new subject without an authored assessment. After the paper is frozen, give only that branch. Do not disclose its diagnostic anchor or coach a response beforehand. Keep the exact question-to-branch decision with the essay record. This selection and source check use the existing scope/dossier hours; the independent response uses its existing one hour, not a second essay or an extra exam.

## Feedback, repair and retrieval

Name the one distinction on which your conclusion depends. If it is confused, make a two-column distinction card with a positive instance and a near miss, check the assigned passage, then revise only the affected inference. If attribution is unsupported, reopen the actual edition; do not ask a model to invent a locator. Tutor feedback records criterion, evidence, source status and next action.

After seven days, reconstruct your pivotal inference from memory in 150 words, then reopen the text and correct one omission. This preparatory retrieval does not replace the final delayed transfer.
