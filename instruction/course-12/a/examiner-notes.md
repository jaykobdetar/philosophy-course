Keep this file out of the learner’s active assessment view. Apply [assessment rules](../study-protocol.md) and the shared [observable rubric](../../../current/assessment.md). Accept defensible disagreement; record source uncertainty. The following anchors are diagnostic, not compulsory wording.

## C12A-M01: independent I01

Look for existential failure in the positive and narrow-negation descriptions; wide negation is true when there is no mayor. The archivist fact does not create a mayor. Identity explanation must distinguish cognitive significance from two referents. Credit a well-defended alternative semantic analysis only if Russell’s own analysis is accurately reconstructed first.

## C12A-M07: independent I07

Systems branch: changing the system boundary adds a genuinely understanding component; whole-system attribution no longer establishes that formal rules alone suffice. This does not show the entire hybrid lacks understanding. Ability branch: a self-locating gain is not automatically new phenomenal information; the learner must argue how it relates to the qualitative gain and avoid settling physicalism merely by a change of indexical perspective.

Select the branch before drafting by subject, withhold the specific independent stimulus until the paper is frozen, and retain the selection record. ## Essential row 1: F1/A1/D1

F1: quantification is over committees, with existence/uniqueness relative to each committee; two secretaries serving different committees provide a countermodel to global uniqueness. Accept an explicit restricted-domain paraphrase if it preserves dependence. A1: quantification ranges over the one inscription, not over a purported editor. Quoting a description does not assert its referent exists; spelling predicates apply to expressions, while age predicates apply to people. D1: distinguish actual reference from the reporter’s mode of presentation and beliefs about satisfaction; ordinary extensional substitution is not a complete semantics for belief. Do not require unassigned epistemic logic.

## Essential row 2: F2/A2/D2

F2: labels that already contain “understands” may conceal the target capacity; a functional explanation needs a specified role and transitions. Test success is evidence, not a cure for circular explanation or a proof of semantic sufficiency. A2: matching a human error profile is distinct from understanding or implementing the same functional role. Adding random errors may affect test impressions without supplying a semantic explanation; accuracy alone is not the missing semantic premise. D2: distinguish expression channel, retained organization and understanding attribution; cable loss alone need not destroy the relevant state, but damage that changes internal causal organization would raise another question.

## Essential row 3: F3/A3/D3

F3: previously unknown physical calibration facts contradict the advertised all-physical-information premise. Supplying them repairs that defect but does not settle whether the experiential gain is new nonphysical information. A3: human-accessible representations and navigational skill do not establish a bat’s point of view; an epistemic limit requires further premises to become a metaphysical result. D3, retained: inability to communicate an experience does not by itself establish nonphysical information. Require the intervening knowledge/ontology inference.
