# Additional packet examiner notes

Withhold during independent attempts. The ordinary shared rubric and assistance rules apply. These packets have neutral criteria in their learner files; no conclusion is mandatory.

## PC-28 foundation

F-FR: one damaged sculpture meets the restricted description’s uniqueness even though the museum has several sculptures; the unrestricted description fails uniqueness. Quantification introduces a bound variable, not an extra object. A-FR: definite description adds uniqueness to existence; an indefinite description does not. A comprehensible sense does not guarantee reference. D-FR: the stipulation establishes distinct referents, so the identity assertion is false rather than magically making them identical. Never assess Kripke or modality here. Full credit requires all shared reading/practice and this independent evidence; English Frege source fidelity remains pending until the assigned translation is inspected.

## Grice

I-GR: an explicit refusal can opt out of the requested disclosure; underinformativeness does not thereby communicate ignorance. Distinguish withholding known information from covertly misleading about knowledge. A-GR: a clash between requested informativeness and available evidence need not be deceptive; quality may prevent the exact answer. Do not turn maxims into an exceptionless command to reveal information. D-GR, retained: group size, remaining seats and adjacency matter; “a few” alone gives no exact count. P-GR’s weak praise and scalar exercise remain coached practice; neither is used as the new independent inferential problem.

## Smart

I-SM: identify the illicit transfer of a presented color/shape to the sensation-reporting process; compare Smart’s actual treatment of after-images. A reply can remove that argument without establishing identity or eliminating every qualitative-property objection. A-SM: phenomenal unity does not transparently show that the process lacks spatial parts; distinguish presentation from the claimed ontology. An argued challenge to that distinction can succeed. D-SM, retained: correlation permits two relata; identity does not. P-SM’s epistemic and multiple-realization map is coached practice and is no longer recycled as I/A.

## Lewis

I-LE: an unusual causal route does not automatically refute an analysis in terms of abilities. The stronger challenge asks whether the stipulated bundle can exist without knowing what the sensation is like, which the story must argue rather than assume. Also distinguish imagery resembling memory from actual remembering: the stipulated surrogate does not automatically establish every element of Lewis’s bundle. Avoid treating Lewis’s ordinary acquisition claim as excluding every extraordinary route. A-LE: a trace may represent ordinary information without the subject possessing that information as belief/knowledge, and without representing special phenomenal facts. Lewis’s reply can admit such enabling traces. D-LE, retained: specify disposition versus performance in adverse lighting before claiming a counterexample. The old loss-of-abilities and learning-an-ingredient forms are retired.

## Packet delay and credit

Fifteen-minute retrieval tasks are budgeted within each addition and occur after at least seven days. They do not replace the course’s later four-to-six-week/two-module delayed transfer. If a route coordinator samples these skills later, use an unused context and record its time in the appropriate existing delayed allocation once. No additional full examination is required merely because an addition exists.
