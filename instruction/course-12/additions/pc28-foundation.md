# PC-28 — shared Frege/Russell foundation

**Twenty added hours for C12B/C/D routes; zero additional hours for C12A.** Prerequisites: Course 1 and Course 2 first-order semantics. This packet consists of the **entire [C12A-M01](../a/modules/01.md)**, including its exact Max Black English Frege reading, Russell article, teaching, worked case, P01, independent I01, revision and retrieval. It is not a shortened overview or an additional copy of that assignment.

The foundation teaches sense/reference, descriptions, existence/uniqueness, negation scope and the limits of formalization. All twenty hours of A-M01 are shared. For a non-12A learner, **replace** A-M01’s I01 with F-FR below inside its existing one-hour independent-work block; replace its twenty-minute retrieval with D-FR. Do not add these tasks to the old tasks. A-FR is the reserved fresh replacement after exposure or repair and uses that same block’s remaining time where possible; substantial extra repair is logged explicitly. None requires Kripke, rigidity or reference-fixing doctrine from A-M02.

**F-FR (30 minutes):** A museum’s numbered register contains exactly one damaged sculpture and two undamaged sculptures. Formalize “The damaged sculpture is insured” and “The sculpture is insured” over that same domain. Explain why finding one insured damaged sculpture settles neither the second description’s uniqueness nor the insurance status of every sculpture. An assistant treats the quantifiers as naming a fourth, abstract object called “the damaged sculpture.” Explain the Russellian mistake and the distinction between an expression and its referent.

**A-FR (30 minutes):** A report says “The winner of the prize is a winner of a prize.” The records establish exactly one winner of this prize but many winners of other prizes. Formalize the definite and indefinite descriptions separately and explain why replacing one with the other weakens the claim. Explain whether understanding the expression’s sense alone establishes that its required referent exists.

**D-FR (20 minutes, after at least seven days):** In a stipulated archive, “the earliest surviving draft” and “the signed draft” pick out different documents. A catalogue editor treats both expressions as referring to one document merely because they occur in a sentence asserting their identity. Explain why an identity assertion is not a guarantee of co-reference; distinguish its sense, asserted truth and reference conditions, and state what the records would need to show. This replaces A-M01’s early retrieval, not the later course delayed sample.

Criteria: explain the definite/indefinite or identity distinction, make existence and uniqueness explicit where relevant, give a justified conclusion and use only the actual Frege/Russell readings. Separate anchors are in [addition examiner notes](examiner-notes.md).
