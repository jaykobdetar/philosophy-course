## Source and readiness limits

English Frege (Max Black 1980), the exact Cambridge Putnam pages, the assigned Kant, Husserl, Merleau-Ponty, Macomber, Frechtman and Jech texts, and the listed social-philosophy readings have explicit reading-copy or passage-verification dependencies. Each option audit states exactly what was inspected. The Danto publisher sample contains the full article with different pages; the local Putnam anthology is pp. 73–79; the Hollander anthology lacks Problema I. No missing assigned translation is silently replaced.

This is an authored packet, not a learner pilot, expert validation, broad rights clearance or visual/accessibility certification. No purchased or full copyrighted texts are included. [authoring.json](authoring.json) records tracks, additions and credit rules.

Addition assessment diagnostics are in the separate [packet examiner notes](additions/examiner-notes.md); withhold them during independent attempts.

The [assessment repair matrix](repair-matrix.md) records the substantive revisions, reserved forms and remaining re-review status; [validation](validation.md) distinguishes structural checks from evidence of learner transfer.
