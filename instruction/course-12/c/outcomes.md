# C12C outcome and evidence map

Every essential row from current/assessment.md needs a named supported artifact and independent fresh evidence. Record actual file/date, source edition, assistance, exposure, feedback, revision and readiness; the mapping is a plan, not completed learner evidence.

| Essential group | Practice artifact | Modules | Independent | Delayed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pragmatist method | P01 James essay + P02 coordination maps | M01–02 | F1 or unexposed A1, 30 min | D1 if least secure, 25+10 min |
| Phenomenological method | P03 explanation + P04 description/report distinctions | M03–04 | F2 or unexposed A2, 30 min | D2 if least secure, 25+10 min |
| Freedom and ethical inquiry | P05 freedom comparison + P06 dilemma | M05–06 | F3 or unexposed A3, 30 min | D3 if least secure, 25+10 min |

The essay also supplies evidence of question/claim, interpretive fidelity, reasoning, objection/reply, organization, attribution and substantive revision. The explanation tests responsiveness; it cannot substitute for missing written or accessible-equivalent row evidence. Early module retrieval supports learning but does not replace the final delayed sample.
