# C12B-M07 — Assessment and delayed check

Baseline allocation: **10 hours**. Prerequisites: Courses 1, 6 and Course 9 Kant vocabulary; prior modules in this option.

## Guiding question and outcomes

Can you transfer the course’s essential skills to uncoached cases?

By the end: Supply independent evidence for each of the three essential grid rows, explain a response under challenge and complete delayed transfer.

## Assigned reading and access

No new reading: source-open use of the assigned editions, your frozen paper and the verified passage selected under assessment.md. Missing texts remain unresolved dependencies for textual criteria.

Consult [source audit](../source-audit.md) before making textual judgments. Reading access is distinct from bibliographic identification; original teaching below is not a substitute for the primary assignment. Audio reading is welcome, but retain a written edition for exact locators. Follow [study and access procedures](../../study-protocol.md).

## Teaching explanation

Assessment combines distinct evidence rather than duplicating the final essay. Your paper may establish sustained argument on one question, but it cannot demonstrate every essential outcome outside that question. The three fresh tasks below therefore sample different groups. Before beginning, list practiced prompts and any examiner notes you have seen. A rehearsed item is replaced by the named alternate form.

A source-open assessment permits checking wording and locators; it does not permit asking a tutor or model to choose premises or improve the answer while it is being written. Dictionary, text-to-speech, speech-to-text and ordinary access support are permitted if they do not supply philosophical content. A twenty-minute conversation, or an equivalent written exchange, tests responsiveness by asking which premise a changed condition affects. Assess reasons, not confidence or speed.

After feedback, repair the identified distinction and use a still-fresh alternate only where evidence was insufficient. Four to six weeks later, or after two later modules, complete the delayed form for the least secure essential group. Its time belongs to this module once, even if taken during the next course. A row remains unresolved when its independent evidence is absent; averaging cannot conceal it.

## Worked practice example

Practice calibration example: an answer can be clearly written yet fail because it calls every disagreement “subjective.” Feedback should identify the missing distinction and ask for a new explanation. Merely substituting technical vocabulary is not evidence of understanding. The independent task must then change the case, not request a polished copy of the coached paragraph.

## Time plan

Planning budget: 60 min evidence/source audit; 90 min fresh written tasks; 20 min explanation or accessible exchange; 335 min feedback and targeted repair; 60 min revision/citation review; 35 min delayed task and citation check. Total 10 h. These are planning allowances, not speed grades.

## Supported practice artifact

P07: Assemble the evidence grid in [outcomes.md](../outcomes.md) with artifact IDs, passage locators, assistance declarations and one uncertainty per row. Rehearse the generic question “Which premise does that challenge?” on an already coached example only.

Criteria: answer the stated question, make the key distinction explicit, connect reasons to the conclusion, and identify the actual source of attributed claims. Label invented examples. Keep your first attempt and help log.

## Independent changed case

Complete F1–F3 in assessment.md, thirty minutes each (ninety minutes total), followed by the twenty-minute explanation. Use A1–A3 only for exposure/repair replacements; the assessor withholds examiner anchors during attempts.

Use assigned sources and ordinary access aids, but no live content help, model answers or examiner notes. Declare prior exposure. Neutral criteria: faithful use of the assigned concepts; a justified inference; an identified limitation; traceable attribution. Freeze the response before feedback. If exposed, use the relevant alternate in assessment.md, or the examiner's unused changed-case procedure for a nonessential subskill.

## Feedback, repair and retrieval

Name the one distinction on which your conclusion depends. If it is confused, make a two-column distinction card with a positive instance and a near miss, check the assigned passage, then revise only the affected inference. If attribution is unsupported, reopen the actual edition; do not ask a model to invent a locator. Tutor feedback records criterion, evidence, source status and next action.

Complete one of D1–D3 in assessment.md after four to six weeks or two later modules, twenty-five minutes plus ten minutes source/citation check. The selected form must not have been rehearsed. Record actual delay and charge once.
