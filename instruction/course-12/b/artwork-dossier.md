# Actual artworks and evidence boundaries

The National Gallery collection records below were text-inspected on 2026-10-20. Metadata is supplied so tasks can begin; the learner still opens the reproduction or the museum's accessible audio description/transcript and records the actual viewing/listening context. This packet includes links, not image reproductions. No visual QA, color fidelity, original-scale viewing or firsthand museum encounter is claimed.

- [Vincent van Gogh, Sunflowers, NG3863](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/vincent-van-gogh-sunflowers):1888; oil on canvas;92.1×73cm; National Gallery, London. It is a particular version of the subject, not all Sunflowers paintings. Practice can compare still life with a symbolic arrangement, while checking what the museum actually supports.

## Fixed assessment pool and exposure rules

| Stage | Work | Reserved inferential problem |

| Final F3 | Stubbs, Whistlejacket | Psychological training effect versus correct historical category |

| Delayed D3 | Monet, The Water-Lily Pond | Category of a cropped reproduction versus the intact work |

The first two practice works are the only works permitted in coached P05 and the case essay. Do not use any of these four reserved works for those activities. Each stage opens its assigned museum record when the independent attempt begins. If the learner has encountered a work casually, record that separately from exposure to the actual task or category analysis. If F3 has been rehearsed, A3 is the replacement; D3 remains reserved. If both final forms were coached, independence is unresolved and a replacement must be authored and checked before use; do not claim that an ad hoc artwork choice is already a complete form. This finite pool supports the normal practice, independent, final, one-repair and delayed sequence without inventing missing material.

- [Hans Holbein the Younger, The Ambassadors, NG1314](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/hans-holbein-the-younger-the-ambassadors): 1533; oil on wood (Baltic/Polish oak, identified); 207 × 209.5 cm; National Gallery, London. The museum identifies the two sitters and describes the anamorphic skull, recognizable from an oblique position. The record’s account supplies evidence for the I05 optical/category distinction; it does not certify a learner’s firsthand experience. Metadata and relevant description were inspected on 2026-10-20 in the repair pass.
- [George Stubbs, Whistlejacket, NG6569](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/george-stubbs-whistlejacket): about 1762; oil on canvas; 296.1 × 248 cm; National Gallery, London. The record supplies commission/context and an accessible description. The differing viewer training in F3 is a hypothetical experiment, not a reported historical event.
- [J. M. W. Turner, The Fighting Temeraire, NG524](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/joseph-mallord-william-turner-the-fighting-temeraire): 1839; oil on canvas; 90.7 × 121.6 cm; National Gallery, London. The full title describes the vessel’s final journey in 1838; distinguish event year and painting date. For A3, the museum reports that masts were removed before the tow but Turner painted three lower masts; that specific account was rechecked during repair.
- [Claude Monet, The Water-Lily Pond, NG4240](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/claude-monet-the-water-lily-pond): 1899; oil on canvas; 88.3 × 93.1 cm; National Gallery, London. The record identifies Monet’s garden and discusses the bridge, reflections and viewpoint. Metadata and relevant description were inspected on 2026-10-20 in the repair pass. D3’s thumbnail crop and label are explicitly hypothetical presentation changes; the intact work’s actual record remains the evidence.

For each use, store privately: maker/title/inventory/date/medium; institutional URL and access date; actual reproduction, transcript or recording used; scale/color/texture/sound limitations; available features; a historical claim with its source; a contested interpretation; category evidence. If image access is unavailable, use the museum’s description and distinguish testimony from direct experience. No image is copied into this packet and no visual QA or rights clearance is claimed.
