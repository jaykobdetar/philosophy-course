## C12B-M05: independent I05

I05 uses Holbein’s The Ambassadors only. Distinguish a category’s standard/contra-standard expectations from the optical transformation caused by position: improved skull recognition need not be caused solely by learning a category. Explain the relation between double portrait and optical display without assuming they are exclusive or equally adequate. ## C12B-M06: independent I06

Category branch: the stipulated history is hypothetical and supplies relevant evidence, not a decree overriding all formal/category evidence. Specify a changed premise for the selected actual work and preserve the distinction between historical correctness and a predicted aesthetic effect. Taste branch: corrected perceptual evidence changes the basis for assessment; revision alone does not prove good taste and persistence alone does not prove prejudice. Explain the relevant Humean qualification or Kantian ground rather than voting between critics.

Select the branch before drafting by subject, withhold the specific independent stimulus until the paper is frozen, and retain the selection record. An approved equivalent question must match an existing branch’s target premise; otherwise narrow the question before approval. Assess changed-premise reasoning, faithful source use and a justified revision, including withdrawal of the essay conclusion.

## Essential row 1: F1/A1/D1

F1: distinguish reliance on another critic’s testimony from making one’s own taste judgment. Humean qualifications can bear on whose testimony is credible; that does not automatically supply the speaker’s own exercise of taste. Kant’s demand for assent is not a proof transmitted by authority. Permit carefully supported differences about the extent of legitimate testimony. A1, retained: prejudice and proof from a determinate concept are distinct problems. D1, retained: agreement can share a ground that fails the account’s conditions.

## Essential row 2: F2/A2/D2

F2: accepting a deliberately incorporated accident does not retrospectively turn a practical boundary into a timeless necessary/sufficient definition. Distinguish the origin of material from artistic identification of a work; Weitz’s open concept and Danto’s historical/theoretical intelligibility do different work. A2, retained: explain what converts a proposed commemorative interpretation into a plausible artistic practice; intention alone is not decisive. D2, retained: resemblance, classificatory context and value remain separate.

## Essential row 3: F3/A3/D3

F3: the stipulated experiment supplies evidence of category-sensitive response, not by itself the correct historical category. Use actual full-work features and commission/context; no emotional response is compulsory. A3: documentary accuracy may play different aesthetic roles under the proposed categories; actual recorded facts constrain interpretation and do not authorize invented history. D3: the cropped reproduction is a different evidential object; context and omitted features can defeat its category’s application to the whole painting. Distinguish an actual accessible observation from museum testimony or a hypothetical crop.
