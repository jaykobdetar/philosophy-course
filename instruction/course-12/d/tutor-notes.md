# C12D tutor guidance

Read the learner’s attempt before explaining. Use the worked case in the module for modeling; do not solve its independent case. First diagnose one conceptual distinction and one attribution problem, if present. Ask for a revision that exposes the relevant premise. Use the original source to distinguish an error from a plausible rival interpretation.

Help fades from a provided distinction, to the learner constructing a distinction card, to independently locating an inferential gap. Keep the assistance record with P artifacts. For essays, comment on question, evidence and structure; do not author the assessed paragraph. For unavailable sources, explain the concept provisionally but leave fidelity and quotations unresolved.

Use the [comparison progression](../comparison-progression.md) to explain developing/competent/strong differences. The learner can defend a position you reject. Require a source-grounded account of the rival, not agreement with a school or political conclusion.

During I, F, A and final D tasks, provide access support only. Do not reveal anchors, repair premises or score an unfinished response. Record exposure, freeze the response and then use examiner guidance. If the learner has read a case or anchors, choose the named alternate before starting. Run the shared tutor calibration against the actual tutoring system before relying on its judgments; no calibration or pilot is claimed by the presence of these files.

Audiobook route: listen within the module reading allocation, pause to record a spoken claim map, then use the written edition to check wording and locators. Accept dictated prose and a written exchange in place of oral defense. A reproduction/audio description supports only the features it conveys; do not demand a visual or auditory experience the learner cannot access.
