# C12D assessment forms

Use three fresh tasks, **30 minutes each**, within the final module’s ninety-minute written allocation. Each response is approximately 300–450 words, with formulas/diagrams where relevant. The assessor selects F or an unexposed A form before the attempt; do not practice these forms. Delayed D tasks use twenty-five minutes plus ten for citation review, four to six weeks later or after two later modules, charged once to Course 12. Select the least secure row; all three delayed alternatives are provided so the choice follows evidence.

Permitted: assigned texts, ordinary dictionary, text-to-speech, speech-to-text and access support. Not permitted: live content advice, model-generated reasoning, coached revision during the attempt or examiner anchors. Record source access, exposure, assistance and actual time. When a passage is unavailable, the interpretation criterion remains unresolved even if the conceptual case is answered well.

Learner criteria for every form: address the precise question; reconstruct the assigned view fairly; explain the inference and its limitation; use checked locators; distinguish your view from the source. No philosophical or political verdict is prescribed.

## 1. Structural and relational justice

F1: A residents’ assembly has equal votes and equal incomes. It votes to allow only the majority’s dialect at meetings. Minority speakers can read that dialect but report that its conversational norms prevent them from contributing on equal terms; some nevertheless support the rule because employers favor it. Compare Young’s cultural-imperialism analysis with hooks’s critique of treating one group’s experience as universal or Anderson’s equal-standing account. Identify the mechanism at issue, assess what the minority’s expressed support does and does not establish, and mark the empirical assumptions needed for a judgment.

A1: A workplace pays the same hourly wage to all but allocates secure shifts through informal networks that exclude carers. Complaints are heard only by supervisors who control promotion. Compare Young with Anderson or Okin, and explain what an equal-pay statistic fails to establish.

D1: An organization removes a rule that excluded a group from leadership. Six months later its leadership composition has not changed because appointments last five years; participants now have equal formal access, but some still defer to the old officeholders. Does the unchanged outcome refute the claim that the abolished rule helped cause the earlier pattern? Distinguish that structural question from the relational question whether members now stand as equals. State what longitudinal and interaction evidence each judgment requires.

## 2. Moral scope

F2: Stipulate two possible futures for a habitat. A rare native population is replaced by an introduced population with the same ecological functions, the same number of sentient individuals, and exactly the same welfare over time. No further consequences are hidden in the stipulation. One analyst infers that the futures must be morally identical because welfare is identical; another infers that native membership alone settles the opposite verdict. Assess both inferences using Singer and Leopold. Explain whether a residual dispute concerns evidence or the unit and kind of moral value, without smuggling extra suffering into the case.

A2: Two reports evaluate the same hypothetical river intervention. Both agree on the suffering caused to animals, but one predicts ecological collapse without the intervention and the other predicts spontaneous recovery; neither prediction has yet been verified. One committee attributes the policy disagreement entirely to Singer versus Leopold. Reconstruct a conditional argument under each account, show how an empirical disagreement can mimic a normative one, and identify a question about individual interests and community value that could remain even if the ecological prediction were settled.

D2: Stipulate a choice concerning an uninhabited geological-and-plant community. Preserving its soils and native plant relations would make no difference to any present or future sentient being’s interests; disruption also causes no suffering. A critic says that because the plants do not suffer, neither Singer nor Leopold can supply any relevant distinction between the options. Assess the inference. Explain the role of sentience in one account and land-community membership in the other, without inventing indirect welfare effects excluded by the case.

## 3. Idealization and method

F3: A policy model assumes every person is selfish, ignores all observed cooperation, and treats domination as inevitable. Its author says: “This is pessimistic rather than utopian, so it cannot be the kind of ideological idealization Mills criticizes.” Assess that defense. Explain how a model’s abstraction, explanatory purpose and relation to inconvenient evidence matter independently of optimism; identify a legitimate use for a deliberately pessimistic model and a conclusion it could not support on that basis alone.

A3: A report accurately records a history of exclusion and models its continuing effects, then evaluates proposed institutions against an ideal of equal standing. A critic rejects the report solely because it uses an ideal, claiming that Mills requires abandoning normative ideals altogether. Distinguish the ideal used as an evaluative standard from idealizing away actual domination. State a further criticism that could test the report’s method without repeating that conflation.

D3: A model expressly assumes away bargaining power to isolate the effect of a particular bargaining rule; it concludes only that the rule has an effect under that counterfactual condition. A ministry then cites it as proving that the rule alone will end exploitation in an unequal economy. Audit the transfer from the restricted model to the policy claim. Explain how the model could be useful while that use of it is misleading, and name the missing bridge premise.

## Passage selection and explanation

For any required unseen passage, the assessor uses the exact assigned edition and selects the first eligible contiguous paragraph span of 120–180 words in the specified section that was not annotated in practice, quoted in the paper or used in a previous assessment. Preserve necessary context, include the preceding paragraph as source-open context if needed, and record beginning/end words privately with edition and page/section. If no span is eligible, use the named alternate text; if that is also unavailable or exposed, the source-dependent task stays pending. Do not fabricate a passage or silently change translation. The selection and response stay in the local learner workspace, not in the distributable course.

For A/B/D, add a fresh 100–150-word span from the assigned primary reading least used in the essay to the twenty-minute explanation: five minutes to identify the claim and argumentative role; ten minutes to answer the neutral question, “Which premise of your response is least secure, and what change in that premise would alter your conclusion?”; five minutes to defend or revise the response. The learner specifies the change and explains its effect without live content help. The assessor may ask for clarification of that stated inference, but **must not reveal or borrow a case from the reserved alternate or delayed forms**. For C the fresh passage is already in F2/A2, so use the same neutral exchange to test the method’s warrant. A written exchange is equivalent. The essay’s separate subject-matched independent task already supplies a concrete changed-case defense.

## Feedback and completion record

For each row record: criterion; evidence in P and F/A; actual source support; uncertainty; one repair; result of fresh recheck if needed. Mark each row supported, unresolved or needs repair. Essential conceptual confusion, fabricated citation or absent independent evidence cannot be averaged away. After the delayed task, explain whether the least secure distinction transferred; a failed delayed task triggers targeted repair and a new unexposed case. Time beyond the planned repair allowance is logged as actual additional study, not silently hidden. No learner completion, expert validation, rights clearance or rendered accessibility outcome is implied by this authored packet.
