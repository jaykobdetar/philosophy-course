## C12D-M07: independent I07

Relational branch: unequal office or expertise does not automatically establish oppressive standing; inspect accountability, dependence, voice and the thesis’s actual criterion. Do not infer just relations from election alone. Moral-scope branch: a geographic boundary is not automatically the moral boundary; restoring omitted effects may defeat an apparent gain without resolving all disagreements about value. The stipulation is not evidence of an actual environmental project.

Select the branch before drafting by subject, withhold the specific independent stimulus until the paper is frozen, and retain the selection record. An approved equivalent question must match an existing branch’s target premise; otherwise narrow the question before approval. Assess changed-premise reasoning, faithful source use and a justified revision, including withdrawal of the essay conclusion.

## Essential row 1: F1/A1/D1

F1: equal votes and incomes do not decide whether majority norms silence others. Identify a cultural/relational mechanism and distinguish expressed support from evidence that participation is equal; do not assume that support is necessarily false consciousness. A1, retained: shift allocation and complaint authority are distinct mechanisms beyond the wage statistic. D1: outcomes can lag institutional change; the abolished rule’s causal contribution and present equal standing require different evidence, with the five-year appointment term relevant to the former.

## Essential row 2: F2/A2/D2

F2: honor the identical welfare/functions stipulation. Ask whether membership/integrity supplies an additional value, without treating native status as automatically decisive under Leopold. The disagreement can persist despite settled consequences. A2: hold normative premises fixed while varying ecological predictions, then identify a possible remaining dispute over value; neither report is established fact. D2: the stipulated absence of all sentient interests removes an instrumental welfare rationale without automatically removing Leopold’s community-based considerations. Do not force a consequentialist treatment of every kind of value.

## Essential row 3: F3/A3/D3

F3: ideological abstraction need not be optimistic; assess excluded evidence and explanatory use. A worst-case model can serve a bounded purpose without describing people exhaustively. A3: a normative ideal is not identical to idealizing away domination; evaluate whether the actual analysis and ideal interact defensibly. D3: a restricted counterfactual finding needs a bridge to unequal real institutions; indict the inflated application without pretending the restricted inference was invalid.
