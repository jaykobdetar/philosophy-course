# Optional listening handoff

Audiobooks and other authorized audio can help orientation and recall. Use them only where the specific edition and recording permit access; do not copy or redistribute an audiobook through this repository. No recording or transcript is required to start the course. The bounded written reading in each packet remains the assessed source. Optional background listening is not counted toward its focused close-reading hours.

After listening, record just two lines in a private learner note:

1. **One remembered argument:** name the claim and one reason, with the recording, location or approximate timestamp if available.
2. **One question:** name the exact claim, term or transition to check in the assigned text.

At the next focused session, locate the corresponding passage in the specified edition and correct the remembered argument against it. If the audio lacks a searchable transcript or matching markers, use the assigned written text or an authorized accessible equivalent instead. A mismatch between narration and course edition is a prompt to check attribution, never a reason to silently change the assigned passage.

The [opening Course 1 reading checklist](../current/readiness/course-01.md) gives the exact written alternatives for Sessions 1–4. Later modules need their own rights, transcript and locator check before an audio recommendation becomes a current assignment. Learners may complete every essential task without audio.
