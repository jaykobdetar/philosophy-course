# Opening learner pilot protocol

**Status: pending an actual learner.** No learner responses or time measurements are claimed here. An AI-run walkthrough can check wording and links but cannot complete PC-39 or validate learning. The user's 20 October instruction authorizes full course authoring before this empirical pilot. This protocol remains the validation task; authoring completion does not complete it.

## Scope

Pilot [Sessions 1–4](course-01/README.md) and [the representative Course 2 logic packet](course-02/first-logic-packet.md) when the learner reaches it. The learner may choose text dialogue instead of speech and may split sessions without penalty. Keep personal responses in an ignored local `learner/` folder.

## Evidence to capture

1. **Before instruction:** Retain the untouched Session 1 paragraph and a short baseline argument reconstruction, with prior experience and help disclosed.
2. **During sessions:** Note whether the learner found the exact passage, understood the task, completed a meaningful attempt, understood the feedback, and could name a specific repair. Record actual focused minutes, access friction, confusing wording, source uncertainty, and admin burden.
3. **After instruction:** Use [fresh transfer forms](course-01/assessment.md), with source access and permitted help recorded. Compare particular skills with baseline: attribution, reconstruction, countercase and revision. Do not infer a percentage of philosophical intelligence.
4. **After a delay:** Use a different changed case and one source passage after roughly four to six weeks or two later modules. Record retention and repairs, including whether earlier help was remembered.
5. **Decision:** For each packet, keep or revise a named feature with a reason and evidence. Mark only the actually observed scope as piloted.

## Stop and repair conditions

Pause the affected packet if the assigned source or endpoint cannot be found, a tutor fabricates a quotation, feedback cites unseen text, an independent check is coached, or the workload substantially exceeds the allocation. Revise the source route, task, or budget and rerun the relevant local checks. External expert review is a separate optional step and needs its own authorization and named remit.
