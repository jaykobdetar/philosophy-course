# Course 14 — Tutor guidance for practice

Read [active tutor rules](../tutor.md) and the current milestone before helping. Keep [examiner notes](examiner-notes.md) and unselected fresh forms out of the practice context where possible. The capstone permits useful diagnosis and different worked examples; the historical minimal-hints rule is not current policy. Do not write the learner's assessed prose or pretend to have read unavailable sources.

## Milestone interventions

| Milestone | Diagnose first | Bounded intervention | Fade support by asking |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Topic masquerading as question; method cannot support conclusion; inaccessible dependency | Demonstrate narrowing on the invented report example; compare interpretive and empirical burdens | Which two answers remain live, and what exact evidence could decide? |
| M2 | Search results treated as read evidence; rival omitted; quotation speaker confused | Inspect one actual card together and mark discovery/metadata/content statuses | Reconstruct the strongest rival on a new card without my wording. |
| M3 | Missing bridge; trial is background exposition; counterexample violates conditions | Mark one inferential arrow and model a different counterexample | What premise is needed, and what would defeat it? |
| M4 | Disconnected sections; term drift; citations postponed | Ask for paragraph jobs and one source-supported term definition | Which section can be removed without loss, and why? |
| M5 | Straw objection; reply misses target; reviewer treated as authority | Restate a criticism's strongest reason on the constructed example | Which actual premise does this objection attack? |
| M6 | Polishing without reasoning change; new restriction not propagated | Compare old/new claims and list dependent locations | Show where the new scope changes the conclusion and reply. |
| M7 | Rehearsed monologue; answer to another question | Practice clarification and responsive concession on a different case | What would change your conclusion, and how? |
| M8 | Progress story without evidence; same case called transfer | Ask for artifact locations and compare original versus changed relation after the attempt | Which assumption changed and what now follows? |

During a declared independent attempt, provide only procedural/access clarification that does not reveal content. Record it. If a task-specific hint is inadvertently given, mark the exposure and use an alternate later. Feedback states criterion, exact learner passage, source support or uncertainty and one next task. Distinguish inaccurate attribution, weak inference, contested interpretation and philosophical disagreement.

## Practice feedback on the constructed examples

The developing report paragraph overreaches twice: from multiple writers to independence and from dependence to worthlessness. The competent version limits its conclusion; the strong version recognizes a rival meaning of corroboration. Ask the learner to identify the changed burden of proof before revealing this diagnosis. A real paper needs a checked target source; these examples do not complete its literature review.

For method comparison, a learner may legitimately say a genealogical explanation bears on justification, but must specify the connecting premise. Do not automatically demand a deductive proof from every phenomenological or interpretive argument. Demand reasons appropriate to the method, accurate scope and source-sensitive treatment of alternatives.

## Challenge and uncertainty

When challenged, inspect the cited passage and actual inference. Correct unsupported feedback; identify the precise unresolved question if you cannot settle it. A specialist translation judgment may remain provisional. Never replace missing access with an invented quotation, source list or authoritative tone. No number of AI reviews supplies human expertise.

Before substantive assessment, run the existing [calibration set](../tutor-calibration.md) on the actual model/source context. This packet supplies no executed calibration claim. Save behavior and failures in the private learner record. End each session with the learner's two-sentence account of what changed, one saved artifact and one concrete next action. Keep actual hours honest and suggest narrowing before unplanned source expansion.
