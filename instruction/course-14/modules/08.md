Prerequisites: revised A6, U7 defense/repair record and T7 immediate transfer. Outcomes: assemble selective program evidence, complete final source audit, write a 1,000-word reflection, demonstrate delayed transfer and define a feasible next project.

## Teaching: evidence of development is specific

A reflection demonstrates change when it compares two actual pieces of work and identifies a changed capability. “I am more critical now” gives no evidence. “My early paragraph inferred that a claim was false because its origin was biased; the later paragraph separates its causal history from evidence against its truth and supplies the missing bridge” identifies a checkable change. It still needs actual locations in your work. Do not fabricate early mistakes or late successes to fit a story of progress.

A portfolio is an argument about what its author can do. Select artifacts for distinct evidential jobs, show assistance conditions and preserve inconvenient gaps. A late paper can demonstrate depth on one question without showing every skill in the program. Independent task records and source-sensitive earlier work prevent the portfolio from becoming one attractive document accompanied by unsupported claims.

Delayed transfer requires more than remembering your thesis. Identify which relation in a new case corresponds to the original distinction, which assumption has changed, and what consequence follows. A renamed version of the paper's own example provides weak evidence. A changed domain or reversal of a pivotal condition forces you to decide whether the method still applies. A well-argued refusal to transfer may be competent if the relevant structural difference is explained.

3. Independently complete S8 final source audit: check every quotation and significant attribution against the actual edition. 4. Write the 1,000-word reflection. Suggested structure: early/late argument comparison 350 words; one source/interpretation improvement 200; one substantive revision and why it mattered 200; assistance and remaining gaps 150; next study decision 100. Cite your artifact locations. Evaluate reasoning rather than effort alone.

## Delayed return: a separate occasion

After roughly four to six weeks (28–42 days) following the defense and immediate repairs, or after two later modules, complete D8 for 30 minutes. Because this is the final course, the default is a personal calendar appointment at five weeks. No intervening course is required. Store the date at M7's end; elapsed waiting time does not count as focused hours. Reserve 2 of the 18 hours: 30 minutes for the fresh attempt and 90 for feedback/correction. Do not rehearse the selected case or consult the paper immediately before the attempt.

Use a previously unseen D form in [assessment](../assessment.md). First spend five minutes recalling the project question, central distinction, main reason and limitation without notes. Then open the assigned sources/citation sheet and write 250–350 words applying the distinction to the new case, identifying one changed assumption and one limit. No live content help or prewritten replies. Mark recall versus source-open work. The reviewer keeps the examiner anchors withheld until submission. If prior exposure occurred, use the alternate; if both were seen, the examiner instantiates the documented method-sensitive case rule and records it before the attempt.

Check with criterion-level feedback after submission. If the distinction was recalled but mechanically misapplied, reconstruct the changed relation and attempt another new case after a recorded further delay. Use remaining M8 time where possible; log additional remediation explicitly. Do not mark delayed transfer complete before the actual return.
