# Course 14 — Independent research capstone

**180 focused hours · eight milestones · authored 20 October 2026.** This packet implements the [current capstone](../../current/courses/14.md) and [shared assessment](../../current/assessment.md). All teaching and instruments are authored; actual learner work, source access for the eventual question, a learner pilot, human review and rendered accessibility validation remain pending. Constructed examples demonstrate moves, not completed learner research. No institutional certification or publication-level originality is implied.

Begin after the common courses, a global tradition, selected area and advanced seminar, or documented equivalent evidence. Bring your seminar paper, its source records, a viable research question and access to essential sources. Use [outcomes](outcomes.md) to locate missing prerequisite evidence. A 5,000–7,000-word research paper is the default product. Bibliography and administrative records are outside that word range; substantive argumentative footnotes count. This word-count convention is a local implementation choice.

All reading, practice, feedback, independent work and revision are inside these totals; module tables show the allocation. Final delayed work remains charged to Milestone 8 even if completed after the main study sequence. Prior course hours are not charged again; capstone rereading, selection and new analysis do consume capstone hours. There are no pre-existing Course 14 opening-session credits. If more research is required, narrow the question first or record explicitly added time. No PC-25–33 content addition belongs uniquely to this capstone; prerequisite additions retain their own course accounting.

At five to seven focused hours a week, 180 hours takes approximately 26–36 active study weeks. Calendar gaps can extend this. Break the modules into rested sessions; a milestone is not one week. Schedule the final delayed sample 28–42 calendar days after the defense and immediate repairs, or after two genuinely later modules if applicable. With no later course, put a dated return appointment in your own calendar. This packet does not create an automation. Reserve 30 minutes for the independent sample and 90 minutes for checking/repair within Milestone 8.

## How to work

Read the current module first. Use [templates](templates.md), [constructed worked examples](worked-examples.md), and the [source-selection procedure](source-selection.md). The fixed methodological readings have exact local sections or linked author-page headings; topic readings become exact only when your approved dossier lists actual editions and ranges. The course does not guess a bibliography for an unknown question.

Practice can include source-bound diagnosis and a different worked example. Independent artifacts permit the assigned sources, source/citation sheet and accessibility tools, but no live content help, generated prose, examiner notes or prewritten answers. Declare help already received. Diagnosis of a draft can precede independent rewriting; the learner must make and explain the actual changes. If someone supplied replacement assessed text, remove it and collect fresh learner-authored evidence. [Assessment](assessment.md) gives learner conditions; [examiner notes](examiner-notes.md) contain withheld anchors and prompt-selection rules. Keep that file out of practice context until submission where practical; this is not a secure examination system.

Audio is optional: listen to your own notes or an authorized recording of a source already selected, then write one remembered inference and one uncertainty. The written/text-accessible version is always sufficient. Focused listening may substitute for equivalent first-pass orientation if logged; distracted listening does not replace close reading. Before citing anything heard, check the exact written edition and locator. Do not generate or redistribute third-party recordings without a checked permission route. Typed unscripted dialogue, assistive speech, breaks and extra processing time may preserve the defense task; record adjustments without grading accent or speed.

Finish with evidence for every essential row, not a percentage. Unresolved central source errors, an invalid pivotal inference, unavailable essential evidence or inability to explain the paper requires repair. Missing external review leaves a provisional assessment label, not an indefinite ban on study. Continue to [future independent study](modules/08.md) only after recording the remaining limitations honestly.

Maintainers: [source audit](source-audit.md), [authoring metadata](authoring.json), and `python3 instruction/course-14/validate.py` from the repository root provide reproducible structural checks. These checks do not establish philosophical quality, permissions or learner success.
