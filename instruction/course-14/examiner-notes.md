# Course 14 — Examiner guidance (withhold until the attempt)

Use with [learner conditions](assessment.md), [outcome map](outcomes.md) and the [active rules](../tutor.md). This file is not practice reading. The bank is accessible in a self-study repository; record exposure rather than claiming a sealed exam. Select an unseen alternate if a form was rehearsed. All judgments require artifact locations and source support. No learner has completed this capstone merely because the packet exists.

## Administration and criterion decisions

Before relying on an AI assessor, run the existing [calibration protocol](../tutor-calibration.md) with the actual model and sources; record its performance separately. A source-bound AI/self review is provisional. Human review is optional and must be described by actual scope; do not imply an expert was contacted. Give accommodations that preserve independence and spontaneous response; timing is a planning allocation, not a speed cutoff.

Assess each outcome as developing, independently competent or strong, with unresolved integrity/access issues separately marked. Competence requires the actual task's observable criteria. Strength in one row never compensates for an unresolved other row. Record interpretation disagreements separately from demonstrable errors. A defensible philosophical conclusion can differ from yours.

For the proposal, dossier, trial, draft and revision, inspect the learner's named artifacts plus assistance record. For A6 ensure the learner authored the sustained argument; a generated paper with fluent oral summaries does not meet that condition. Declare what the process record can establish and what remains uncertain. If replacement prose was supplied, collect independently rewritten affected sections and a new inferential question; do not simply relabel the old artifact.

## Task-specific anchors and repair

**F1A.** Competent work distinguishes bibliographic existence from readable support, identifies precisely what the missing source was meant to do and either narrows the question to the accessible evidence or justifies suspension. Reject “the abstract probably supports me” as source evidence. Strong work preserves the intellectual goal while stating a rival the new question can still address. Repair: a claim/source dependency table and alternate F1B.

**F1B.** The textual evidence may establish stated reasons or conceptual commitments; it does not alone establish how societies behaved. Competent work changes the conclusion to an interpretive/comparative one or identifies the specific empirical evidence now required and its access cost. Strong work explains why the narrower comparison matters without treating authors as transparent representatives of whole societies. Repair: separate textual, historical and evaluative claims, then fresh rescoping of a different dependency.

**F2A.** The draft attributes the rejected sincerity test to N, erases its restriction to checked reasons, ignores the open inquiry question and overstates what was read. A competent corrected attribution reports N's rejection and conditional alternative, identifying paragraph 1; it does not claim a complete inquiry theory. No need to quote. Strong work explains that the known paragraph 2 further limits the argument and that “complete theory” is not justified metadata. Repair by auditing a new actual dossier claim, not rewriting the model answer.

**F2B.** R assumes accuracy for argument; it does not discover prevalence. Possibility under no-warning conditions differs from “usually know” and from “always reasonable.” The purported quotation is not present and must be removed, not softened into another quotation. Competent work distinguishes assumption, conditional claim and empirical frequency, with accurate attribution to both paragraphs. Fabrication is an integrity issue requiring correction before judgment, not merely a low style score. Strong work notes that no-warning availability remains unestablished empirically. Repair: inspect a new draft claim with the same distinctions.

**F3 argument/application A.** Trace the conclusion's dependence on the withdrawn supplied premise; either offer a different justified route or state the conclusion no longer follows. Strong work distinguishes weakening confidence from loss of entailment. **B:** test whether the offered counterexample genuinely satisfies all premises. A contradictory counterexample can be rejected with reasons; a genuine one requires repair. Do not reward inventing an exception and then ignoring its cost.

**F3 interpretation A.** Two readings compatible with one sentence need additional discriminating context. A second quotation is useful only if its role actually favors one reading. **B:** verify the nearby qualification in the real source before assigning it. Competence revises the attributed claim and tests the criticism against that revised target. An inability to select an actual passage is an examiner preparation gap, not learner failure.

**F3 comparison/method A.** A competent answer refuses identity of concepts based on shared translation while retaining a narrower justified comparison. **B:** description or genealogy does not by itself warrant the evaluative result; an explicit bridge can be defended, qualified or withdrawn. Strong work explains why the chosen standard of evaluation is appropriate. Repair: targeted source/context reconstruction followed by the other unseen form. Record newly selected actual loci in the prompt record.

**F4A.** Inspect the relationship among the two actual sections and final thesis. Competent work may show the second section independently suffices, provides only partial support, or is itself dependent on the missing first reason. Judge the demonstrated relation rather than require failure. The transition must reflect the new burden. **F4B:** competent work preserves the objection's force and gives a substantive narrower conclusion or argues that none survives. Strong work tracks changes to introduction, inference and conclusion. Repair: new dependency map and a fresh removal/change of a different premise.

**F6A.** Universalizing a restriction requires evidence beyond an attractive broader claim. Competent work rejects unsupported expansion or defends it with a genuine argument; not all restrictions are necessary, so do not predetermine the verdict. Strong work tests a boundary case and makes the revised wording match. **F6B:** a controversial premise is not thereby false or indefensible. Competent work states the rival's actual reason, answers it or acknowledges that it survives. Strong work distinguishes dialectical relevance from truth. Repair: diagnose a different review demand and independently rewrite its affected inference.

## U7: selecting a fresh unscripted challenge

Read the actual paper, source sheet and prior-exposure list. Write the questions into the examiner record before the exchange, without sharing them. Use 10–15 minutes for the presentation and 20–30 for questioning; do not add the generic shared twenty-minute dialogue. Select at least one truly unrehearsed challenge and adapt follow-ups to the actual response.

Choose questions from these families and bind them to a precise paper passage:

- Pivotal inference: “On page/section X you move from P to C. If P is granted but condition K is removed, what follows?” K must matter and must not be a new untaught factual claim. Ask which premise restores the inference if needed.
- Interpretation: “You reject reading R of passage L. Which actual textual detail most strongly favors your reading? How would your argument change if R were right?” Supply the actual passage and use a plausible alternative, not a fabricated author view.
- Source integrity: “This paragraph uses source S as evidence for Q. Does S assert Q, assume it, or report it as an opponent's position? Show the relevant context.” Select an attribution outside the repeatedly rehearsed examples.
- Objection/revision: “The best rival accepts your case but denies your bridge premise. State its strongest reason; what would you concede?” Follow with a new implication from the answer, not a memorized list.
- Change of mind: “Name a finding or argument that would change your conclusion. Would it refute the claim, restrict its scope, or only change its applicability?” Ask how the distinction affects one actual conclusion sentence.

Competent answers identify the question, respond with a relevant reason, locate support when asked, recognize a real limitation and can change a claim. Strong answers clarify the underlying issue without evading the question and track downstream effects. Do not demand immediate encyclopedic recall, penalize processing pauses or score confidence. A self-administered form with recorded questions and answers supplies limited provisional evidence; disclose lack of independent interaction. If a serious error appears, record it, allow correction and ask a new unrehearsed follow-up after repair. Do not grant completion solely because the exchange lasted long enough.

## T7/D8 anchors by case

These cases test the learner's central distinction, not agreement with a prescribed moral or metaphysical answer. Require an explicit mapping to a real project passage, a relevant difference and a reasoned consequence. Mere mention of the project's vocabulary is insufficient.

Evidence lane: T7's observed rain with the sprinkler off yields wet soil under both H and J, so that observation alone does not discriminate between them or establish H. A competent further test holds rain absent and runs the sprinkler: H predicts dry soil, J wet soil. The answer must state both predictions and connect their difference to the learner's project distinction. Merely saying that more evidence is needed is insufficient. The case stipulates the mechanisms; do not import real gardening complications. Strong work distinguishes discrimination between these two live accounts from proof that the selected account exhausts every possible explanation. D8A changes from competing accounts of a known condition to discordant measurements whose reliability depends on an unknown condition; independence does not identify the applicable temperature condition. Competent work does not infer that both instruments are worthless or that one is dishonest. D8B separates independent translations from completeness of source context; agreement does not recover an omitted qualification. Strong work states which additional context check would matter. These are analytic features of invented cases, not scientific conclusions.

Value lane: T7 distinguishes equal voting entitlement from practical participation or the distribution of burdens; multiple normative conclusions can be defended. D8A introduces urgency, limited duration and later review; an answer must explain whether those changes bear on the central principle rather than simply repeat the club verdict. D8B distinguishes formal veto from usable power under credible retaliation. Strong work states which fact or normative premise drives the change and which remains disputed. Do not require one theory's verdict.

Interpretation/method lane: T7 differentiates a depicted speaker's defense from narrator endorsement; a narrator's evaluation is itself an interpretive datum, not automatic proof of the author's view. D8A changes dialogue to memoir and adds explicit temporal rejection, so past belief does not establish current endorsement. D8B requires tracking the criterion across a chain of comparisons. A and B share the stated justification; B and C share the revision rule. Those two similarities do not establish agreement between A and C on either dimension: their justifications and revision rules both differ. Competent work keeps one criterion fixed across all three and reports the resulting relations accurately, or gives a two-dimension comparison that preserves the differences. Strong work explains why an alleged higher-level equivalence needs an explicit common criterion and further evidence; it must not be inferred solely from overlapping pairs. The shared fact that all endorse the same rule can support that limited comparison but not identity of position. Connect this diagnosis to the actual project distinction; do not substitute a generic warning against comparison. This is a change of comparison criterion, not a genealogy-to-evaluation inference.

If a prewritten lane is poorly matched to the project, prepare a new prompt before the attempt using this bounded rule: select the central distinction and one familiar case from the paper; move to a different domain; change exactly one pivotal relation (e.g. dependence to independence, voluntary to constrained action, author assertion to reported speech); specify all facts needed; require mapping, revised inference and one limit. Record the actual 100–150-word case, the changed condition and an examiner anchor before delivery. Do not ask the learner to invent the assessment or introduce specialist facts. Use a different domain/condition for the delayed form. This procedure is for genuine topic fit or exposed forms, not a claim that an uninstantiated case has been assessed.

## S8 and final judgment

Audit actual source use independently: every quotation and significant attribution needs an edition, precise locator, inspected context and honest read scope. The learner's audit is independent work; the reviewer checks its adequacy against accessible evidence and marks inaccessible judgments provisional. A misquotation must be corrected everywhere it affects interpretation. For contested reading, state competing readings and evidence, not a false certainty.

D8 occurs after 28–42 days or two later modules. Record dates and actual minutes against M8. Short retrieval inside milestones does not count. If delayed transfer is weak, identify whether the problem is recall, mapping, inference or scope; prescribe one targeted practice and a new changed-case attempt after a recorded delay. Exceeding the existing budget requires honest extra-time logging. Finish only when every essential row has practice and independently competent evidence; no numerical total can override a missing row. Record source/access limits, any absent human review, learner-pilot status and the exact scope of any verified outcome.
