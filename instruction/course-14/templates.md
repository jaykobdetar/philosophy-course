# Reusable capstone forms

Copy blank forms into your private learner workspace. These are structures, not completed evidence. Do not upload private draft material or copyrighted source packets into distributable course files.

## T1 — Proposal and decision record

- Question, exactly one interrogative sentence:
- Why two informed readers might disagree:
- Provisional answer and scope conditions:
- Contribution: distinction / objection / interpretation / application / failed comparison; specify the one move:
- Method and what would count as support or failure:
- Numbered central argument, including a marked supplied premise:
- Strongest anticipated objection, its target, and tentative reply:
- Essential primary passage(s), edition, locator, actual access test:
- Rival scholarship needed and discovery route:
- Exclusions and why excluding them does not beg the question:
- 5,000–7,000-word feasibility; remaining reading estimate:
- Decision: proceed / narrow / change adjacent question; date and reason:

## T2 — Source card and reading contract

Source ID; full citation; author; title; edition/translator; venue/date; DOI or stable catalogue/author/publisher link; actual copy used; legal access route and permission limitations; exact assigned range; pages/sections actually read; whole item or excerpt; date read; bibliographic metadata verified where/date; quotation verification status.

Question addressed; source argument in your own words; passage supporting this reconstruction; strongest rival reading; claim you plan to support; whether source asserts that claim, reports an opponent, or supports only your further inference; dissenting evidence; remaining context required. Add source role (primary / interpretation / objection / empirical premise / orientation). A title appearing in a search is “discovered,” not “read.” A source card can record “unavailable: not usable as support.”

Reading contract table: source ID | exact range | purpose | new/rechecked | planned hours/pages | actual hours/pages | access confirmed | stop/expand decision. Aim for roughly 40–70 additional close-reading pages in M2, adjusted for density and exact editions. Eight to twelve items is a likely working set, not a quota.

## T3 — Argument and section plan

Question → answer → pivotal premise → warrant → serious objection → reply or qualified conclusion. For each arrow write why the step follows. Section table: section title | argumentative job | premises/conclusion | source IDs and locators | words | what fails if removed. Mark empirical assumptions, interpretive assumptions and normative premises separately.

## T4 — Claim/source and contribution ledger

Paragraph ID | exact significant attribution or quotation | source ID/locator | quotation/paraphrase/my inference | checked against actual copy/date | context or translation caveat | correction needed. For original claims use “my argument” and provide the supporting inference; do not fabricate a source. For a source-free constructed case use “invented case” and state its assumptions.

Contribution statement: “Earlier position P relies on ___; I show ___ by ___; this changes ___; it leaves ___ unresolved.” Fill it without claiming nobody has ever made the point. A literature search supports a bounded claim about sources checked, not universal novelty.

## T5 — Review request and response plan

Assignment/version; exact question; method; one-page argument map; source packet with edition/locator and permitted short context; relevant draft sections; assistance declaration. Questions: Where is the most consequential misreading? Which inference has least support? What is the strongest objection to the actual conclusion? Record reviewer type and limits. Preparing this packet does not authorize sending it to anyone.

Response table: comment verbatim or clearly labelled paraphrase | target passage | error/disagreement/uncertainty | accept/modify/reject/defer | reason and evidence | precise change | dependency elsewhere | check after revision. If advice conflicts, compare the two reasons with the source and question; do not count votes.

## T6 — Three-change revision memo

For each of three major changes: old claim and location; problem; relevant criticism/evidence; new claim and location; how the inference/interpretation/scope changed; remaining limit. If fewer than three substantive defects were found, explain the three most consequential actual changes without inventing errors. Include one rejected suggestion with a reason. Add an authorship/assistance statement covering search, explanation, feedback, drafting and editing tools.

## T7 — Defense and independent-task record

Prompt/form ID; date; prior exposure; allowed materials; presentation duration; exchange duration; accommodations; question actually asked; answer summary or accessible transcript excerpt; relevant evidence; what would change the conclusion; errors admitted; repair action. Mark reviewer human/AI/self and any unavailable source. No reviewer signature is fabricated.

## T8 — Completion and future-study record

Row Q/S/A/R/U/T | practice artifact and locator | independent artifact and locator | observed criterion | satisfactory/developing/unresolved | reason | repair and added time. Portfolio index: artifact | date | why selected | assistance/independence conditions | limitation. Delayed return: defense/repair end date | return date 28–42 days later | form unseen? | actual attempt minutes | feedback/repair minutes.

Future study: one unanswered question; next finite source range and access check; method; output; estimated focused hours; stop rule; why this is a new project. Separate this estimate from the completed 180-hour capstone.
