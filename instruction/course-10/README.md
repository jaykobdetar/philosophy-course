# Course 10 — Philosophy of science

Complete authored teaching sequence, 20 October 2026. **120 baseline hours; 126 only if the optional six-hour PC-31 probability bridge is taken.** Source curriculum: [Course 10](../../current/courses/10.md). Prerequisites: Course 1 and Course 5's induction module. Basic propositional logic helps; advanced mathematics and first-order proof proficiency are not assumed.

The course asks how evidence supports theories, how explanations work, how scientific commitments change, and how social criticism can sustain objectivity. A philosophical analysis can clarify a scientific inference without independently certifying its empirical premises. Every scientific claim in the case paper must remain traceable to the bounded packet.
