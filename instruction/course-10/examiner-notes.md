## Module independent tasks

**I1, returned batteries.** Within returns, P(flash|fault) = 72/80 = 90%, while P(fault|flash) = 72/74 = 36/37, about 97.30%. The latter need not describe retail batteries: the sampling frame was selected by return, and the retail base rate/transport assumptions are missing. Hume's foundational challenge remains even with a representative sample; selection bias is a contingent methodological flaw, not the same argument. A competent testing scheme states H plus working detector and defined sampling conditions entails a predicted pattern. Repair a reversed denominator by selecting the conditioning group; repair “Hume proves sampling useless” by returning to IV/V.

**I2, rare failures.** A 1% chance claim does not say zero units fail. Three observed failures do not by themselves form a deductive contradiction; assess statistical evidence only after specifying the model, sampling assumptions and decision rule. No advanced calculation is needed. An unrestricted “every failure is an exception” removes a constraint unless independently characterized. Credit a justified proposal to inspect dependence or selection. Do not require the learner to announce acceptance/rejection from an unstated threshold.

**I3, two dials.** The correlation and A's reading allow prediction of B, but the common master gear can explain both; intervention on A alone need not alter B. In the direct-drive variant an intervention on A can matter to B through the stipulated mechanism. Competence reconstructs both without invalidating the correlation-based deduction. Strong work distinguishes information used to learn B from what produces/explains B. A blanket “all deduction fails” overstates the objection.

**I4, detectors and standards.** Precision improvement alone is consistent with normal science; changed causes, standards and exemplary solutions may support a revolution even without a new detector. These facts underdetermine a real historical classification: ask whether community commitments, scope or central exemplars changed as described. The answer must not call the second case revolutionary merely because it contains the word “different.” Require checked Kuhn locators; the authoring did not inspect the assigned chapters.

**I5, links/glow.** The new observation discriminates R from S, unlike the practiced equivalence case. It supports R's observable predictions and empirical adequacy without deductively establishing exact truth about links. Constructive empiricism can accept R and its research use while remaining agnostic about unobservables. A realist can reasonably regard the success as defeasible evidence, but accepting all detailed claims with certainty is stronger. Reward recognition that empirical adequacy goes beyond existing data.

**I6, abstract/editorial.** The abstract can establish what method and estimate its authors report; it cannot supply inspection of every modeling choice or robustness analysis. The exact method/claim must be checked against the actual abstract; do not fill these details from this key. A criticism process can expose assumptions without rendering conclusions arbitrary. Competence names a possible objection, evidence needed and uptake, with a Longino locator. If the source is inaccessible, judge the conceptual argument separately and leave source comprehension unresolved.

**I7, distribution of burdens.** The unchanged scientific premise survives by stipulation. A policy conclusion that depends on a prior comparison may require new distributive evidence or a value judgment. An epistemic thesis may survive while its practical application changes. There is no mandatory “change your conclusion” verdict: competence identifies the learner's actual premise and its role. Strong work recognizes how a formally unchanged priority can yield a different policy when omitted people are included. Do not invent a real finding about the default packet.

## Fresh and alternate written forms

**F1.** H-and-filter-and-detector implies no violet. Violet conflicts with the conjunction; a detector self-check supports only one auxiliary and is itself defeasible. Independently test the filter or replace it under specified controls; predict an outcome that would distinguish the repair from H's failure. A no-violet repeat is consistent with H but may be consistent with rivals. Competence corrects both reporter and laboratory, without insisting H can never be rejected. Strong work describes how an auxiliary test itself has assumptions and how independent evidence reduces rather than magically abolishes the uncertainty.

**A1.** Five absences in 100 are compatible with a claim of a 95% production rate; neither exact agreement with the rate nor a nonzero count proves the theory. Incorrect shielding labels may affect whether specified conditions held, but post hoc exclusion without checking labels is not independent support. Propose an auditable relabelling or fresh blinded run with recorded shielding. An answer that says “five failures refute 95%” replaces the original claim by a universal. An answer that says the chance claim is forever immune also fails. Statistical thresholds are not required.

**F2.** The restricted rule plus V's being a sample of material M, its copper label and its temperature above T entails melting in the stipulated model. The general rule shows the label is dispensable: material and temperature suffice regardless of label. The result challenges the inference from inclusion in a valid derivation to explanatory relevance. It need not refute the repaired law-based account. Competence distinguishes valid inference, relevant conditions and surplus information. Strong work avoids claiming that adding any irrelevant true sentence automatically destroys an otherwise good explanation; the specific error is attributing explanatory work to the label merely because it appears in the argument. Verify Hempel attribution against the actual chapter.

**A2.** A's start time plus the one-minute rule yields melting at 12:01, explaining its occurrence/timing under the stipulated conditions. To derive why A precedes B, include B's start time and the same rule, yielding 12:06. A-only premises do not determine B's melting time: B could have begun earlier while A's facts stayed fixed. Competence separates the two explananda and supplies the missing comparison condition rather than declaring the first derivation invalid. Strong work explains that adequacy depends partly on the precise question answered and does not mistake different questions for rival causal mechanisms. Check any chapter-specific attribution; the case itself supplies the needed timing premises.

**F3.** Accepting T with belief in its empirical adequacy does not establish belief in all its detailed unobservable claims. Belief that couplers exist is distinct from belief in T's interaction mechanism, and the case explicitly withholds the latter. A realist acceptance commitment reaches beyond empirical adequacy to truth claims about the theoretical account, possibly qualified; existence belief alone does not establish that commitment to all of T. Constructive empiricism's weaker requirement for acceptance must not be turned into a requirement to deny entities. Distinguish a minimum belief required by acceptance from a ban on every additional belief a person may have for separate reasons. The case gives no grounds to validate the metaphysical argument or to infer the scientist's entire philosophical position from one utterance. Competence keeps required, optional and actually stated commitments separate. Strong work explains why both colleagues overinfer while avoiding a categorical label for the scientist without further clarification. Verify the interpretation of acceptance against the assigned §§1–3; this key does not claim that an unsighted passage independently confirms the extra-belief analysis.

**A3.** Unobserved is not unobservable. Observable-in-principle future phenomena fall within empirical adequacy; fitting present records is insufficient. An empiricist cannot insulate a failed prediction by saying it had not yet been observed. Successful discrimination favors the theory's empirical claims without making every proposition about its structure certain. A realist's beliefs can be revised if the experiment fails. This form tests observational scope rather than only underdetermination.

**F4.** Check the learner's actual selected A.2.1 clause and footnote 4 before judging exact content. The key does not certify an unsighted clause. Confidence is not by itself a numerical probability of a local policy being “correct.” Evidence about local hazard/exposure, effectiveness/cost and a decision criterion is needed; one empirical and one evaluative premise must be explicit. Strong work distinguishes source scope from locality and notes that “most expensive” neither establishes nor defeats effectiveness. It should not imply all action requires certainty.

**A4.** Check A.2.2 and footnote 4 directly. The priority for the worst-off is already supplied; a response claiming “no value premise at all” misses the changed case. Missing information concerns who receives benefits, policy effectiveness and whether the means serve that priority. Uncertainty does not make every option equally supported. A criticism route should name a relevant challenge and how it could receive uptake, with source-grounded Longino analysis. Exact scientific claims and chapter attribution remain unresolved if sources cannot be inspected.

## Explanation and paper defense

For the first eight minutes, require the distinction between demonstrative impossibility of nature's change and the circular use of past experience to justify future resemblance, followed by a separate account of custom. A learner may challenge the exhaustiveness or demand for justification if the reconstruction is fair. “Induction has failed sometimes, so always distrust it” is not the required argument.

**F-defense:** successful independent support for an auxiliary can strengthen the case against that source of error. It need not close an unrelated value, explanatory or realism gap. Require the learner to locate the exact affected premise in the paper and explain relevance, rather than reward automatic strengthening. **A-defense:** mechanism replacement threatens claims identifying the original unobservable account as true more directly than claims about empirical adequacy or policy priorities. Neither answer is mechanically fixed: inspect the paper's actual commitments. Strong work answers an objection and acknowledges the remaining limits of the abstract/synthesis.
