## Reading order and output

Within M06's five-hour reading allocation, first read Longino chapter 4, then the SPM Introduction/footnote, A.1/A.2, figure/caption, and abstract. Give the packet about 2.5 hours, adjusting inside the block if Longino needs more time; log actual timing. For the figure, identify the variables, units, population/time scope, legend and uncertainty information from the actual source. If any part cannot be accessed, name it explicitly rather than infer it from memory. A text description must preserve these functions; it may replace visual access once checked against the official figure/caption, not merely omit the evidence.

Create **P6**, a ledger with at least eight entries: two A.1 claims; two A.2 claims; one figure claim; the abstract's stated method; the abstract's main conclusion; and one policy inference you formulate. Use the following columns or a numbered equivalent:

| Source and locator | Claim in your own words | Type of evidence or method actually stated | Scope | Uncertainty wording | Background assumption or value | What this does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fill from actual passage | Keep this distinct from interpretation | Do not invent omitted methods | Population, timescale and target | Check footnote 4; avoid automatic percent conversion | Mark your inference as such | Name a specific limit |

Practice tutoring discusses A.1.1 and another A.1 claim; the final written forms use A.2.1 and A.2.2 without item-specific coaching. Reading the assigned packet is permitted exposure; rehearsing the final item is not. Record the difference.

Then write 250 words comparing the abstract and synthesis. Identify their purposes, breadth and how uncertainty is reported. Do not assume that different ranges contradict one another before checking target quantities, periods and conventions. Do not claim that this study directly supplies Figure SPM.1 without tracing that relation; no such relation was verified here. An abstract's absence of a limitation does not prove the full paper lacks it.

Finally write 150 words on a criticism route: identify an assumption, a possible relevant objection, the evidence needed to evaluate it, who has pertinent expertise or affected experience, and what uptake would look like. An actual documented objection is not required; label a proposed one as hypothetical. Do not impersonate a domain expert or suggest that philosophy alone settles attribution science.

## Citation and access rule

Use source name, year and exact SPM paragraph/figure or “abstract” locator. Distinguish paraphrase from a quotation and from your inference. A quotation from an unsighted passage cannot be checked and must remain absent from the final paper until accessed. Store legitimate short source excerpts used for review in the private learner workspace, not this distributable packet. Full texts are linked, not reproduced.
