## Reproducibility and exact limits

`check.py` reports a SHA-256 for the local Hume comparison text and validates assigned local paths. Its optional `--external` flag checks URL transport status only, without executing JavaScript or rendering any navigator. External status may differ by time, host or authentication; record fresh outcomes separately and do not overwrite this historical audit. A 200 is not proof of full passage availability, correct edition, accessibility or permission.

No quotations from copyrighted readings were added to the lessons. Original explanations require learner comparison with the actual assigned text; full source-dependent assessment remains pending access for Popper, Hempel, Kuhn, van Fraassen, Longino and the scientific packet. The exact blocked components are chapter-specific attribution/locators, SPM uncertainty/figure comprehension, and the abstract's stated method/claim.
