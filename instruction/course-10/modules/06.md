## Explanation

A set of measurements supports a claim through background assumptions: what the instrument measures, which comparison is relevant, and which alternative explanations matter. An assumption's presence is not evidence of fraud. Some assumptions are empirically testable; others concern selection, categorization or explanatory priorities. Values can shape what questions receive resources, what risks deserve attention, and what errors have serious consequences. These roles should be distinguished before claiming that a particular conclusion is biased.

Longino's account redirects attention from the imagined isolation of an impartial individual to social inquiry in which assumptions can encounter criticism. For the assigned reading, identify what makes criticism able to change the evidential situation, not just the existence of many people. This lesson uses four diagnostic questions: where can objections be raised, are they answered, what standards govern the answer, and whose relevant expertise can actually be heard? Verify how the assigned chapter develops its criteria before attributing a precise formulation to Longino. Consensus alone does not establish that criticism was effective; disagreement alone does not establish that inquiry failed. An objection must engage the evidence or the assumptions connecting it to the conclusion.

The IPCC uses calibrated uncertainty language. Confidence assesses evidential support and agreement; likelihood uses probability language for outcomes where appropriate. These have different roles. A label of high confidence cannot be mechanically translated into a particular percentage. Read footnote 4 before assigning any numerical meaning, and copy a percentage threshold only when the packet explicitly supplies it. The magnitude of an estimated change, uncertainty about that estimate, confidence in a broader conclusion, and a recommendation about action are four distinguishable matters.

A research abstract and a synthesis have different scopes. An abstract offers a compressed claim and stated method from a study; it is not enough to inspect all analysis choices, data quality, limitations or robustness. A synthesis assesses a wider body of work. Their similarity does not establish a direct citation relationship. Never infer that Gillett directly underlies Figure SPM.1 without tracing references beyond this packet; such tracing is outside the required task. A philosophical paper can assess a move from evidence to belief or policy while acknowledging that it has not independently reanalyzed the science.

## Worked invented example

A flood panel reports strong evidence for increased exposure but leaves the choice between a barrier and relocation open. A committee chooses a barrier because it gives extra weight to protecting existing homes. The exposure estimate, the forecast model's assumptions, the priority given to current residents and the policy choice are distinct steps. Disagreement about that priority need not undermine the exposure estimate. Conversely, an affected group's report that the exposure map omits informal dwellings could challenge a measurement assumption, not merely express a preference. Inviting comments without correcting a demonstrated omission is weaker than a process with documented uptake.
