# C10-M08 — Assessment, repair and delayed transfer

**8 hours. Guiding question:** Can I use these distinctions independently when a consequential premise changes? Prerequisite: M01–07 and access to assigned passages. Outcomes: demonstrate every essential row, reconstruct an argument without notes, defend a bounded case-paper inference, and transfer the least secure distinction after a delay.

**Reading:** no new required source. Reopen the exact assigned passages needed for the source-open written sample and citation check; preserve editions in [source audit](../source-audit.md). Learner prompts are in [assessment](../assessment.md); the [examiner guidance](../examiner-notes.md) remains separate during attempts.

## Exact allocation — 480 minutes

| Activity | Minutes |
|---|---:|

| Fresh written sample: four rows × 25 minutes | 100 |
| Independent explanation/defense | 20 |

| **Total** | **480 = 8 hours** |

The four-row grid overrides the generic “about ninety minutes” introduction. The 50-minute repair block can resample at most two rows at 25 minutes each; if more are unresolved, record the remaining need and additional actual time rather than compress all outcomes into inadequate samples. Accommodations may extend access time; retain outcome criteria and log the change. All post-feedback work is marked as such.

## Preparation and worked example

Create four rows labelled induction/testing, explanation/change, realism, values/uncertainty. For each enter the named practice artifact, evidence location, fresh item, decision and needed repair. Paper quality does not substitute for all fresh items. A worked invented decision: a learner gives a sound realism comparison but misidentifies high confidence as a numerical likelihood. Mark the realism row secure if its evidence warrants that; mark uncertainty unresolved, identify the footnote distinction, and use the alternate uncertainty item after repair. Do not average the error away because the prose was fluent.

Use the first 30 minutes to identify access gaps and reconstruct your case paper's central inference from memory; afterward check it against the submitted paper. During the actual 20-minute defense, use the reconstruction prompt specified in assessment. It is not a second coached version of the readiness exercise.

## Independent occasion and criteria

Use Form F unless exposed, then Form A. Each form contains four fully specified prompts with changed hypotheses, mechanisms, observational status and policy premises. Work source-open except the first eight minutes of the defense. No generative completion, tutor hints or examiner anchors during the attempt. A reader, scribe, dictation tool or accessible text format may support access without supplying reasoning; declare their use. If a source is unavailable, do the other items and mark the exact reading-dependent criterion unresolved.

A competent response reconstructs the actual claim, gives an explicit reason, uses the specified source where required, and states a relevant limitation. Strong responses investigate a consequential assumption or rival interpretation. Developing responses may be relevant but miss a denominator, replace a chance claim by a universal, conflate acceptance with truth, or hide the policy premise. Refer to the shared rubric for a fair evidence-based decision; style cannot compensate for these inferential errors.

## Repair, retrieval and course exit

Spend the repair block on at most two consequential problems, each with an exact passage or inferential criterion, learner revision, and one new task from Form A. Record the original answer and revised answer separately. The [tutor notes](../tutor-notes.md) explain how to challenge unsupported feedback.

Set the delayed occasion for roughly four to six weeks after the fresh assessment, or after two later modules. Use D1 or D2 according to the least secure row, not an already rehearsed prompt. The 30 minutes include a 20-minute independent response and ten minutes for evidence review; written/recorded formats are equivalent. Delay is calendar time, not added study hours; this half hour is charged once here. If the delayed sample fails, record the limitation and plan a specific additional repair rather than declare completion.

Exit record: list demonstrated outcomes and unresolved rows; paper and revision locations; source/assistance declaration; actual hours; delayed date/result; chosen continuation and any optional bridge credit. Completion of this introductory course does not confer independent climate-science expertise, a degree, or advanced Bayesian competence. No learner result has been produced by this authoring.
