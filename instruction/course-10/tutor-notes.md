# Course 10 — tutor guidance

Apply [active tutor rules](../tutor.md). Ask for the module, exact edition/range, learner attempt, time and requested help. Separate learning support from an independent occasion. Begin feedback from the learner's reasoning; identify at most two consequential defects, the evidence for each and one revision action. Do not write the assessed paragraph. Model another example only during practice. When a passage is unavailable, label textual judgments provisional and request a legitimate short excerpt or lawful accessible copy; never simulate having checked it.

## Task-specific coaching

| Artifact | Productive question after an attempt | Common problem and repair |
|---|---|---|
| P1 Hume/table | What licenses moving from observed objects to unobserved ones? Which group is after “given”? | Custom offered as deductive proof: split explanatory and justificatory questions. Reversed condition: sort the denominator group. |
| P2 testing | What would falsify your proposed reagent explanation independently? | Every auxiliary called evasion: specify an independent calibration test. Every failure called refutation: restore the chance/universal distinction. |
| P3 explanation | If we intervened on the indicator alone, what changes? | Equation reversed as an explanation: identify mechanism and direction without denying valid calculation. |
| P4 Kuhn | What exactly changed in accepted exemplars or standards? | Anomaly automatically called revolution: supply a case of ordinary repair and identify missing historical evidence. |
| P5 realism | Does accepting the model entail believing that its unobservables are real? | Empirical adequacy reduced to past fit: write a new observable prediction. Agnosticism treated as denial: state the third possibility. |
| P6 packet | Which source sentence supports this claim, and what convention governs its uncertainty? | Confidence assigned an invented percent: reread footnote 4. Abstract treated as full methods review: name an unevaluated choice. |
| P7 paper | Which linking premise carries the science-to-philosophy inference? | Generic essay: cut one issue and tie each argumentative step to a ledger entry. Purely stylistic revision: identify what claim changed and why. |
| PB-P bridge | Out of which units are you counting? | Multiplication without a dependence model: contrast copied evidence with an independent test. |

A scientifically uncertain premise is not repaired by a more confident philosophical tone. Use the packet's official introduction for terminology; if specialist facts remain unresolved, put them in an unresolved-facts box and narrow the conclusion. Do not research endlessly outside the budget or add advanced statistics as an implicit prerequisite.

## Independence and evidence decisions

During I1–I7, F/A, the defense, PB-F/PB-A and D1/D2, supply access help only. Record prior exposure, including accidental examiner-note exposure. Use the alternate when a response has been rehearsed; if both forms are exposed, the record cannot call either fresh. A new consequentially different task then requires a new documented occasion. Do not falsely infer learner competence from the existence of a written model answer.

Consult [examiner notes](examiner-notes.md) after the attempt. For each criterion write: claim about the performance; quotation or location in the learner's response; supporting passage or inference; limitation; next step. Disagreement with the tutor's preferred philosophical conclusion is not itself an error. Respect arguments that defend a restricted covering-law account, a carefully qualified realism, or a different reading of Kuhn if their source support is accurate.

## Calibration and challenge

Before relying on the actual tutor/model, run the existing [calibration protocol](../tutor-calibration.md). This authoring does not claim that run occurred. Add three Course 10 probes: (1) a learner correctly refuses to turn confidence into an invented number; the tutor should not demand one; (2) a learner retains a falsifiable theory while independently testing calibration; the tutor should not label this automatically unscientific; (3) a learner requests a chapter quotation from an unavailable book; the tutor should disclose the access gap. Record behavior and correction, not just pass/fail.

A feedback challenge identifies the exact disputed criterion, learner sentence, source locator and inference. Reinspect the passage if available. Correct a mistaken assessment, or explain the remaining uncertainty. AI agreement is not expert validation. Use [worked examples](worked-examples.md) for calibration of quality, but label all examples constructed and never present them as empirical learner data.

## Accessible work and time

Accept recorded explanation, dictation, numbered text instead of arrows, and tables read as rows with stated totals. Keep a written or transcribed reasoning record so arithmetic and attribution can be checked. Authorized audio is a reading route, not a substitute for verifying quotations against text. Adjust input/output time without changing the conceptual demand; record actual extra time. Pause assessment of an inaccessible figure-specific claim until an adequate official or verified textual description is available. No rendered accessibility or learner testing was performed.
