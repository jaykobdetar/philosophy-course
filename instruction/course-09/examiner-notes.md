## Fresh A

**A-K:** The objection adds a positive temporal-gap requirement that Kant's discussion of simultaneous cause and effect explicitly distinguishes from the relevant determination of order. The continuing clamp action and bend can coexist; the release/straightening must still be represented as a rule-governed change rather than merely as whatever the observer happens to notice first. Competent work reconstructs the necessary-rule move, distinguishes ongoing causal activity from a measurable delay, and explains why the stipulated lack of a gap does not alone defeat the account. The general argument does not identify the actual laws, material properties or reliability of a real apparatus. Strong work explains the asymmetry of dependence involved instead of treating coexistence as proof of either causal direction; it may raise a further objection to the required rule. Developing work either declares all simultaneous features noncausal or treats all coexisting features as causes of one another. Repair by separating the sustained state, the represented change and the observer's perceptions; check the passage on simultaneous causation, then resample B-K. No empirical claim about real clamp dynamics is required.

**A-S/H:** §§180–184 describe reciprocal, doubled activity; the other must undertake the corresponding activity, not merely be manipulated as a thing. Institutional proclamation does not establish by itself the relevant relation, but the passage alone does not determine all legal criteria of institutional recognition. Competent work traces one transition and marks that application limit. Developing work paraphrases “recognition is good”; strong work distinguishes a status declaration that may have legal effects from the stronger intersubjective relation. Repair by annotating who acts upon whom; resample B-S/H if outside essay focus and unrehearsed.

**A-S/M:** III challenges a division between those shaped by circumstances and educators treated as exempt; VI places human essence in social relations rather than an isolated abstraction. The council's formation therefore matters to the interpretation. This is not a proof that reform or education is impossible, nor a complete empirical theory of every institution. Strong answers show how the two theses bear different parts of the inference. Check edition/version: Lough's wording cannot be attributed to Livingstone/Benton. Repair the council's omitted premise; resample B-S/M.

**A-G:** Unlike the practice cases, the reactive origin is a hypothesis rather than a stipulated fact. Explanatory fit with public rhetoric does not by itself discriminate sincere reactive value creation from strategic rhetoric under unchanged commitments. Competent work identifies that evidential gap and proposes relevant evidence—dated private deliberations, changes in evaluative commitments, or conduct where the public rhetoric yields no commercial advantage—while acknowledging that no single source is automatically decisive. It separately identifies the bridge needed from an established origin to a criticism of the values; the second step does not become valid merely by repairing the first. Distinguish this empirical historical inquiry from Kant's transcendental claim and Marx's account of productive relations without treating all three as an identical test. Strong work distinguishes a genealogy of the public vocabulary from a genealogy of sincerely held values and avoids inferring the rival account's truth from the first account's underdetermination. Developing work assumes the reactive account was supplied as fact, or answers only with the label “genetic fallacy.” Repair by writing the two historical hypotheses, an observation that would distinguish them and the separate evaluative conclusion; resample B-G.

## Fresh B

**B-K:** Disagreement about a particular cause is compatible with judging an objective event. The probabilistic proposal raises a different challenge: does temporal objectivity require the deterministic/sufficient-condition reading of the rule? Competent work recognizes that this confronts a substantive premise rather than providing automatic disproof. It may argue for a more general rule-governance reading if textually defended, while acknowledging the stronger necessity in Kant's argument. No verdict about real quantum physics is required or licensed. Strong work separates empirical ignorance, statistical law and absence of any law. Repair the distinction among these before further assessment.

**B-S/H:** §196 explicitly complicates an inference from limited formative skill to comprehensive independence. Fear, service and formation figure together; mastery in one domain is not alone the stronger achievement. The excerpt neither guarantees political liberation nor proves educational work useless. Strong answers resist reducing fear to everyday nervousness and acknowledge contested interpretations of its role. A claim that Hegel regards any craft skill as sufficient needs correction against the passage.

**B-S/M:** I includes practical, sensuous human activity in the criticism of contemplative materialism; II connects truth with practice. Neither provides a simple rule equating any immediate political success with truth. Competent work gives the missing bridge and explains why the interpretation cannot erase reflection merely by citing practice. Strong work can defend a controversial practical theory of truth while showing that it is an argued interpretation. Repair by distinguishing “belief true,” “action effective” and “goal justified.”

**B-G:** The case supplies an origin-dependent authority claim plus evidence directly bearing on it. Therefore “origins are always irrelevant” is too strong. The account can defeat that specific account of authority, but it need not show all judgments false or rule out independent present reasons. Strong work specifies the defeater and its scope. Unlike A-G, the relevant historical premise is supplied as established and directly bears on an explicit authority claim; do not reward simply reciting “genetic fallacy.” Repair by distinguishing invalid origin-to-falsehood inference from relevant evidence about an asserted warrant.

## Essay and conversation anchors

Essay competence requires a bounded thesis; a fair reconstruction supported by three primary locators; accurate use of one actually read specialist commentary; a plausible competing reading; a direct objection/reply; and a substantive revision memo. Strong work explains why the competing reading attracts a reader and what evidence adjudicates it. Developing work may be fluent while replacing argument with a historical timeline. Fabrication suspends the source-integrity judgment until repaired. Do not require the interpretation favored by the chosen SEP entry.

During the 20-minute explanation, assess whether the learner identifies the load-bearing premise and responds to a real clarification. Allow pauses, typing and a transcript. Do not mark accent, confidence or reading speed. Record the exact follow-up and enough of the answer to support the judgment.

## Feedback record and remedies

Use: **item / criterion / quotation or location in learner work / inspected source or uncertainty / diagnosis / one repair / fresh form needed / result**. Give one or two consequential problems, not an exhaustive stylistic edit. For textual ambiguity, compare two readings and label unresolved scope. For missing evidence, name the missing edition/passage instead of guessing. For a learner challenge, re-open the source and withdraw unsupported feedback. Existing budget supports targeted repair; a continuing gap requires an explicit future plan.
