# C09 Delayed A — learner form

Administer **roughly four to six weeks after the fresh session or after two later modules**. Select **one** least-secure row. Source-open under the same independence rules; 25 minutes response, five minutes checking, charged to M08. About 300–400 words; record actual date and exposure.

**DA-K:** A witness remembers hearing a crash before seeing an intact window, but a timestamped photograph suggests the window was already broken. The witness argues that changing her memory order changes the event itself. Use the Second Analogy to distinguish the claims. Explain the role of an objective rule, what remains empirically uncertain, and one limitation of using a timestamp as evidence. Assigned range: A189/B232–A211/B256, Guyer/Wood 1998.

**DA-S:** Interpret Marx, **Theses on Feuerbach VIII and XI**, Livingstone/Benton 1975. A reader says that because social life is practical, interpreting social practices has no legitimate role. Explain the relation between the two theses and assess the inference. Distinguish a textual claim from a proposal for political action. This item is for the S row; if it was central to the essay or previously coached, use DB-S instead.

**DA-G:** A fictional rule began as an expression of generosity, but current adherents use it to humiliate outsiders. Its defender argues that the admirable origin guarantees its present value. Evaluate this genealogical inference using Nietzsche First Essay §§10–14, Diethe 2007, and distinguish an origin-based defense from a present justification. Say how a social explanation and a transcendental argument would ask different questions.
