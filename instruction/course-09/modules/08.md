# C09-M08 — Assessment and delayed check

**8 hours. Guiding question:** Can you transfer a reading method to a fresh passage and case after support has faded?

**Prerequisites:** M01–07 artifacts and essay. **Outcomes:** independently meet each essential row; explain a response to an objection; diagnose and repair a specific weakness; retain the least secure method after a meaningful delay.

## Reading and preparation

No new primary work is added. Have the exact assigned editions and ranges from M01–06 available, plus the source ledger. Read [assessment procedure](../assessment.md) and [outcome map](../outcomes.md), but do not rehearse the selected form or open examiner guidance. “Fresh” refers to an uncoached passage question/case; reading the core texts previously is expected. Record any question or examiner-answer exposure.

## Teaching: what an independent sample establishes

An essay shows what sustained attention and revision can accomplish on a narrow topic. The independent sample asks whether the method transfers when a premise or interpretive problem changes. These are complementary kinds of evidence. Neither speed nor a confident voice supplies a missing argument. A learner who accepts Kant and one who rejects his pivotal premise can both be competent if each reconstructs the argument accurately and gives relevant reasons.

The three rows are: Kantian conditions and causation; recognition and social explanation; genealogy and method. A polished genealogy answer cannot cancel failure to distinguish apprehension from objective succession. Equally, a minor prose problem need not erase an otherwise traceable argument. Use the observable rubric: accurate interpretation, explicit inference, relevant objection, source integrity and substantive revision. Record each row as demonstrated, needs repair or unresolved through access/exposure, with a sentence of evidence.

**Worked evidence example, not an assessed answer:** An essay offers three accurate Hegel locators and a revised account of dependence. This supports a module artifact for recognition. It does not prove independent Kantian competence. The evidence grid therefore retains the Kant fresh task and uses a Marx passage for the post-Kantian fresh task, outside the essay's focus. A missing row is recorded explicitly, not averaged into a total.

## Exact budget

| Activity | Minutes |
| --- | ---: |

| Source-open fresh written sample: K 35, S 30, G 25 | 90 |

| Feedback diagnosis | 60 |
| Targeted repair practice | 90 |

| Revision memo and next-course handoff | 40 |
| **Later delayed sample plus check, reserved now** | **30** |
| **Total** | **480 = 8h** |

The reserve permits one or two affected rows in the alternate form. If all three need a full 90-minute resample, use 30 minutes of the repair practice allocation for it and record the change; do not add hidden hours. Persistent unmet criteria after the budget are a recorded dependency requiring an explicit revised study plan, not an automatic pass or an unbounded retest loop.

## Independent task I08 and course forms

Use [Fresh A](../assessment-forms/fresh-a.md), or [Fresh B](../assessment-forms/fresh-b.md) if A is exposed. Write the three responses in **35/30/25 minutes**. Record all assistance. The post-Kantian passage must be outside the essay focus; if the essay is post-Kantian, the Kant sample is the converse independent interpretive evidence. A source-open oral submission may replace written responses with equivalent saved explanations, with neutral transcription and a locator sheet; allow pauses and appropriate access adjustment while retaining the same reasoning requirements.

Use the explanation prompt: “Which premise in one response does the most work, and what would change if it were false?” The assessor then asks one clarification genuinely responsive to the answer, such as “Does that change the conclusion or only its scope?” No mid-attempt feedback or scoring. A written exchange over two turns serves the same purpose; fluency is not a criterion.

## Repair, delayed retrieval and next step

Choose the least secure row from the evidence grid. Complete **one** corresponding item from [Delayed A](../assessment-forms/delayed-a.md), or [Delayed B](../assessment-forms/delayed-b.md) if exposed, **roughly four to six weeks later or after two later modules**. Reserve 25 minutes for the source-open attempt and five for citation/exposure checking, charged here. Set the date/trigger in the learner record; do not claim the delayed check has occurred merely because it is scheduled. Short M01–07 retrieval is additional practice inside those modules and cannot substitute.

Record the outcome and exact repair action. If delayed transfer fails, reopen the affected row and agree a finite further plan before reporting completion. Proceed to Course 10 or the learner's selected continuation with the unresolved claims and scheduled delayed trigger visible.
