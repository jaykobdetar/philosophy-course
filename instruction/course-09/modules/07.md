# C09-M07 — Essay and revision

**24 hours. Guiding question:** What can one carefully delimited passage establish, and what would a serious rival reading change?

## Teaching and worked planning example

A historical essay has two linked burdens. It must explain what an author is doing in a passage, and give reasons for accepting or challenging a specified move. A contemporary counterexample can test an argument reconstructed from the passage; it cannot replace evidence that the author made that argument. A commentator's authority is likewise not a premise that settles the dispute. Identify the commentator's inference and test it against the primary source.

**Constructed planning example on a different focus:** “Kant calls a judgment a priori, therefore it was psychologically present at birth” is a poor thesis because the proposed inference confuses warrant and origin. A productive question is whether the argument needs a claim about how we acquire the relevant representation. An outline would first distinguish the two senses, then locate the text's explanatory demand, then ask whether the proposed explanation presupposes an acquisition claim. This transforms a sweeping historical allegation into an answerable interpretive issue. Do not use this example as the essay itself.

Use a claim ledger: **claim / exact source and edition / paraphrase or quotation / inferential role / rival reading / confidence and unresolved check**. A quotation must be transcribed from the actual copy, including any ellipsis. Record canonical locators alongside edition page numbers. For Marx, where paragraph numbering varies, record section, edition page and a short opening phrase in the private dossier. Keep copyrighted extracts out of distributed course files.

## Budget and production stages

Primary/commentary reading 5h; question/dossier/outline 3h; draft 6h; feedback 2h; revision 5h; independent transfer 1.5h; retrieval and submission checks 1.5h = **24h**.

**P07:** Submit an outline, 1,800–2,200-word draft, checked bibliography, final essay and **300-word revision memo**. Suggested paragraph budget: question and thesis 180–220 words; textual reconstruction 500–600; disputed inference/rival interpretation 350–450; objection and response 500–650; qualified conclusion 150–200. This is guidance, not a mechanical marking scheme. Include at least three primary locators, one accurately represented commentary claim, one serious rival reading and one unresolved limit.

Feedback procedure: ask the reviewer only two questions—“Which inference is least supported?” and “Where have I misrepresented the passage or rival?” Sort feedback into textual correction, inferential challenge and optional expression advice. Verify textual corrections first. Revise the claim or argument when warranted, then explain what changed and why. Do not silently let a tutor replace your prose.

## Independent task I07

Source-open, 75 minutes plus 15 minutes source checking; no access to your prepared reply paragraph. Use the route-specific objection: **K:** the critic grants objective ordering but denies it implies a necessary causal connection. **H:** the critic grants dependence on another but says dependence can constitute autonomy rather than defeat it. **M:** workers endorse the arrangement after an informed, noncoerced choice, but the supposed capacities remain unrealized. **N:** descendants retain the value while rejecting the originator's evaluative standard and offering independent reasons. Write 400–500 words locating the affected premise, revising or defending the thesis and identifying further evidence required. These change a premise; a stock repetition of the essay's conclusion is insufficient.

## Repair, retrieval and next step

If the essay surveys four authors, cut to the selected passage and one question. If the commentary replaces evidence, rebuild one paragraph with a primary locator and explicit inference. If revision is cosmetic, change one premise, interpretation or scope claim and explain its consequence. A week later recover the thesis and best rival in 15 minutes, within submission checks.
