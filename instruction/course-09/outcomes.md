# Course 9 outcome and evidence map

All rows in the [shared Course 9 grid](../../current/assessment.md) require a satisfactory practice artifact **and** an independently competent fresh response. Neither the paper nor a composite score erases a missing row. The three fresh allocations total 90 minutes.

| Essential row | Taught and practiced evidence | Independent evidence | Required observable performance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kantian conditions and causation | M01 P01 concept sheet; M02 P02 argument/objection; M03 P03 paired timeline and P03-CM modal comparison; M04 P04 limits memo | I01–I04 support; final **A-K or B-K, 35 min** | Explain subjective/objective succession, reconstruct the necessary-rule move, distinguish particular causal knowledge, state a relevant scope limit. |
| Recognition and social explanation | M05 P05 map/700-word interpretation; M06 P06-M 500-word reconstruction | I05/I06 support; final **A-S or B-S, 30 min**, outside essay focus | Interpret a specified Hegel/Marx passage, explain the relevant dependence/social premise and distinguish textual argument from application. |
| Genealogy and method | M06 P06-N 500-word reconstruction and 250-word comparison | I06 support; final **A-G or B-G, 25 min** | Evaluate a new genealogy-to-verdict inference; identify any needed evaluative/epistemic bridge; distinguish it from social explanation and transcendental argument. |

**Form mapping after freshness review:** A-K tests simultaneous causal activity and the temporal-gap objection; B-K tests the probabilistic/sufficient-condition challenge. A-G tests evidence for rival genealogical explanations as well as the evaluative inference; B-G tests history bearing on an explicit origin-based authority claim. DA-G tests an origin-based defense of present value; DB-G tests a genealogical principle's reflexive application and scope. These retain the same essential rows, reading ranges and time allocations. Explanatory and diagnostic anchors remain in the separate examiner file.

Curriculum outcomes not separately named as final grid rows retain practice evidence: analytic/synthetic and a priori/a posteriori (P01/I01); intuition and transcendental conditions (P02/I02); limits (P04/I04); sustained interpretation, competing reading and revision (P07/I07). Final K samples conditions and limits through a causation problem. The survey does not claim independent twice-tested mastery of every sentence.

**Essay routing:** A Kant essay requires a post-Kantian S passage; a Hegel/Marx/Nietzsche essay requires a Kant K response outside the essay's focus and an S passage outside its main focus. A Hegel essay uses Marx S; a Marx essay uses Hegel S; a Nietzsche essay may use either. All three rows are sampled regardless of essay choice.

**Evidence record template:** learner/date; row; practice artifact/path; practice feedback; selected fresh form/item; primary edition/locator; prior exposure; assistance/access adjustments; judgment and cited evidence; repair; alternate form/result; delayed item/date or two-module trigger; delayed result; unresolved dependency. Save actual work in the learner's private workspace, not the public course package.

**Progression rule:** unresolved source access, fabricated attribution or task-specific assistance prevents an independent judgment for the affected item. Repair the problem, use a fresh form when needed, and retain the earlier record. Criteria assess philosophical responsiveness rather than speed or confidence.
