## Preparation and exposure rules

## Criteria and result

K: reconstruct the passage's reasoning, use the case details, distinguish general/particular claims and identify a limit. S: interpret the selected passage, support a transition, distinguish text and application. G: identify the target conclusion, explain the inference from origin, and compare methods accurately. Across all: traceable sources, fair rivals, relevant objections and explicit reasoning.

Developing work has a consequential gap or confusion. Competent work is accurately attributed and explains a justified, bounded answer to the actual question. Strong work also tests the pivotal assumption or a serious rival and states the remaining limit. Agreement with a favored philosophical conclusion is not required. A missing source or fabricated citation is a source-integrity problem requiring correction, not simply a lower score.

The assessor records evidence row by row and provides a precise repair instruction. Use the repair/resample reserve in M08; do not silently add work. The essay, independent evidence, explanation, revision record and delayed check jointly support completion. Authored forms are not evidence that a learner has passed them.
