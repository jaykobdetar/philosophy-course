# Course 3 — Fresh and delayed assessment forms

**Learner-facing prompts; answers are in [separate examiner notes](examiner-notes.md).** The assessor chooses an unexposed form before the attempt. Do not inspect unused forms or examiner guidance. These texts are original prompts and invented cases, not copied exercises. All tasks are within Module 8's eight hours.

## Conditions and neutral criteria

Declare actual editions, source access, essay passages, assistance and prior prompt/answer exposure. Use clean assigned primary texts for the three fresh tasks; no notes, module teaching, commentary, tutor/AI content assistance or feedback during the attempt. Screen readers, dictation and verbatim transcription are allowed. A permitted helper may locate a named section without interpreting it. Each slot takes **30 minutes**, producing 200–300 words or an equivalent spoken response with a checked transcript. The three slots are required even when the paper supplies depth in one group. Choose a Plato passage not discussed substantively in the paper. Reading the whole assigned corpus previously is expected; rehearsing this exact prompt is not.

Apply the same criteria to both forms: faithful attribution and context; explicit premises and inference; a distinction applied to the new case; relevant comparison or objection; traceable evidence in the edition actually read; a conclusion no stronger than the reasons. No preferred philosophical verdict is required. Do not award full source-dependent evidence without the text. Final Aristotle quotation/Bekker verification needs Barnes; final Sextus textual verification needs Annas/Barnes. Epicurus can fill the final Hellenistic slot while the required Sextus reading remains separately pending.

## Fresh Form A — 90 minutes

**F-A-P — Plato, 30 minutes.** With *Republic* IV **434d–435b** visible, identify the methodological relation proposed between examining city and individual. Explain why the passage's comparison is not yet an inventory of the soul's parts. An invented reader concludes: “A city contains rulers and workers, so each person literally contains little rulers and workers.” Reconstruct the inference that would be needed for a defensible comparison and connect it to the question of justice. Identify speaker and context. Exclude this slot if these lines are central to the essay; use B or C.

**F-A-A — Aristotle, 30 minutes.** Invented case with stipulated facts: a fig cutting takes root and produces leaves in the characteristic sequence of its kind; a craftsperson carves a discarded branch into a leaf-shaped ornament. A gardener supplies water to the cutting. Explain how *Physics* II.1–3 distinguishes the sources and objects of change. Give three different explanatory questions about the growing cutting and state whether a claim about its end requires more than identifying the gardener. Use *Categories* 1–5 to distinguish this individual cutting from one of its changing qualities. No outside botany is needed; mark any extra empirical assumption.

**F-A-H — Epicurus and Socrates, 30 minutes.** Use the complete Yonge *Letter to Menoeceus*, especially the death passage, and *Apology* **40c–41c**. Invented case: Ianthe fears the pain of dying, the absence of future festivals after death and the distress her death would cause friends. Reconstruct Epicurus' argument and identify which of these concerns it directly addresses and which requires another premise. Compare the logical structure of his reassurance with Socrates' alternatives in *Apology*. Do not treat a common practical aim as proof of a shared account of death. Cite Yonge's actual Book X/XXVII letter cue rather than invented paragraph numbering.

## Fresh Form B — 90 minutes, also available for rechecks by slot

**F-B-P — Plato, 30 minutes.** With *Republic* VII **519c–520e** visible, reconstruct the dispute about returning educated philosophers to political work. Explain the relation between the good of the whole city and the good of its members. An invented reader says: “Because contemplation is better, political duty cannot be just.” Identify the premise needed for that conclusion, one response in the passage and what remains to be shown about individual benefit. State who raises the challenge. Exclude if central to the essay.

**F-B-A — Aristotle, 30 minutes.** Invented stipulated facts: a fledgling grows feathers in a regular developmental sequence; a sculptor attaches real shed feathers to a clay bird. Both can become wet; only the fledgling feeds and grows. Using *Physics* II.1–3, distinguish the natural object's form, material, source of development and a proposed end. Explain why listing clay and feathers cannot answer every question about what either object is. Use *Categories* 1–5 to explain one change that need not destroy the individual's identity. State the extra premise a defender of natural ends would need when using regularity from II.8; do not import a modern biological theory unannounced.

**F-B-H — Sextus and Epicurus, 30 minutes.** Use **Annas/Barnes, Outlines I.19–30**, and the Yonge letter. Invented case: two equally persuasive accounts of whether an unfamiliar custom is good by nature leave Phaon undecided. He still follows the local custom and feels hunger. A critic says that action and hunger refute his suspension, and that tranquility proves the custom objectively good. Explain the skeptical distinctions relevant to those claims and compare that route to freedom from disturbance with Epicurus' reasoned choice of pleasures. Give exact references in the assigned edition. **Do not administer as completed textual assessment while Annas/Barnes is unavailable.** Use F-A-H if unexposed and source-ready; keep Sextus reading unresolved.

**F-C-P — Reserved Plato replacement, 30 minutes.** If A and B are both central to the essay, use *Republic* IX **577c–578b**. An invented reader equates satisfying one dominant appetite with the whole person's freedom. Explain the passage's alternative account and the function of its city comparison. State one inference needed to move from this case to a general defense of justice. If this passage too is central to the paper or the task has been rehearsed, the assessor must prepare and log a different source-bound task within the assigned ranges before administering it. No source-free generic question can fill this slot.

## O-A / O-B — Explanation and new essay objection, 20 minutes

Use no notes for the first five minutes: explain a comparison not central to your paper, naming each author's argument and one difference. The assessor then supplies the appropriate unused objection below. Spend ten minutes answering, with the primary text available if needed, followed by five minutes acknowledging a limit or revising the claim. Written equivalent: five-minute independent comparison paragraph, ten-minute objection response, five-minute correction. No real-time coaching.

| Essay | O-A objection | O-B alternate objection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Republic IV | “Your defense values the whole soul. What justifies assigning reason authority to determine the whole's good?” | “If ordinary just behavior is only a sign of inner order, could an exceptional violation preserve the order you defend?” |
| Affinity | “The soul changes when it learns. How does that affect the likeness to the unchanging on which your account relies?” | “Why should knowing stable objects make the knower more stable, rather than merely give it a stable object?” |
| Aristotle's soul | “If soul is a standing capacity, what distinguishes a sleeping organism from an object that could be made into an organism?” | “Which claim in your interpretation survives the excerpt's qualifications about separable capacities?” |

## Delayed forms — choose one group, 30 minutes

Schedule **roughly four to six weeks or two later modules after the fresh session**, ordinarily during Course 4, charged to Course 3. Choose the least secure essential group in the grid, using an unexposed task. First twenty minutes: closed sources/notes, no help, 180–250 words. Last ten: inspect the relevant assigned text and append corrections with checked references; do not overwrite the initial attempt. The primary task is reconstruction and transfer, not recall of exact printed line numbers.

## Decision after marking

Record feedback by criterion, evidence, source support/uncertainty and one repair. Use the unused alternate slot for a recheck after repair; previously exposed answers cannot establish fresh transfer. Essential misattribution, missing inference or fabricated citation requires repair. Competent work in all three groups, a reasoned revised paper, a responsive explanation and an adequate delayed sample support readiness. Missing required reading or exact-source verification is reported explicitly, even if all available conceptual evidence is competent. No certificate, pilot validation or numerical pass mark is implied.
