## Final fresh task anchors

### F-A-P / F-B-P / F-C-P

**F-A-P:** The city offers a larger comparison to test against the individual; the shift does not by itself demonstrate psychological parts. Competent work notices the proposed comparison is conditional on finding a relevant likeness and points toward the need for a separate account of motivations. Little people are a misleading literalization. Strong work identifies the respect in which justice would be shared and explains why likeness under a name needs evidence, not an assumed transfer of every property. Check 434d–435b in the edition used. Do not demand an exhaustive partition argument from a short prompt.

**F-B-P:** The passage includes an objection that compelling philosophers to return gives them a worse life, and a response centered on the whole city and the education it supplied. Competent work distinguishes the good of the whole from each member's preferred activity and identifies a premise such as “a just demand may never reduce the recipient's preferred good.” Strong work explains why reciprocal educational obligation or whole-city benefit might defeat that premise yet leave a question about individual benefit. Do not infer that the city’s good automatically equals each citizen’s happiness or attribute Glaucon's challenge to an unnamed unanimous voice.

**F-C-P:** The dominance of a tyrannical appetite can make the soul as a whole unable to achieve what it wants; one part's success is not identical to freedom of the whole. Competent work explains the city analogy and its limited inferential role. Strong work identifies the missing generalization from a tyrannical case to injustice more generally. Keep evaluation within 577c–578b and the assigned context; no demand for later pleasure arguments.

### F-A-A / F-B-A

**F-A-A:** Rooted growth belongs to the cutting as a natural thing; the gardener's water is an external condition or contribution and does not erase the internal principle. Three different questions might concern material, organized kind/form and development's source; an end question concerns what a capacity/development is for. The learner should specify what is being explained. The carving's leaf-like shape does not give it the cutting's life. An individual cutting's increasing height or changing color illustrates persisting substance with changing attribute. Strong work recognizes that the biological facts are stipulated and evaluates final explanation without treating intention by a gardener as its only possible basis.

**F-B-A:** Feather material can occur in both the living bird and artifact while the relevant form and capacity differ. The sculptor explains an artifact's production; feeding and regular development point to the natural being as the subject of investigation. A quality such as wetness or a quantity such as size can change while the individual remains. II.8's regularity reasoning requires a bridge from noncoincidental development to an end, or a reason the available alternatives are exhaustive. Strong work distinguishes a living form from outline or external resemblance and identifies the bridge without pretending to have empirically refuted Aristotle.

Developing work merely lists four cause names, calls the gardener/sculptor the material, or treats every predicate as an accidental quality. A competent response uses at least three nonredundant explanatory questions and one substance/attribute distinction. A strong response makes clear when two descriptions concern different explananda. Inspect assigned-edition terminology and any Bekker number before affirming citation accuracy.

### F-A-H / F-B-H

**F-A-H:** Competent reconstruction includes the sensation-related good/evil premise and death's lack of sensation; it explains why Epicurus denies death's harm to its nonexistent subject. Pain during dying is experienced while alive; others' grief concerns surviving subjects; deprivation of festivals raises whether harm requires experience. These concerns cannot all be dismissed by repeating “death is nothing.” *Apology* uses alternative pictures, including absence of perception and relocation, in a defense framed as grounds for hope. Strong work distinguishes the practical aim from confidence in an account and evaluates the deprivation objection without asserting its answer is already settled. Yonge lacks the supplied modern paragraph mapping; accept honest Book X/XXVII letter cues and check the actual words.

**F-B-H:** Subject to checking Annas/Barnes I.19–30, competent work distinguishes appearance/feeling from a judgment about nature; customary action and learned practices can guide ordinary activity; hunger can persist despite suspension. Relief is not a proof of the custom's goodness. Comparison with Epicurus should locate a difference between substantive ethical reasoning about pleasure and the route through unresolved opposition/suspension. Strong work notes limits to tranquility in unavoidable affections and avoids “every argument has actually been defeated” or “nothing can ever be true.” Patrick supports authorial preparation only; exact assigned-edition interpretation is pending without access.

## Oral/written explanation and essay

The five-minute comparison must identify two arguments and a real difference, not just names or shared concerns. For the selected objection, competent work identifies its target, responds with reasons and admits a limit or narrows the claim where appropriate. Do not assess speed, accent or spontaneous eloquence. The twenty-minute written alternative has identical criteria.

**O-A justice:** Look for an account of reason's authority tied to the whole's good, rather than the fact of its dominance. **O-B justice:** Look for an explicit view of the relation between character, ordinary acts and exceptional cases; accept a defended qualification. **O-A affinity:** Distinguish the soul's changing condition in embodied life from its proposed kinship with stable objects, and explain why the difference helps or hurts the inference. **O-B affinity:** Require a reason for moving from cognitive object to cognitive subject. **O-A soul:** Distinguish a living body's standing capacity from raw material's merely remote possibility; check the assigned definition. **O-B soul:** Require acknowledgement of the qualifications about separability and an appropriately limited claim of explanatory unity.

For M07's essay assess: specific answer to the chosen question; fair reconstruction of at least two central passages; one substantial objection and direct reply; honest source/assistance record; organization that makes the disputed inference visible; and substantive revision with a memo. A sweeping comparison with an unassigned modern author cannot replace close interpretation. The three offered thesis directions are not answer keys. At strong level, the learner explains why a plausible rival reading is less supported, changes the defended claim when warranted and states a remaining difficulty. A polished but misattributed or uncited central claim requires repair. See [tutor notes](tutor-notes.md) for original developing/competent/strong samples; do not use verbal similarity to those samples as a mark.
