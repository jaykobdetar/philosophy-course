# C03-M08 — Independent assessment and later recall

**8 hours, including a delayed half-hour charged to Course 3.** Guiding question: Can you use the studied distinctions on a new passage, example and objection without coaching? Prerequisites: module artifacts and revised paper. Outcomes: demonstrate all three essential groups independently, respond to a fresh objection and show retention after a delay.

## Sources and time

No new book is assigned. Use the exact editions and permitted alternatives in [readings](../readings.md), selecting only the stated ranges. For source-open work supply clean primary texts, not annotated notes, module explanations or examiner answers. Record unavailable texts. Annas/Barnes-dependent criteria cannot be completed from Patrick; Aristotle edition/locator judgments remain pending if only older local texts are available.

The delay is calendar time. The thirty-minute attempt occurs during the next course if necessary but is not charged there. Earlier modules' scheduled retrieval exercises are distinct from this final delayed sample and stay within their original budgets. If repair exceeds its allowance, record actual additional time and an unresolved outcome instead of silently changing the 120-hour baseline.

## How to prepare without rehearsing the assessment

Create an evidence grid using [outcomes](../outcomes.md). For each essential group identify a module artifact, its exact passage, help received and one difficulty. Add the essay's covered passages. The assessor selects the fresh form before opening your answer and keeps the [examiner notes](../examiner-notes.md) separate. The text has been read previously; “fresh” means the particular interpretation task/case has not been coached or rehearsed. A passage central to your essay is excluded from the final Plato slot. Prior exposure to a form requires the alternate form; both exposed means a replacement must be recorded before administering it.

The [assessment packet](../assessment.md) contains exact A/B prompts and delayed alternatives. Do not read unused forms in advance. Each thirty-minute task produces 200–300 words or an equivalent structured spoken answer with a checked transcript. Spend approximately five minutes locating/reading, twenty reasoning and five checking attribution. Quantity is a guide, not a fluency test.

## Teaching: what counts as evidence

A polished essay may show depth in one dispute while leaving another group untested. That is why a total impression cannot replace the grid. An accurate label is also weaker evidence than a correct explanation: saying “final cause” without stating what is explained and how does not establish competence. Conversely, an honest provisional citation can accompany a strong conceptual analysis; record the source criterion as pending rather than converting an edition gap into a philosophical error.

**Original worked portfolio example:** A learner's paper accurately analyzes the cave, while a fresh Aristotle response calls clay the purpose of a pot. The paper does not repair that specific explanatory confusion. Feedback should target material versus final explanation, followed by a different object on the recheck. It should not ask the learner to rewrite the Plato paper. This is an invented diagnostic scenario, not a report of a completed learner pilot.

Before submission, practice organizing the portfolio only: list file, task ID, edition, assistance, first attempt/revision and criterion addressed. Do not answer a fresh prompt during this organization. For the independent session, assistance is limited to access/format logistics, exact allowed source-location help and verbatim transcription. No task-specific hints, generated answers, feedback or scores during the attempt. Declare any accidental exposure immediately; retain the attempt as practice and use an unused form later.

After feedback, repair the exact error in the assigned passage, compare two interpretations if needed and explain the change in 150 words. Then complete a different recheck from the assessment packet. Readiness requires competent evidence in each group, an accountable revised paper and response, and the delayed sample; pending access/attribution prevents a claim of full completion. Do not average fabrication or a missing group away.

At roughly four to six weeks or two later modules, do the designated delayed task without notes or sources for twenty minutes, then use ten minutes for a clearly separated source check. Keep the initial response intact. A weak delayed result returns only the affected group to repair. Finish with a one-page handoff to Course 4: strongest argument reconstructed, one technical distinction retained, one source/interpretation gap and a dated next step. Continue available learning while exact access gaps are resolved, with the course status stated honestly.
