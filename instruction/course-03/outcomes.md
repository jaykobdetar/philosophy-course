# Course 3 outcome and evidence map

The [current assessment blueprint](../../current/assessment.md) controls the three essential groups. Each needs both module evidence and a genuinely independent fresh sample. The essay supplies depth; it does not replace a missing group. A form ID means an administered attempt, not that viewing the prompt establishes competence.

| Essential group | Measurable outcome and coached practice | Independent evidence | Readiness test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platonic argument and dialogue | M01 chronology/400-word attribution; M02 separate challenge maps/600-word reconstruction; M03 partition and benefit ledger; M04 line/cave chart and 700-word evaluation | M01-I through M04-I; final **F-A-P**, **F-B-P** or **F-C-P**, on a passage not used in the essay | Distinguish speaker/function; reconstruct an actual inference; identify its necessary premise and scope using checked textual evidence |
| Aristotelian explanation | M05 two-object table, substance/attribute comparison and teleology argument; M06 first-actuality explanation | M05-I; relevant M06 artifact; final **F-A-A** or **F-B-A** | Distinguish explanatory questions, form/matter and natural source; connect categories to the given case; avoid claiming local files verify Barnes wording/Bekker numbers |
| Hellenistic argument and historical comparison | M06 whole-letter notes, death reconstruction, Apology comparison, suspension/practical-guidance explanation | M06-I; final **F-A-H** or **F-B-H** | State premises and conclusion of Epicurus or a specified skeptical move; compare with a studied rival without conflating aim and argument; source-specific gaps remain visible |

Use **not attempted**, **developing**, **competent**, **strong**, or **pending source verification**, with a sentence explaining the evidence. Do not collapse an unavailable required reading into a score. “Competent conceptual work; Barnes citation pending” is a possible mixed record. It does not mean the assigned reading has been completed. The common [rubric](../../current/assessment.md) and [examiner anchors](examiner-notes.md) govern judgments; correct source attribution and honest assistance disclosure are essential.

The baseline remains 120 hours. The eight module allocations include these outputs and checks; no independent-evidence row adds another course. A delayed task undertaken during Course 4 uses Course 3's reserve only. No PC-25–33 addition belongs to this track, and no shared language/Sartre packet is billed here.
