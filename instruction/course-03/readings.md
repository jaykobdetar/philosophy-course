## Locate before quoting

Write work, translator, edition/site, stable section, and access date for a web copy. Open that location and verify the words and speaker. For Aristotle, add a Bekker number only after inspecting a numbered edition: no automatic correspondence between an unnumbered TXT paragraph and a Bekker line is supplied here. A book/chapter plus short cue is an honest provisional reference. A final citation requirement may remain pending even when the interpretation is sound.

For listening, make a six-column note: source; translator; assigned start; assigned stop; audio stopping point; checked text locator. Use a human or screen-reader transcription of your own response if useful. Check names, negatives and technical words against the text before submission. An abridged audiobook cannot establish completion of the specified range. No source is newly redistributed or relicensed by this register.
