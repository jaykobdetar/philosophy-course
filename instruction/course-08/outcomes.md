# C08 outcome and evidence map

The three required rows below reproduce the scope of the [Course 8 assessment grid](../../current/assessment.md). Each needs a satisfactory supported artifact **and** competent independent fresh work; the narrow essay supplies depth but does not erase a row. Timing is 30+30+30 minutes plus one 20-minute explanation. Political agreement, rhetorical fluency and arithmetic alone are insufficient.

| Essential row | Measurable performance | Named practice evidence | Independent final sample | Repair and delayed option |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Authority and liberty | Distinguish security/consent, permission/restriction and empirical/normative premises; defend an inference | P1 five-step/500-word reconstruction, P2 map/comparison, P3 two-case analysis; F1–F3 formative independent tasks | A1 or B1, 30 min | Correct benefit→authority or falsity→suppression leap; D1 or E1 |
| Distribution | Explain an original-position/principle argument and a historical-entitlement challenge under changed conditions | P5 numerical example, Z variant, five-step Chamberlain reconstruction, revision; F5 | A2 or B2, 30 min | Add prior conditions, provenance and rectification; D2 or E2 |
| Gender race and idealization | Interpret an assigned passage, explain a structural mechanism and what an idealization omits; identify an empirical premise | P4 reconstruction/form annotation, P6 textually supported rival reading and claim map; F4/F6; H-P supplements | A3 or B3, 30 min | Rebuild passage/inference/context; D3 or E3 |

Additional outcomes: P7 draft, essay, revision memo and F7 assess sustained argument and response. H-P/H-F (or H-A) assess PC-32's three projects and category test within its additional eight hours. Module 8's P8 is rehearsal, not independent evidence. All forms are in [assessment.md](assessment.md); answers and diagnostics are in [examiner-notes.md](examiner-notes.md).

## Working record template

Copy these fields into a private learner record for each required row:

`Row | practice ID/date/path | exact source/edition/locator checked | assistance | fresh form/date/minutes | prior exposure | source availability | interpretation/reasoning evidence | developing/competent/strong | repair/alternate | final delayed form/date/minutes/result | assessor limitations | next step`

Completion also needs essay revision and integrity audit. Assign the delayed form to the least secure row using actual evidence, after roughly 4–6 weeks or two later modules; bill the single 30-minute sample to C08-M08. If a row has no checked primary source, keep its source-bound interpretation judgment provisional even if the case reasoning is good. These are coverage and readiness records, not a credential or empirical validation.
