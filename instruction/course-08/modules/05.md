# C08-M05 — Fairness and entitlement

**20 hours.** Question: does justice depend on the pattern of holdings, the history producing them, or the institutions governing both? Prerequisites: consent/property and equality work. Outcomes: reconstruct a choice situation and an entitlement argument; distinguish principles from applications; explain a numerical comparison without making arithmetic decide justice.

## Explanation

Rawls's original position is a device for selecting principles under constraints intended to model fairness. It is not a historical meeting, a survey of preferences, or an instruction to forget all general social knowledge. The veil of ignorance excludes information that would allow parties to tailor principles to their own social position. Reconstruct what is known, what is excluded, what the parties seek, and why those conditions are supposed to justify the selected principles. Agreement in a stipulated choice situation needs a further account of why the situation is appropriate.

The basic structure is the primary subject: institutions distribute rights, opportunities and advantages over time. Do not apply a table of household cash as if it exhausted basic liberties, fair equality of opportunity or the social bases of self-respect. The difference principle concerns the prospects of the least advantaged within the relevant institutional comparison. “Maximize the smallest number” can model one restricted choice only after metric, feasibility and prior constraints are stated. It is not a universal decision algorithm extracted from the full theory.

Nozick's entitlement approach asks about just acquisition, transfer and rectification. A current unequal pattern is not by itself proof of injustice; a pleasing pattern is not by itself proof of just history. The Wilt Chamberlain argument asks what happens when people entitled to their holdings voluntarily transfer small amounts to a popular performer. Preserving a fixed pattern may require interference with transfers. The challenge is serious only if the starting holdings and transfers meet the relevant conditions. “Voluntary” cannot simply be stipulated for every actual market, and historical injustice does not disappear because today's exchanges look consensual.

The theories can therefore disagree before arithmetic begins: which facts matter, what counts as a just starting point, what institutional obligations constrain transfer, and what idealization is legitimate? A strong comparison locates that disagreement rather than deciding that one author likes equality and the other likes freedom.

## Worked practice: invented institutional options

Three representative groups have lifetime resource indices of (20,20,20) under X and (25,35,70) under Y. Assume both options are feasible, liberties and opportunity conditions are equal, and these indices appropriately represent the least advantaged group's prospects. Under those deliberately narrow assumptions, Y's minimum exceeds X's. That does not establish Y's justice if Y also denies a basic liberty, or if its apparent gains depend on unrectified confiscation. A historical entitlement analysis needs the provenance of holdings and transfers, which the table omits.

**P5:** Compare X/Y above, then construct Z with greater total resources and a lower minimum than Y. In 600–800 words state the Rawlsian selection argument, the information an entitlement analysis still needs, and one serious objection to each. Separately reconstruct Wilt Chamberlain's move in five steps. Label all numerical cases invented; do not present them as empirical welfare estimates. Criteria: no inference from greater total to Rawlsian justice; no inference from unequal shares to Nozickian injustice; explicit conditions and missing history.

## Independent F5

An inventor receives 1 credit voluntarily from each of 100 citizens; initial holdings of 10 each were justly acquired. A new institution would raise the future lifetime index of the least advantaged from 10 to 14 while funding public access through a levy on future license revenue. The actual licensing system also excludes competitors by a legal privilege whose origin is unspecified. In 400–500 words explain what the transfer establishes, what remains open under each theory, and why the future comparison cannot simply be equated with the initial transfer. Identify the factual inquiry needed about the privilege. Source-open; no content help or answer keys.
