## Explanation and supported rehearsal

Independent evidence demonstrates a capacity in a changed task; a polished coached essay cannot supply it for every outcome. An empirical premise is a claim about how the world is or would be; a normative premise concerns justification, duty, value or legitimacy. One sentence can contain both. “The register is inherited, therefore its use is wrong” is incomplete until the relevant normative principle and effects are specified. Replacing that leap with a conditional argument is a substantive repair.

Worked invented example: “All residents benefit from street lighting, so the mayor may censor complaints.” Benefit is a factual claim. Unlimited authority requires another justification. A learner can accept the benefit, reject the leap and still defend limited taxation using a separately argued principle. Before assessment, rehearse this distinction on that example in 150 words; it is **P8**, supported practice only. Do not rehearse the selected final form.
