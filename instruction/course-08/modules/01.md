## Explanation

A state-of-nature argument removes a common political superior and asks what follows. It need not assert that every actual society began this way. Hobbes's equality is principally mutual vulnerability and comparable hope of attaining ends, not identical strength or virtue. Scarcity and uncertainty can make preemptive action intelligible even to people who would prefer peace. The important inferential movement is from vulnerability and distrust to insecurity, then to a need for reliable assurance. War here includes a known disposition to conflict, so an interval without fighting is insufficient evidence of stable peace.

Keep three levels distinct. A prudential reason concerns one's safety. An obligation concerns what one is bound to do. Authority concerns a person's or institution's standing to make decisions for others. The claim that a guard deters attacks establishes a benefit; an additional argument must connect that benefit to rightful rule. Chapters 14–15 develop the difference between a liberty to preserve oneself, a rational precept directing peace, and the obligations created by covenants. Test whether the relevant undertaking can be safely performed. Do not reduce all of these to “fear makes right.”

An argument can be valid under specified conditions while its account of human relations is incomplete. A cooperative institution could challenge the supposed necessity of one coercive authority without disproving that assurance matters. Conversely, one successful cooperative day would not establish durable assurance. Distinguish a counterexample to universal necessity from evidence about long-term feasibility.

## Worked practice: invented island storehouse

Ten households prefer to leave food in a shared store. Each fears theft if its own watch is absent. Everyone adds guards, leaving fewer people to grow food. A single paid watch could reduce costs. This supports a conditional coordination claim: if the watch is reliable, joint protection may improve security. It does **not yet** show that the watch may decide marriages or punish criticism. A reconstruction must state the missing premise licensing the scope of authority, then test that premise.

## Independent changed case F1

During a wildfire, its elected coordinator demands permanent control of all communications, arguing that coordinated warnings have saved lives. In 350–450 words, reconstruct the best security argument, identify the step requiring an additional authority premise, and explain what evidence would bear on whether permanent control is necessary.
