## Entry, continuation and exclusions

No prior course is required. M02 requires attribution; M03 requires basic reconstruction; close readings use these together. M10 requires competent source attribution and paragraph reasoning, not a particular philosophical verdict. M11 requires the whole supported portfolio and essay. M12 follows after an interval. Course 2 can begin when its ordinary-language entry skills are demonstrated, while unfinished Course 1 requirements remain recorded.

Formal proof systems, full histories of Plato scholarship, a complete theory of knowledge, contemporary aid evaluation, and an exhaustive argument about divine-command theory are not assessed here. The assigned Meno omissions and Meditation III–VI are not silently imported as completed reading. Later courses extend these questions.
