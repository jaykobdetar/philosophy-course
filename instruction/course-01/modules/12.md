# C01-M12 — Delayed consolidation

**Planned allocation: 6 focused hours (360 minutes), within the 120-hour baseline.**

**Guiding question:** Can I recover and apply the distinctions after time away?

**Prerequisites and outcomes:** For retrieval session 1, the opening four sessions and the recorded early trigger; for all remaining activities, the Module 11 record, with any unresolved criteria named. Recover the central distinctions, apply one to a genuinely different case, correct a mistaken recollection, and state the evidence for the next step (O1–O5).

**Assigned work:** No new reading. Reopen exact passages from previously assigned texts only after the memory portions; use [the delayed assessment forms](../assessment.md#full-course-delayed-demonstration). All six hours belong to Course 1 even when the calendar overlaps the opening modules of Course 2. They reduce the week's time available for Course 2; they do not remove Course 2 requirements.

**Teaching explanation:** Recognition while looking at a text is easier than recovering a distinction and deciding where to use it. Begin with retrieval so the record reveals what persisted. Opening the text afterward is part of correction, not a reason to conceal an error. A comparison needs a specified common question and a difference: two authors' use of “knowledge” does not show that they offer the same account. Transfer means identifying which feature of a new case is relevant and explaining the inference, rather than attaching a familiar philosopher's name.

**Worked example, invented:** A learner remembers “a necessary condition guarantees the result.” The source check shows that guarantee describes sufficiency; necessity rules out the result when the condition is absent. A useful correction adds a new explanation: a charged battery is necessary for this stipulated device to operate, while a damaged switch can still prevent operation. Merely copying a definition would hide whether the direction is now understood.

**Retrieval session 1, 45 minutes, at the earlier opening-check trigger:** Perform this session four to six weeks after Session 4, or on completing two subsequent modules, whichever comes first; record the date after Session 4. Do not wait for Module 11. Without notes, spend 15 minutes recovering validity/soundness, necessary/sufficient conditions, and example/definition, each with an original example. Use 15 minutes for the opening delayed Form D, or its alternate in assessment if exposed. Use 15 minutes checking and marking changes in a different colour or separate file. This is the previously promised opening delayed check, counted once.

**Retrieval session 2, 45 minutes, at the full-course delayed trigger:** This session and the remaining activities occur four to six weeks or two later modules after Module 11’s independent demonstration. The earlier 45-minute session is already debited, leaving 315 minutes. Spend 20 minutes recovering one claim each from *Apology*, *Meno*, Descartes, Gettier, and Singer, with its reason and limitation. Spend 25 minutes checking the actual assigned sources, adding exact locators and marking unsupported memory. Do not pretend a guessed quotation is a source passage.

**Independent changed case:** In the 60-minute application block, complete delayed Form D1 or D2 in [assessment](../assessment.md), with the 30-minute least-secure-group task first and the remaining 30 minutes for its 200–300-word cross-text comparison. Record source exposure and assistance. Criteria: identify the relevant distinction, reconstruct an inference, explain a changed feature, and support any textual comparison with the actual edition. This is independent evidence only when the selected prompt has not been rehearsed.

**Feedback, repair and output:** Assemble one page showing remembered claim, checked source, correction, and a new example. Use feedback to select one weakness for 60 minutes of targeted practice. Within the final 90 minutes, spend 30 revising the explanation, up to 30 on an unexposed alternate task for the repaired outcome, and 30 recording the final evidence status and next step. A failure does not erase earlier success; it identifies what still needs practice. If both forms are exposed, an assessor records a comparable new form before use or leaves the criterion unresolved.

**Next:** Carry the outcome record and one precise practice need into Course 2, with the accumulated 120-hour planned total and actual time separately shown. The course is complete only when essential groups have satisfactory module and independent evidence plus this delayed record; authoring a packet is not evidence that a learner completed it.

**Attempt rules:** For every independent task, the assigned source and a citation sheet are allowed; answer keys, prewritten answers and AI/tutor content help are closed until the first response is saved. Record prior exposure and assistance. If rehearsed, use an unexposed alternate from the course assessment or have an assessor document a comparable new prompt; do not call a rehearsed answer fresh. Practice may use the [tutor guidance](../tutor-notes.md). After submission, use the module's anchors in [examiner notes](../examiner-notes.md).

**Accessible route:** Read in text or use text-to-speech on the same assigned edition/range. Pause to record speaker changes and argument steps. A licensed audiobook can support reading only after its translation and coverage are checked against this assignment; do not assume the local compact audio is complete. Check quotations and locators against the written source. Spoken planning and dictation are allowed; preserve a text equivalent of the assessed output. Dialogue may be a paced typed exchange with the same questions. Adjust time without reducing the intellectual task, and log overruns.
