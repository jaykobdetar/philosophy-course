# C01-M11 — Independent demonstration

**Planned allocation: 10 focused hours (600 minutes), within the 120-hour baseline.**

**Guiding question:** Which skills can I demonstrate on a new task without content help?

**Prerequisites and outcomes:** All earlier modules and the essay. Demonstrate every essential group of [the shared Course 1 grid](../../../current/assessment.md#course-1), not merely the essay topic. O1–O5 are sampled; no average score can hide an unresolved group.

**Assigned work:** Use the primary texts already assigned in Modules 4–9, exact editions/ranges from those packets. No new primary reading is imposed. [Course assessment](../assessment.md#full-course-fresh-demonstration) supplies two complete fresh forms, objection choices, permitted assistance, and exposure rules. Examiner notes remain closed until submission.

**Teaching explanation:** Supported practice and an independent demonstration answer different questions. Revision shows you can respond to feedback; a new prompt tests whether you can perform the skill without the same support. Independence does not require memorizing bibliographic trivia or closing the source. It requires that the reasoning and wording be yours and that no task-specific help arrives during the attempt. A previously read passage can carry a fresh inferential task, but rehearsing its answer destroys that particular evidence.

**Worked example of an evidence decision, invented:** A learner writes a careful essay on Gettier, then cannot distinguish the two speakers in an *Euthyphro* passage. The essay supplies useful evidence of reasoning, but not replacement evidence of accurate attribution in the failed passage. The next step is a targeted attribution repair and different passage check. It is not another entire essay, and it is not a global judgment of the learner's ability.

**Procedure and outputs:** Make three rows: argument/conditions, interpretation, and writing/response. Link each to a supported module artifact and its fresh sample. Reserve the full 150 minutes specified by the grid: 30 for reasoning, 60 for the passage, 60 for a 500–700-word new-objection response. These first two together fit the curriculum's up-to-90-minute fresh reasoning/passage session; the essay response is a separate hour. A twenty-minute unscripted discussion or equivalent typed exchange follows. Extended time may preserve the same outputs when needed; record actual minutes.

For each prompt save the untouched response, exact form, source edition, source availability, and assistance/exposure declaration. A tutor may clarify scheduling or access but cannot explain the case, hint at a conclusion, or score an unfinished answer. After submission, feedback must identify a criterion, a passage or inference, and an actionable repair. The learner may challenge unsupported feedback with exact evidence.

**Independent changed task:** Complete one full assessment form and the essay-topic objection assigned there. During the dialogue answer the course form's questions about an actual submitted inference, not a memorized overview. Criteria: accurate attribution, faithful premise/conclusion structure, explained conditions/countercase, responsive objection handling, traceable sources, and intelligible organization. Conclusions may differ from the assessor's.

**Remediation decision:** For a weak row, spend its 120-minute repair block on one diagnosed error using a different practice case. Use the other fresh form only after repair and only if unexposed; the 120-minute retest/review block can cover at most the needed row or rows that fit, not automatically another complete 150-minute exam. If more work is needed, log extra hours explicitly. Preserve both outcomes and do not relabel a coached revision as fresh evidence.

**Delayed retrieval and next:** Schedule Module 12 across roughly four to six weeks or two later modules. Record the least secure outcome and select an unexposed delayed form. Its six hours remain Course 1 hours within the shared calendar budget. Course 2 can begin where its entry skills are satisfied; an unresolved interpretation/writing requirement remains visible.

**Attempt rules:** For every independent task, the assigned source and a citation sheet are allowed; answer keys, prewritten answers and AI/tutor content help are closed until the first response is saved. Record prior exposure and assistance. If rehearsed, use an unexposed alternate from the course assessment or have an assessor document a comparable new prompt; do not call a rehearsed answer fresh. Practice may use the [tutor guidance](../tutor-notes.md). After submission, use the module's anchors in [examiner notes](../examiner-notes.md).

**Accessible route:** Read in text or use text-to-speech on the same assigned edition/range. Pause to record speaker changes and argument steps. A licensed audiobook can support reading only after its translation and coverage are checked against this assignment; do not assume the local compact audio is complete. Check quotations and locators against the written source. Spoken planning and dictation are allowed; preserve a text equivalent of the assessed output. Dialogue may be a paced typed exchange with the same questions. Adjust time without reducing the intellectual task, and log overruns.
