## Precise remaining dependencies

1. **Translation-sensitive modern-edition claims:** Grube/Cooper Plato or CSM Descartes passages must be obtained and inspected if an assessment turns on their exact wording. No ordinary assignment requires pretending the current reading copy is that edition.
2. **Quoted-text quality:** Descartes and Singer are scans with extraction limitations. A learner/tutor must compare any actual quotation with its page image before accepting it. This packet uses paraphrase and source locators, not unverified verbatim source blocks.
3. **Local access and distribution:** The local Singer scan and Gettier republication are readable here, but this audit does not supply new distribution permission or guarantee another learner's copy. Source text availability and rights are distinct records.
4. **Human evidence:** Learner pilot, measured timing, external expert review, deployed-tutor calibration after changes, and rendered accessibility/visual QA remain pending. Authoring and link checks do not satisfy these.
