# C01-S01 — First encounter with a philosophical text

**Guiding question:** What does Euthyphro claim about prosecuting his father, and how does that differ from my reaction to the case?

**Before reading:** No philosophy background is required. Keep a copy of your original response; this is a baseline, not a graded test.

**Exact sources:** Read [OpenStax 2.5, Reading Philosophy](https://openstax.org/books/introduction-philosophy/pages/2-5-reading-philosophy), then the [marked Jowett *Euthyphro* PDF](https://persweb.wabash.edu/facstaff/helmang/phi110-1516F/phi110-handouts/Euthyphro.pdf) **from 2a up to but not including 5d** (PDF pages 1–4). Stop at the visible 5d marker, not merely at a page edge. The PDF was checked on 2026-10-13 for its Jowett credit and those markers. Internet access is needed for the marked copy; the local Jowett text has no printed Stephanus markers.

**90-minute route:** 15 minutes OpenStax; 20 minutes Plato, noting speaker and emerging question; 20 minutes write a roughly 120-word paraphrase of Euthyphro's reason for prosecuting his father; 10 minutes separate his claim and your reaction; 10 minutes explain the paragraph aloud or in text and request one diagnostic feedback point; 10 minutes complete the small independent check below without task-specific help; 5 minutes save the untouched baseline, note difficulties and choose a next question. Split or extend the session if needed; log actual extra focused minutes.

**Output:** Save `C01-S01-baseline.md` locally with source/edition, locator, untouched paragraph, claim-versus-reaction pair, focused minutes, and one question. Keep personal work in your ignored learner folder. Do not put the essay in a distributable package.

**Practice feedback:** Ask the tutor for at most two consequential issues. Check whether the paraphrase identifies the prosecutor, his father, the death, and the reason Euthyphro thinks prosecution is justified without importing your verdict. If a detail is uncertain, reread 4b–5c. The tutor may show a **different** worked paraphrase, never rewrite this one. See [examiner notes](../examiner-notes.md#c01-s01).

**Small independent check:** Close the tutor exchange. In two sentences, distinguish what Socrates asks at the approach to 5d from Euthyphro's earlier account of the prosecution. Record whether you used the text, notes, or help. This is a fresh wording task, not a secure examination.
