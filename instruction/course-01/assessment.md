# Opening assessment and retrieval forms

These checks cover the first four sessions' outcomes only. They are open-book self-study tasks, not secure examinations or the full Course 1 independent demonstration. Use the [current evidence template](../learner-record.md); record prior exposure and assistance. If a form has already been practiced, write or select another case with the same inferential structure and record why it is comparable.

## Baseline before help

Keep the untouched [Session 1](sessions/01.md) paraphrase and two-sentence speaker/reaction distinction. Do not compare it to a model answer before saving it.

## Fresh transfer after Session 4

1. Read a short *Euthyphro* passage in the assigned 5d–8b range that your tutor has **not** explained line by line. Name the speaker, proposed answer, and one objection with visible Stephanus locator. The source may remain open. Do not use task-specific AI help until the attempt is saved.
2. Analyze: “If a manuscript is translated, its wording may vary. This wording varies. Therefore this manuscript is translated.” Give a countercase or explain why one is impossible. This changes the subject of the opening implication problems; record whether the logical form was previously seen.
3. Explain in three sentences why an action offered as an example may fail as a definition, using a new everyday category of your own choice. Do not borrow the tutor's example.

**Marking anchors:** C01-O1 requires correct speaker/source attribution; C01-O2 requires premises, conclusion and a form-sensitive countercase; C01-O3 requires a real instance/definition distinction; C01-O4 requires an intelligible claim/reason paragraph. The examiner notes support diagnosis. Missing one criterion calls for targeted repair and a different recheck, not a percentage certificate.

## Delayed changed case

After roughly four to six weeks, or two later modules, return to a new short passage and a new conditional case. First attempt from memory what a valid inference rules out and how a definition differs from an example. Then open the source and correct the attempt. Charge actual focused time to Course 1's delayed-consolidation budget; it occupies the same weekly study budget as the next course. Do not repeat a rehearsed form as if unseen.

## Feedback challenge

If an assessor calls an interpretation wrong, ask for the exact criterion, edition/locator, and inference that fails. The assessor should revise an unsupported judgment or mark it provisional. Philosophical disagreement alone is not evidence of incompetence. Keep the challenge and resolution in the substantive record.
