# Opening assessment and retrieval forms

These checks cover the first four sessions' outcomes only. They are open-book self-study tasks, not secure examinations or the full Course 1 independent demonstration. Use the [current evidence template](../learner-record.md); record prior exposure and assistance. Allow about 30 focused minutes for the fresh form and 15 for a delayed form **within Course 1's later Module 4 and delayed-consolidation allocations**, respectively. They are outside the four opening 90-minute sessions and are not added to the 120-hour source baseline. If extra time is needed, log it.

## Baseline before help

Keep the untouched [Session 1](sessions/01.md) paraphrase and two-sentence speaker/reaction distinction. Do not compare it to a model answer before saving it.

## Fresh transfer after Session 4

Use the [marked Jowett PDF](https://persweb.wabash.edu/facstaff/helmang/phi110-1516F/phi110-handouts/Euthyphro.pdf). Choose **Form A** unless the tutor already worked through that slice line by line; then choose B. Both ranges have visible markers in the PDF. The learner may keep the source open but receives no task-specific help until the attempt is saved.

| Form | Exact passage and interpretation prompt | Independent conditional case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | *Euthyphro* **6d up to before 7c**: attribute the proposed account of piety, then explain what Socrates asks that could test it. Cite one visible marker. | “If a manuscript is translated, its wording differs from the original. This wording differs from the original. Therefore this manuscript is translated.” Supply a true-premises/false-conclusion case. |
| B | *Euthyphro* **7c up to before 8b**: explain the move from disagreements to the objection against the proposed account. Name the two speakers and cite one visible marker. | “If a gallery is open, its lights are on. The gallery is closed. Therefore its lights are off.” Supply a true-premises/false-conclusion case. |

For either form, write three further sentences distinguishing one instance of a category from a rule for all instances; choose a new ordinary category and state one limit of the comparison. Do not borrow the tutor's example. Save the passage answer, conditional analysis, and category explanation before feedback. An earlier coached map of the same passage is prior exposure, so the result is a changed prompt under open-book conditions, not proof of unseen-text mastery.

**Marking anchors:** C01-O1 requires correct speaker/source attribution and an objection grounded in the stated slice. For A, the gods' disagreement matters to the proposed criterion; for B, identify the dispute over just/unjust rather than treating every disagreement as arithmetic. C01-O2 requires premises, conclusion and a form-sensitive countercase: A affirms the consequent, B denies the antecedent. C01-O3 requires a real instance/definition distinction. C01-O4's sustained independent writing outcome remains **pending** at this opening scope; three sentences here are only a small practice sample. The [examiner notes](examiner-notes.md) support diagnosis. Missing one assessed criterion calls for targeted repair and a different recheck, not a percentage certificate.

## Delayed changed case

After roughly four to six weeks, or two later modules, use **Form D**: reread *Euthyphro* **5d up to before 6d** in the same marked PDF and explain, with one locator, how an example and a proposed general answer differ. First state from memory what a valid inference rules out. Then analyze: “If a parcel has express postage, it has a tracking number. This parcel lacks a tracking number. Therefore it lacks express postage.” Explain why the direction differs from Form A or B. Open the source and correct the interpretation. Charge actual focused time to Course 1's delayed-consolidation budget; it occupies the same weekly study budget as the next course.

**Exposure rule:** These forms are public in the repository. If the learner has studied a form or an assessor has already supplied its answer, mark that form exposed and do not call its result independent. Have a person choose a different short slice within the already assigned 5d–8b and a new conditional with the same valid/invalid structure, record both exact prompts and why they are comparable, and keep any tutor feedback closed until the attempt. If no fresh comparable form is available, mark the criterion unresolved rather than invent a score.

## Feedback challenge

If an assessor calls an interpretation wrong, ask for the exact criterion, edition/locator, and inference that fails. The assessor should revise an unsupported judgment or mark it provisional. Philosophical disagreement alone is not evidence of incompetence. Keep the challenge and resolution in the substantive record.
