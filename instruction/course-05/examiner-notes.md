## PC-30 anchors

**PA30:** accurate speakers; challenge and target attributes; charitable finite-perfection response; compatibility versus evidential inference; explicit residual burden. Cleanthes D11.1 relaxes infinity and proposes tradeoffs. A reply can be serious without preserving the original conclusion unchanged.

**FA30:** limited capacity may defeat an inference from unprevented harm to malice, but does not by itself prove goodness or the actual necessity of a tradeoff. Unknown goods may make a hypothesis compatible; ignorance is not positive confirmation. Good work reconstructs both sides rather than assigning religious labels. Strong work asks what evidence would establish the proposed constraints.

**BA30:** not every evidential criticism requires logical contradiction; a consistency defense and a favorable inference from observed effects have different burdens. Compensation is proposed, not observed in the scenario. Strong work compares Demea's D10.29 suggestion, Cleanthes's D10.30 objection, and Philo's D10.35 concession without conflating them.

**DA30:** possible purpose does not entail actual purpose or pure goodness inferred from mixed effects. **EA30:** finite constraints change what prevention can reasonably be demanded; residual suffering still needs an evidential assessment and the original infinite-attribute claim has been revised. No position on the existence of God is required to meet the criteria.

## Constructed performance anchors (not learner results)

**Interpretation — developing:** “Elisabeth does not believe minds move bodies.” Relevant topic, wrong target: the exchange asks for intelligibility under nonextension. **Competent:** “Her question grants the phenomenon for discussion but asks how a nonextended cause meets the conditions that seem needed for bodily motion.” Correct target; add letter evidence. **Strong:** “The primitive-union reply disputes the transfer of bodily explanatory conditions to union; whether this answers her depends on whether she demands a mechanism or coherence between distinctness and interaction.” Identifies a genuine interpretive fork and what evidence would decide it. A full submission still needs exact locators.

**Comparison — developing:** “Spinoza believes in one thing, Leibniz many.” A numerical slogan. **Competent:** “Spinoza asks whether what is conceived through another can be substance; Leibniz asks what can ultimately compose a composite without itself having parts.” Distinct explanatory premises. **Strong:** “Both reject treating an ordinary composite as an unexplained final stopping point, but the no-shared-attribute argument and the simple-constituent argument do different work; agreement on dependence alone does not choose between them.” Supported comparison with an explicit limit.

**Research — developing:** “An entry lists a book, so the book supports my reading.” Bibliographic discovery mistaken for evidence. **Competent:** “I read the relevant entry section, traced its primary reference and recorded the inaccessible book as a lead only.” Honest evidence status. **Strong:** “The accessible interpretation depends on material outside our assigned range; I restrict its claim and preserve a rival reading rather than borrowing an unseen argument.” Shows source scope actively shapes the thesis.

**Draft-to-revision comparison pair (invented):** Draft: “Locke's secondary qualities are unreal, so Berkeley agrees with him.” Diagnosis: it confuses real powers with resemblance and hides the disagreement about matter. Revision: “Locke's secondary qualities can be real powers without resembling the ideas they produce. Berkeley challenges the material basis presupposed by that account, so agreement that experience varies is not agreement about what explains it.” The improved inference is what earns credit, not smoother wording.

**Draft-to-revision research pair (invented):** Draft: “The aid says 'vivid and clear'; that is Shapiro's wording.” Revision: “This wording is from Bennett's adaptation. My substantive claim is provisional until I inspect Shapiro's dated letter; I will cite Bennett for the present paraphrase and leave Shapiro's page blank.” Attribution repaired, though the prescribed-edition check remains unresolved. Do not reward filling the blank by guesswork.

Defense: score responsiveness through the actual exchange. A learner can meet the criterion by revising the conclusion. Prepared fluency, confidence, accent and agreement with the examiner are irrelevant. No part of these constructed examples constitutes pilot, calibration or expert-validation evidence.
