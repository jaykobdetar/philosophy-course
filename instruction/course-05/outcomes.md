# Course 5 outcome-to-evidence map

Use the exact essential groups and 30/30/30-minute written allocation in [current assessment](../../current/assessment.md). Each row needs both named practice and an uncoached fresh artifact. Record source access and assistance alongside the evidence. A paper is depth evidence; its scope does not stand for the rest of the course.

| PC-30 only: evil challenge and serious reply | PA30 dialogue map and 600-word evaluation | FA30 or BA30, plus DA30 or EA30; extra 10h, separate from baseline rows |

Use statuses **demonstrated / needs repair / unresolved access / insufficient independent evidence**. For each status add a sentence quoting or identifying the learner's actual inference and the relevant criterion. Do not claim completion from time spent, attendance, religious agreement or an AI score. The module formative checks and final sample are distinct occasions; record if their reasoning became rehearsed.

The source audit records authoring inspection only. The learner creates a separate completion ledger: actual edition; range read; dates/time; page or stable locator checked; audio/text mode; practice and independent artifact; assistance/exposure; reviewer uncertainty; next action. The final delayed task selects the least secure row rather than adding three new exams.
