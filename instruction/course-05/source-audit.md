## PC-30: newly fixed edition and range

**Actual source:** Peter Millican's Hume Texts Online *Dialogues Concerning Natural Religion*, [editorial notes](https://davidhume.org/texts/d/notes), [Part X](https://davidhume.org/texts/d/10), [Part XI](https://davidhume.org/texts/d/11). Notes identify a text following the 1779 first edition with minimal corrections. Inspected 20 October 2026. Start/end verified: **D10.1–D10.36**, with the Part X note also available; **D11.1–D11.21**. The last paragraph of XI records Demea's departure. Complete Parts X–XI are assigned, not merely the excerpts inspected for authoring.

Passages read closely: D10.24–25 (challenge to moral attributes); D10.29–35 (future compensation, effects-based inference and compatibility concession); D11.1–2 (finite perfection and independent-assurance versus inference distinction); D11.9–12 (capacities/general arrangements and renewed evidential limit); D11.14–21 (hypotheses and dialogue close). These support the packet's distinctions. Speaker attribution and D locators were checked; no long quotation is reproduced. The serious reply is the primary **Cleanthes D11.1**, with Philo's D11.2/12 response. It requires no unavailable contemporary response text. KS page markers are site-provided references, not independently collated scans. No claim is made that this is Beauchamp's *Enquiry* edition or that Philo is simply Hume's unqualified voice.

## Outstanding dependencies and release claims

Teaching, exercises and marking design cover all eight baseline modules and the addition. Exact CSM, Shapiro, Curley, Ariew/Garber, Nidditch, Dancy and Beauchamp copies/wording remain **unmatched/access-dependent**. A learner may begin with declared provisional alternatives; edition-specific quotations and translation-sensitive judgments remain unresolved until the actual copy is inspected. This audit is not a replacement for the learner's reading log. No source rights grant, full-text redistribution permission, learner pilot, external expert validation, rendered accessibility check or new tutor calibration is claimed. Links were opened during authoring; later availability is not guaranteed.
