# C05-M08 — Assessment and delayed check (8 hours)

**Question:** Can your reasoning transfer outside the essay's practiced topic? Prerequisites: P7 revised essay and P1–P6; all source records. Outcomes: independently diagnose a Cartesian transition or interaction reply; compare substance/ideas positions on a new question; test a new defense of induction and a claim about necessary connection; respond to a challenge and reconstruct later.

**Sources:** the same exact prescribed ranges and editions as Modules 1–6. The [assessment](../assessment.md) specifies the smaller source range for each task. No new primary work is required. If a required scholarly edition is inaccessible, record the actual accessible text; wording-sensitive scoring remains provisional until checked. The assessor must inspect the passage before judging a dispute about it.

**Budget:** evidence/exposure and source audit 1h; independent written session 1.5h (three tasks of **30 minutes**); defense or written exchange 0.5h; feedback 1h; targeted repair 2h; delayed independent sample 0.5h; final revision/record 1.5h = 8h. Waiting roughly four to six weeks (or until two later modules are completed) adds no study hours. The defense uses 20 minutes of substantive response and 10 minutes recording evidence.

## Prepare without rehearsing the held-out form

Use the [outcome map](../outcomes.md) to record artifact identifiers, relevant paragraphs and unresolved issues. Do not replace this with an overall percentage. A strong paper on Hume does not demonstrate understanding of Spinoza or Elisabeth. The assessor selects an unexposed Form A or B and chooses the Cartesian/interaction and substance/ideas variants away from the essay's central argument. Every essential row receives a fresh independent sample; essay depth does not erase these short samples.

Teaching example for evidence auditing (invented): a learner's polished 2,000-word Berkeley essay supports interpretation, but a comparison saying “Spinoza rationalist, Locke empiricist” does not state either author's explanation. The repair is to identify a shared question and write each premise/conclusion, then attempt a fresh comparison. More prose on Berkeley cannot fill the missing comparison row.

Save **P8**, a one-page grid containing practice evidence, independent evidence, edition, assistance, status and next action for every row. Before testing, check that the selected prompt has not appeared in tutoring. Record a source unavailable to the assessor. Do not claim that a new chat makes a rehearsed prompt fresh.

## Complete, review, repair

Take the selected three prompts in [assessment.md](../assessment.md) with primary sources only. A text-to-speech reader, dictation or prearranged breaks may be used with a record; no content-generating help. Save the untouched attempt. For the defense, explain one premise and answer a genuinely unprepared objection; an asynchronous written exchange with the same response allowance is equivalent.

Feedback names criterion, exact evidence, source support or uncertainty, and one next action. Repair the weakest inference within two hours: 30 minutes passage annotation, 30 minutes reconstruction, 30 minutes testing a counterexample or rival interpretation, 30 minutes revision. If the original attempt was coached or a central misconception remains, use the alternate form's corresponding row after repair. Log any extra time beyond the allowance; do not create an automatic hidden retest budget.

At roughly four to six weeks or two later modules complete the 30-minute **D** prompt for the least secure group, or **E** if D was exposed. Use no notes for the first 10 minutes; consult only the named primary text for the final 20 and mark corrections. Save both phases. Criteria are accurate reconstruction, a supported comparison or objection, exact scope, and retention without live help. Delay is a separate occasion, not a relabeled revision.
