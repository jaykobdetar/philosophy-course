# C05-M07 — Essay and revision (22 hours)

**Question:** Can you defend a bounded historical interpretation while taking its best objection seriously? Prerequisites: P1–P6 and source access for the chosen topic. Outcomes: sustain an argument over 1,800–2,000 words; use two exact primary passages and one checked scholarly interpretation; revise reasoning and distinguish historical interpretation from contemporary repair.

**Sources:** use the same prescribed edition/ranges in Modules 1–6 for your chosen question. Do not add an entire monograph. The bounded scholarly starting points below are research aids, not substitutes for the primary text. Actual wording and pagination remain dependent on the learner's copy. Follow the [reference protocol](../../../current/reference.md).

**Budget:** question/proposal 2h; targeted rereading 3h; scholarly source exercise 3h; outline/draft 7h; feedback 2h; independent I7 1h; revision/citation audit 3h; delayed D7 1h = 22h.

## Choose and limit the question

Choose exactly one:

1. Does the distinction between present clear perception and remembered proof answer the Cartesian circle? Use III and V, with IV as needed. Limit the paper to the dependency, not every proof of God.
2. Does the primitive notion of union answer Elisabeth's interaction objection? Use 6 May, 21 May and 28 June 1643, checking 10 June and 1 July for the exchange's development. Distinguish intelligibility from a mechanical model.
3. Does Berkeley's account of general signs answer the need for abstract ideas? Use Introduction §§10–16, within the full assigned Introduction; use Part I only if it supports your exact inference. Do not equate a challenge to abstraction with a complete proof of immaterialism.
4. Does Hume's appeal to custom answer the question raised by his argument about induction? Use Enquiry IV–V and a bounded connection to VII or XII. Separate explanation, practical indispensability and justification.

Fill a 200-word proposal: **question; provisional answer; three-premise argument; strongest objection; two primary locations; rival interpretation; excluded question**. A question is ready when a serious rival could answer it differently using the same assigned evidence. If the proposed conclusion requires unseen sources or doctrines outside the excerpts, narrow it.

## Research procedure and source dossier

Within the three-hour research allowance, spend 30 minutes locating a relevant section, 60 reading it, 45 tracing one primary reference and one dissenting citation, and 45 recording the result. Suitable starting routes: [Descartes' Epistemology](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/descartes-epistemology/) §6.1; [Elisabeth, Princess of Bohemia](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/elisabeth-bohemia/) §4.2; [George Berkeley, Supplement A](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/berkeley/meaning.html) §A.2; [David Hume](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/hume/) §2.3. These headings were available at authoring; entries change, so record the displayed author, revision date and section actually read. The Hume entry is the 2026 replacement by Hsueh Qu and Elizabeth S. Radcliffe; do not attribute its current prose to the previous authors.

Use a scholarly interpretation only after reading its actual argument. An accessible SEP section can satisfy the minimum; a bibliography item seen only in a citation cannot. If a source relies on unassigned primary material, report that dependency and restrict its use. Inspect the cited primary passage yourself. A later citation search tests whether a dispute has moved; record “no usable later source found within the search bound” if that is the result. Never invent a research consensus.

Dossier template: full citation/link; edition; section/pages read; question addressed; interpretive claim in your own words; supporting primary locator; strongest alternative; what the source changes in your argument; access/quotation limits. Keep full borrowed excerpts in the private learner workspace, not the distributed course.

## Teach and model a revision

An interpretation argues that a passage has a certain role; a philosophical evaluation tests that role's reasoning. A contemporary repair changes a premise or standard. Mark these three activities separately. For each paragraph, write its job in the margin: establish text, explain inference, introduce objection, answer objection, or qualify conclusion. If removing the paragraph leaves the argument unchanged, it probably needs a clearer role.

Invented miniature draft: “Hume says habit makes us expect the future, therefore he proves induction.” Diagnosis: the conclusion shifts from an explanation of expectation to a justification of its reliability. Revised model: “Custom explains the transition to an expectation. A defender must still say whether this answers IV's demand for a reason or replaces that demand with a different account of reasonable inquiry.” The revision changes the disputed inference and exposes the next question. It does not supply your paper's verdict.

Write a 1,800–2,000-word draft: approximately 200 framing, 500 reconstruction, 400 interpretive dispute, 350 objection, 350 reply/qualification, 100 conclusion. These are planning allocations, not mandatory paragraph lengths. Use at least two precise primary passages and the checked scholar. Ask for feedback on one textual interpretation and one inferential bridge. Submit **P7** revised essay plus a 300-word memo with old claim/new claim/reason/evidence/residual difficulty. Record help; an AI-written replacement paragraph does not demonstrate your own revision.

## Independent I7 (60 minutes)

Use your source dossier and relevant primary text; no draft, tutor, AI or examiner notes. Invented source note: “A library catalogue lists a 2007 book on the correspondence, so it confirms that Elisabeth accepted Descartes's answer. A web adaptation uses 'vivid and clear'; I shall quote that wording as Shapiro's.” Write 250–350 words explaining what is and is not established, and give a finite verification plan with a stop condition. Then write a 100-word statement separating one historical claim in your own project from a proposed repair. Criteria: evidence status, edition fidelity, bounded next action, distinct argumentative roles.

## Repair, retrieval, next step

If an objection targets a view the author does not hold, replace it before polishing. If a source is merely decorative, give it a specific argumentative job or remove it. D7 after 7–14 days: 20 minutes closed notes reconstructing your thesis and its hardest remaining objection; 20 minutes checking your dossier; 20 minutes explaining whether you still endorse the thesis. Next: Module 8. Give the assessor only your topic, evidence grid and exposure record before form selection.
