# Outcome and evidence map

The three essential rows reproduce the [shared Course 7 grid](../../current/assessment.md). All three require both practice and independent evidence; a polished paper cannot hide a missing row. Independent means no content assistance during the attempt. Exposure to a model answer invalidates that form as fresh evidence.

| Essential row | Measurable outcomes | Named coached evidence | Independent fresh/alternate | Delayed repair sample |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge testimony and injustice | Separate truth, belief, justification and knowledge; evaluate a reliability condition; compare experiential and default-trust proposals; identify identity-prejudicial credibility discount without treating all error as injustice. | P1 map + 600 words; P2 eight-cell matrix; P3 600-word analysis and overbreadth counterexample. | F1 or B1, 35 minutes, [assessment](assessment.md). Explicitly addresses all three distinct questions. M1–M3 I tasks provide earlier evidence but do not replace the final integrated sample. | D1 or E1, 30 minutes, 4–6 weeks/two later modules. |
| Self and identity | Distinguish sameness of organism, consciousness and psychological continuity; explain branching; accurately reconstruct one SN 22.59 claim and its practical purpose; restrict cross-tradition comparison. | P4 continuity map; P5 700-word comparison with direct/interpretive evidence labels. | F2 or B2, 30 minutes; actual assigned passage selected as specified. | D2 or E2, 30 minutes. |
| Freedom and responsibility | Reconstruct PAP and a Frankfurt-style challenge; reconstruct the 1975 van Inwagen argument; identify the exact premise targeted by a changed objection. | P6 seven-line map and 600-word objection paper. | F3 or B3, 25 minutes; labeled 1975 premises. | D3 or E3, 30 minutes. |

Course outcome “defend a focused position” is assessed through P7's revised 1,800–2,200-word paper, an independent defense and the fresh samples. Interpretation, evidence/attribution, reasoning, objection/reply, organization and revision use the [shared rubric](../../current/assessment.md); no compensatory aggregate pass score is used.

For every row record: source edition and locator; artifact path; date; help/exposure; criterion-specific evidence; status (demonstrated / needs repair / source judgment pending / not attempted); exact next task; delayed date. Additions require their own evidence and do not repair a missing baseline row by substitution. Verified source access is a condition for source-based judgments, independent of reasoning quality on the supplied invented cases.
