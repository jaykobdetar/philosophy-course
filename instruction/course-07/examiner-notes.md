## Unscripted 20-minute defense

Spend 3 minutes having the learner restate their own claim, 7 on one objection, 5 on revision/limit and 5 checking attribution/ownership. Select a challenge from an actual weakness rather than reciting a model conclusion. For a truth/justification conflation, ask how a justified false report affects their claim; for an identity argument, ask which link could branch; for responsibility, ask whether their conclusion needs PAP as an extra premise. Keep the exact changed feature unannounced until the exchange. These are examiner selection rules, not prewritten learner answers. Record the actual question and response. Accessible written exchange can replace speech with equivalent reasoning demand.
