## Entry check and working conventions

Prerequisites are Course 1, Course 5 and the semantic/symbolization parts of Course 2. In 20 minutes within Module 1, translate “A reliable report can still be false” without treating “can” as a truth-functional connective; explain why `R → T` differs from a high success rate; give a case satisfying `J ∧ B ∧ T` while challenging a knowledge attribution. A correct explanation of antecedent, consequent, counterexample and quantifier scope is enough. Advanced proof completion is not an additional gate. Repair failures with the relevant Course 2 semantic practice, logging extra time if the M1 repair allowance is exhausted.

Read in a text-accessible edition or use lawful audio/text-to-speech of the assigned range. Listening is credited within reading time, not in addition to it. Pause to record claim, reason, locator and question. Before citing, return to the specified written text: audio timestamps do not replace page/section locators. Spoken exercises may be transcribed or replaced with equivalent written responses; assessment response modes use the same reasoning criteria. No audiobook availability is asserted.

Each packet separates explanation, coached practice and an independent changed case. Do not ask a tutor or AI to solve an independent prompt. Record ordinary access aids, sources used, prior exposure, time and assistance. A missing required text pauses its source-bound judgment, not unrelated practice. Do not substitute a summary for the assigned reading or call a provisional interpretation verified.

Run `python instruction/course-07/verify.py` from the repository root. See [source audit](source-audit.md) and [verification](verification.md) for what was actually checked. The materials do not claim comprehensive metaphysics, all epistemology, all Buddhism or all Indian philosophy.
