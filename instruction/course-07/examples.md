# Annotated practice responses and a substantive revision

All passages below are **original invented teaching responses**, not learner submissions or source quotations. They are for coached practice only, not any F/B/D/E prompt. Labels apply to the displayed criterion, not to a person's ability or a validated numerical score.

## Practice case

A garden club's temperature notice is copied by three volunteers. The notice was posted before a sensor developed a fault. Sana, whose handheld instrument was recently calibrated, reports a lower temperature. The chair ignores her because of an identity stereotype. Later the original temperature happens to recur. Evaluate support, dependence and credibility injustice separately. No precise accuracy rate is supplied.

### Developing response

“The three volunteers agree, so the notice is reliable. Sana was wrong because the original temperature later returned. The chair was unfair, which proves testimonial injustice.”

**Annotation:** It identifies reliability and fairness as relevant but treats copied reports as independent, uses later truth to settle earlier warrant and omits the specific causal relation between prejudice and credibility deficit. The central repairs are evidential independence and the injustice conditions. No prose polish would fix those inferences.

### Competent response

“The volunteers repeat one source, so their agreement does not by itself add three observations. Sana has a relevant independent instrument, though calibration alone does not guarantee this reading. The later recurrence does not show which belief was justified earlier. On the central account of testimonial injustice studied here, the chair's identity stereotype supplies a mechanism for discounting Sana as a knower; whether there is also legitimate contrary evidence should be assessed separately.”

**Annotation:** Distinguishes time, truth, justification and source dependence; identifies the relevant injustice mechanism and leaves a reasonable evidential qualification. In a submitted source-based response it would also require a checked Fricker chapter locator and a located Hume/Reid claim. This example demonstrates reasoning, not a completed citation audit.

### Strong response

“The agreement inherits the original notice's evidential strengths and weaknesses because it has one informational origin. The sensor's later fault may not undermine the notice as originally posted; we need the times of posting, fault and Sana's observation before judging the present claim. Sana's calibration gives a reason for uptake, not infallibility. Even if that reason were defeated by independent evidence, the chair could still impose an additional unwarranted discount through prejudice. The wrong therefore cannot be read directly from whether the number is true. A repair would compare equally supported reports under the same evidential standard while preserving the distinction between empirical uncertainty and prejudicial treatment.”

**Annotation:** Tests a pivotal temporal assumption, gives a serious alternative, and explains how warranted doubt could coexist with an unjust additional discount. It does not invent missing times. Exact source attribution would still need verification; sophistication does not waive that condition.

## Draft → revision: comparing projects

**Draft:** “Locke and SN 22.59 agree that the self is just memory. If memory splits into two people, both people are the same person, and the Buddhist text proves there is no real responsibility anyway.”

**Feedback:** The draft combines four claims without their bridges. Identify the different questions; check whether SN actually proposes a memory criterion; test the fission claim against transitivity; locate any premise concerning responsibility. A claim unsupported by the assigned text must be withdrawn or defended with an explicitly different argument.

**Revision:** “A branching case tests whether the proposed psychological relation is sufficient for numerical identity: two distinct successors cannot both be identical with the earlier person under ordinary transitivity. That constraint does not decide whether psychological continuity can still matter practically. The assigned discourse examines whether aggregates are appropriately identified as self and links its inquiry to liberation. Comparing the role of consciousness therefore requires two separate reconstructions. Neither a branching diagram nor this discourse alone establishes that responsibility disappears.”

**Revision commentary:** The revision removes the unsupported memory definition and the invalid identity inference. It replaces the claim of full agreement with a narrow comparative question and withdraws a normative conclusion lacking a bridge. The next step is source-bound: add a precise Locke or Parfit passage and SN segment, then test whether the reconstructed questions fit those passages. This is substantive repair rather than a change of tone.

## Research miniature

Weak source note: “An article says Moore refutes skepticism.” Improved note template: author/title/year; exact inspected section; author's specific interpretation; Moore passage it interprets; one rival reading; access/edition limit. If only an abstract is available, label “metadata/abstract only” and leave the interpretive evidence pending. This miniature teaches dossier quality without inventing an article or a scholar's conclusion.
