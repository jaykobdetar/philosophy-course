# Module 8 — Assessment and delayed transfer

**10 hours**, including the later delayed check. Guiding question: can you reconstruct and apply the course's distinctions on fresh material without coaching? Prerequisites: P1–P7, exact source access and a completed evidence grid. Outcomes: independently demonstrate all three essential clusters, explain one's own reasoning, correct gaps and retain a least-secure distinction.

## Assignment and access

Use the exact assigned primary readings in [register](../readings.md), the [assessment forms](../assessment.md) released by the assessor, and the [outcome map](../outcomes.md). Examiner notes are separate. A source-bound score is provisional if the assessor cannot inspect the actual assigned passage. This is not remedied by agreement among AI outputs.

## Explanation and worked preparation

Assessment checks transfer rather than recall of a practiced verdict. Source-open means you may consult the listed readings; it does not mean a tutor may reconstruct the answer for you. “Fresh” describes exposure to this task, not whether the philosophical subject has been studied. An earlier coached response cannot become independent merely by being submitted later.

Original preparation example: a learner's identity essay is excellent, but its evidence grid has no responsibility argument. The next step is the responsibility sample, not another identity essay. A second learner uses a clear three-condition testimonial-injustice analysis but attributes it to an unread chapter. The reasoning may be promising while the attribution criterion remains pending. Keep these statuses separate.

Before the assessment, rehearse only the procedure on your own already marked P artifacts: locate claim, premise, inferential gap, source and limit. Do not rehearse the released F/B forms. The independent passage uses a genuinely uncoached part of the assigned text selected according to the assessment protocol; if all listed passages have been coached, the assessor must select another bounded assigned passage and record it before the sitting.

Unused repair/retest time becomes independent review and source checking within the same budget. More than one failed alternate or substantial new reading may require transparently logged extra time. A pass is not inferred from spending 600 minutes.

## Independent changed task and criteria

The actual changed cases are F1–F3 (35/30/25 minutes). They evaluate testimonial support/injustice, continuity with a precise SN claim, and a premise-specific responsibility objection. The examiner supplies one of the stated exact passage ranges and checks it before the sitting. Submit written work, sources used, assistance/exposure declaration and time. The 20-minute follow-up probes an unannounced objection to the learner's actual claim, following the bounded procedures in examiner notes. A text exchange of comparable reasoning demand is available.

## Remediation, delayed retrieval and next step

If two theories are conflated, first complete a question–premise–conclusion comparison. If evidence is missing, locate and inspect the passage before repairing the claim. Feedback may coach repair; it must stop before a different independent form starts. Record unresolved essential rows instead of averaging them away.

At roughly **4–6 weeks or after two later modules**, release D1/D2/D3 for the least secure essential row, or E if D was exposed. Spend 30 minutes on the new case. This is the course's final delayed transfer, normally scheduled during Course 8 on the default route, **charged once to Course 7**. A 3–7-day module retrieval does not replace it. Next: carry the unresolved distinctions and scheduled delay forward in the learner's progress record; completion remains conditional until every essential row and source-bound criterion is resolved.
