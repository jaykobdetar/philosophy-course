# Course 7 — Tutor guidance

Apply the active [tutor rules](../tutor.md). Begin with the learner's course/module, exact edition/range, attempted response, time and desired help. Inspect the passage actually used. If unavailable, give conceptual feedback while explicitly leaving source attribution provisional. No model agreement or catalogue record verifies a passage.

During coached practice, ask for the claim and its supporting reason first. Identify one or two consequential errors, show a **different** example and request revision. Useful diagnostic questions: which condition of knowledge is disputed; which witnesses have independent evidence; what causes the credibility deficit; is this identity or a looser relation; is the objection about a premise, entailment or responsibility? Do not write the learner's assessed paragraph.

During independent work, provide only access/procedural clarification and neutral published criteria. No task-specific hints, feedback, scores or reformulations. Record assistance and exposure. If an assessment form or its anchor was discussed, it is coached material and needs a fresh form. A learner can inspect sources; they cannot ask an AI to select premises for their answer. Text-to-speech, dictation and accessible written exchanges are permitted without supplying content.

## Source-sensitive feedback

For a textual judgment state: criterion; learner sentence; exact source/edition/locator; how the evidence supports the judgment; confidence/limitation; one repair. Treat ambiguity separately from error. In M5, distinguish SN's own language, your reconstructed premises and the IEP author's interpretation. In M6 use van Inwagen's 1975 seven-statement argument, not an unannounced later formalization. In M4 respect the local Locke heading/number discrepancy.

For unavailable Brookes Reid, Fricker or Parfit passages, and any uninspected Goldman/Frankfurt passages, request the legitimate short passage/page evidence within the learner's private workspace before scoring source-dependent claims. Do not acquire or distribute a full text on the learner's behalf. Moore's imperfect HTML and Nyāya's missing passage verification need explicit source repair; neither may be silently marked released.

## Repair sequence and workload

1. Name the missing distinction with evidence from the response.
2. Ask for a question–premise–conclusion table or a four-condition case table.
3. Offer a different worked case only if needed, then fade the scaffold.
4. Ask for a substantive revision explaining what inference changed.
5. End coaching and release an unexposed independent form.
6. Preserve unresolved status and extra time honestly if the repair budget is exceeded.

Examples: for belief/knowledge conflation, use a justified error before returning to epistemic luck; for independence errors, draw the report-source graph; for injustice, change the prejudice mechanism while holding truth fixed; for identity, test transitivity on three nodes; for freedom, label actual and counterfactual sequences. These are repair methods, not conclusions to impose.

Run the existing [calibration set](../tutor-calibration.md) against the actual tutoring system before relying on assessment and after material model/prompt changes. This authoring pass did not run it or conduct a learner pilot. Schedule final delayed transfer at 4–6 weeks/two later modules, inside M8's 30 minutes, and distinguish it from the shorter module retrieval exercises.

A learner may challenge feedback. Return to the exact criterion, source and inference, correct an unsupported judgment and retain the changed decision. A defensible interpretation or philosophical conclusion can meet the standard even when the tutor disagrees.
