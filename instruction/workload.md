## Credit and access rules

C12A's 20-hour Frege/Russell foundation is already in its baseline. Other C12 routes add the shared bridge once. If later taking C12A, retain and credit that work once. C06 Sartre credit in C12C requires the specified evidence and Macomber check; no credit is assumed before it is earned.

An authored budget does not establish access to every required edition. Consult course source audits; unavailable reading-dependent tasks remain pending.

Each module includes its own practice, feedback, assessment and delayed work. Delayed checks take time from the current calendar's study budget but remain charged to their originating course. Keep actual hours and any extra remediation separate.
