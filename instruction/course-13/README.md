# Course 13 — Advanced seminar and research apprenticeship

**Authored 20 October 2026.** Choose **one** route: [13A AI language and consciousness](tracks/13a.md), [13B Comparative moral cultivation](tracks/13b.md), or [13C Social knowledge and justice](tracks/13c.md). Each route combines its complete reading/teaching packet with **all eight shared modules below, totaling 160 hours**. Route packets are part of these hours, not three additional courses. Use a module and the corresponding row of your route packet together.

Prerequisites: complete Course 11 and one compatible Course 12 option. A requires 12A; B requires 11A and Course 6 and accepts any completed Course 12 option; C requires 11B and 12D. A learner who cannot reconstruct two prerequisite arguments completes the identified repair before the seminar; extra repair time is logged separately. Existing opening sessions belong to Course 1 and receive no second credit here. Revisited prerequisite readings receive focused seminar rereading time, not duplicate prerequisite completion hours.

Teaching and tasks are authored; the course is **not empirically validated or unconditionally source-ready**. Several modern translations and copyrighted readings require library/personal access and passage checking. The exact substantive comparison of Van Norden/Soles, detailed Dotson distinctions, and quotation-level judgments from unavailable texts remain provisional until the specified passages are inspected. A bibliographic record is never enough to certify these judgments. No full copyrighted readings are distributed here.

Read aloud or use lawful text-to-speech for the assigned text; keep a written citation/locator sheet and a text transcript of your own spoken work. Listening counts against reading time, with pauses for reconstruction. A summary podcast is enrichment, not an equivalent edition. The defense has a typed equivalent with the same questions and reasoning demand; adjust pacing for accessibility and record time without penalizing speech speed. All instructions are available in Markdown; rendered visual/accessibility testing remains pending.
