# Course 13 outcome and evidence map

The exact Course 13 grid in [assessment baseline](../../current/assessment.md#course-13) governs all three tracks. “Competent” requires identifiable textual support, a relevant inference, calibrated scope and honest source/assistance records. Strong work handles a live alternative; style or confidence cannot compensate for missing evidence.

| Essential group | Named supported practice | Independent fresh evidence | Planned time/location | Required finding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source evaluation and debate mapping | R1–R4/AM1–2, M3 D and source notes, M4 DM/P | M8 SA, S1 or S2 on an unfamiliar verified related paper | 30 min in M8 | Correctly distinguish metadata, declared aim and demonstrated argument; explain relevance, limit and concrete verification need |
| Specialist interpretation and argument | M5 E1, M6 E2/O and change memo | M7 DEF with a new objection and source/translation decision | 10-min explanation + 20-min exchange = 30 min in M7 | Fair reconstruction, direct answer, source reason for accepting/rejecting a rival, substantive response rather than repetition |
| Transfer | M7 T1: independently written 800-word changed-case analysis | M8 T2: different delayed case, 250–350 words | T1 2h in M7; T2 30 min in M8 after 4–6 weeks or two later modules | Apply a taught distinction to a genuinely changed inference, explain limits, preserve accurate attribution |

T1 is an independent first occasion and is the grid’s module evidence; T2 is the separate later occasion. Do not reuse T1 as T2. SA’s 30 minutes belong to the existing source/readiness audit, not an extra examination. DEF is the existing M7 defense, not a second dialogue. The short M1–M6 independent checks diagnose methods but do not erase missing course-grid evidence.

## Route coverage within the grid

| Route | Interpretation anchors | Comparative/transfer distinction | Source integrity gate |

| C13C | C1 ignorance mechanism; C2 chapters 1/7; C3/C4 institution/equality arguments; C5 silencing mechanism | Credibility versus intelligibility; interpersonal versus structural; diagnosis versus remedy | Actual full chapters/articles; empirical premises distinguished from stipulations; no real-person allegations |

The Q/P/E2/portfolio sequence additionally checks question feasibility, sustained reasoning and readiness for capstone. The paper uses the shared dimensions of question/claim, interpretation/context, reasoning, objection/reply, evidence/attribution, organization/expression and revision. DEF checks dialogue. Formal work is not a separate Course 13 outcome. Translation sensitivity is required for B and whenever an actual interpretation depends on a translation; A/C justify their source/version/terminology decision instead of inventing a translation issue.

## Blank record

For each row save: learner/date/route; artifact IDs; precise passage(s); assistance and exposure; criterion; observed evidence; competent/developing/unresolved; source-access caveat; exact repair; fresh form/date; actual minutes; decision. Record additional remediation beyond module reserves explicitly. A defensible minority interpretation can pass when supported; assessor agreement is not the criterion. No empirical learner or expert validation is represented by this map.
