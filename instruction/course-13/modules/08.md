# C13-M08 — Capstone readiness portfolio (10 hours)

**Question:** Does the evidence justify beginning a feasible capstone? Prerequisites: E2, DEF, T1 and source dossier. Outcomes: audit traceable evidence; independently evaluate an unfamiliar verified related abstract/introduction; transfer reasoning after a delay; propose a question with a lawful access plan and identified weakness.

Read [outcomes](../outcomes.md), [assessment](../assessment.md) only at release, and the actual cited passages selected for audit. Follow the route-specific fresh source selection protocol; do not substitute a fabricated abstract for the required verified paper. The separate original exercise below teaches the method but does not satisfy the source-evaluation fresh row.

## Teaching

A portfolio is an argument for readiness. Each essential outcome needs a named practice artifact and an independent performance. A polished paper cannot establish that you can evaluate a source outside its narrow literature. Nor does a source quiz establish sustained argument. Audit by outcome, then inspect any missing link before forming a global judgment.

For each cited claim ask: did I actually read the source; does the exact passage support this sentence; is the edition identifiable; is the author’s voice distinct from mine; do quotation marks and locators match? A page mismatch can be corrected. An invented source or attributed quotation requires removing the claim and reconsidering every argument depending on it. Treating such a problem as a minor formatting issue would leave the portfolio’s evidence unreliable.

Readiness is narrower than mastery. A feasible capstone question has accessible sources, a method you can explain, a manageable objection and a plan for the weakest skill. Unresolved source dependencies can make a proposal premature even when the conceptual exercises are competent. Choose a narrower feasible project or obtain the source before proceeding.

**Worked exercise, invented:** A bibliography lists ten impressive titles, but only two were read, one supports a contextual footnote and the other challenges the thesis. The useful dossier may still be only two entries. Correct action: label the other eight “discovery only,” remove unsupported claims, preserve the real challenge and see whether the thesis survives. Bibliography length is not evidence quality.

## Portfolio and independent tasks

Compile Q, R1–R4, AM1–2, D, DM, P, E1/E2, O, change memo, DEF and T1. Add a one-page evidence grid using [outcomes](../outcomes.md). Choose six pivotal citations across at least three core sources and both M3 sources; check wording/locator/role against the actual text. “All citations checked” is not acceptable if only those six were sampled; report the sample and any wider audit separately.

SA: **30 minutes** to evaluate one verified related paper’s unfamiliar abstract/introduction, using assessment form S1 or S2. This is source-open and independent, with no live help. The assessor must verify identity, lawful access and passage extent before release. Without a usable paper the row is **blocked**, not satisfied by a hypothetical substitute.

T2: after **roughly four to six weeks or two later modules**, write **250–350 words in 30 minutes** on route-specific delayed case D1 or D2. Sources visible; no content help, key or previous answer. This differs from M7 T1 and is the second transfer occasion. Log actual date and elapsed interval. Early retrieval practice cannot replace it.

Write a 500-word capstone readiness note: proposed question; two accessible primary anchors; one objection; evidence of independent skill; weakest prerequisite; repair/access plan; explicit exclusions. Completion requires every essential row competent with source integrity; use “ready with specified access dependency” only when the future project can proceed without pretending that dependency is resolved.

## Budget, remediation and exit

Portfolio selection/source reading **2h**; citation audit **2h**; SA **0.5h**; T2 **0.5h**; feedback **1h**; readiness proposal **2h**; targeted repair, fresh retest and final record **2h** = **10h**. Reserve the delayed half-hour even if other work finishes early. If a group fails, use the alternate unexposed form after a targeted repair; log any time beyond the reserve. Next step: carry the evidence and access plan to Course 14’s proposal milestone.
