# C13-M07 — Independent defense and transfer (10 hours)

**Question:** Can I explain and adapt the argument when the question changes? Prerequisite: E2, O and revision memo. Outcomes: defend a claim responsively; justify one source/translation decision; analyze a genuinely new related case in 800 words independently.

Read your own E2 and its source locators, not the examiner key. Source-open rereading uses the exact edition/ranges in [sources](../sources.md) and D. The assessor chooses one unexposed [assessment form](../assessment.md). If an item has been rehearsed, use its alternate; do not count it fresh. No surprise specialist text outside the taught packet is required.

## Teaching and practice before release of the fresh form

A defense asks whether you can track the implications of your own argument. Begin an answer by identifying the claim challenged. Then give the relevant reason, qualify if needed and state what follows. “I do not know because the needed source is unavailable” can be an accurate source judgment; it does not establish the missing interpretation outcome. A confident but unsupported answer is weaker.

A case transfers learning only when something philosophically relevant changes. Replacing names in an already answered example is weak evidence. Here the fresh tasks change the relation between evidence and conclusion, cultivation and agency, or institutional diagnosis and remedy. Your job is to show which premises survive the change. A defensible answer may remain uncertain, but its uncertainty must be explained.

**Worked practice, invented and not a final case:** A researcher concludes that a test gives evidence of a capacity. Asked whether failing the test proves absence, she replies: “Only if possessing the capacity entails passing under these conditions. I have defended an evidential connection in the other direction, so failure alone is insufficient.” This answers the changed inference instead of repeating the original claim. Practice by giving a two-minute version of one argument and answering “Which premise could be false even if your conclusion were true?” Save the coached practice as DEF-P, separate from DEF.

## Independent outputs

DEF: ten-minute explanation followed by **twenty minutes** of new questions, within the curriculum’s fifteen-to-twenty-minute range. Total **30 minutes**, counted once for the specialist interpretation row. Typed equivalent: give a prepared outline, then respond independently to the same questions in an accessible pacing arrangement. Assess responsiveness and reasons, not speed/accent/confidence. Assessor must ask why a rival interpretation or source was rejected and one fresh objection.

T1: **800 words**, selected route’s fresh case A or alternate B, **two hours planned**, source-open with only assigned texts and citation sheet. No AI, tutor, solution, E2 prose copying or live content help. Record prompt/version, prior exposure, actual time and tools. Neutral criteria: identify the relevant change; reconstruct two source-based claims; state an explicit inference; consider a serious rival; distinguish stipulated facts from what requires further evidence. No prescribed verdict.
