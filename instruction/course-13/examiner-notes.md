## Feedback procedure

Identify the criterion and quote or locate the learner’s relevant sentence. State one consequential success and at most two repair priorities. Distinguish logical error, missing evidence, plausible alternative and simple disagreement. Model a different example, then have the learner revise. Never generate assessed prose. After independent work, record conditions before releasing feedback; contaminated forms need an unexposed alternate. Source/quotation fabrication requires correction before continuing assessment. Do not average a major source error against good style.

For each R reconstruction look for local question, attributed premises, bracketed additions, clear support relation, rival reading and qualified evaluation. For DM check whether the three positions address a common issue. For E2 check that at least one serious objection targets the actual argument and that the response has an inferential effect. The revision memo should distinguish accepted criticism, rejected criticism and remaining cost. Different defensible answers can meet the same standard.

## Module-specific independent task anchors

**M1-I, authenticity question.** A competent answer narrows the universal claim, for example to whether one described training process undermines reflective endorsement. It notes that one fictional case cannot establish what every socially learned response is like and a paragraph requires surrounding context/genre. A good exclusion is all social learning or empirical prevalence. Failure: merely replace “every” with “usually” without evidence for frequency. Repair: state a conditional claim about the stipulated case and identify the exact primary question.

**M2-I-A, silent planner.** The planner’s lack of conversation does not by itself establish absence of subjective experience; the test and target property differ. A shared-storage description does not yet establish the precise functional conditions of a theory, and even a verified indicator needs its connecting assumptions. Accept a reasoned challenge to the indicator framework. Fail a confident consciousness verdict based on a component name. Source check: A1 §§1–2; A5 §§1.2, 2.2, 2.5. Do not infer architecture absent from the case.

**M2-I-B, developing concern.** Improvement is compatible with development of initial dispositions and with a transformation account; the observation alone underdetermines which explanation is correct. A competent response distinguishes that logical point from each text’s actual account and cites B2/B3. Strong work identifies a substantive remaining difference concerning the source/normative role of development. Fail treating improvement as proof of no initial disposition, or simply asserting the authors agree. Exact B2/B3 reading verification pending until copies are supplied.

**M2-I-C, understood but distrusted.** The case stipulates conceptual comprehension and prejudice in credibility. An exclusive conceptual-gap diagnosis neglects the stipulated mechanism; credibility repair is relevant. A suitable remedy might change evaluation procedures and accountability, with evidence about implementation. Do not require a particular policy. Fail claiming that a vocabulary lesson alone resolves stipulated prejudice. Full C2/C5 conditions require text checking; a plausible conceptual answer cannot substitute for it.

**M3-I, index/abstract/blog.** A competent answer reports a verified bibliographic record and the abstract’s announced project, without saying the argument succeeds. It proposes reading the actual critique and checking the targeted premise/primary citation. It gives a finite search/access action rather than unlimited browsing. Fail using the blog’s verdict as proof or falsely marking the paper fully read.

**M4-I, three predicates.** Reliability, social effects and subjective experience can coexist; the claims are not contradictory merely because they differ. A common question might concern what reliable reports justify about experience, with a connecting evidential premise. The social-effects claim may belong to another inquiry unless linked by argument. Strong work distinguishes explanatory and moral relevance. Fail forcing the claims into three exclusive camps.

**M5-I, failed argument versus false conclusion.** A’s first statement denies the sufficiency of the efficiency argument; it does not assert the policy is unjust. B offers another justification, so the two claims can both hold. Inferring injustice needs an additional premise excluding other adequate justifications, and that premise itself requires support. The revised universal denial now conflicts with B’s claim if this policy belongs to the specified kind and “just” has the same relevant sense. A strong response identifies those scope/meaning conditions and evaluates B’s independent right-based premise rather than treating a new argument as automatically successful. Fail equating failure of one proof with disproof of its conclusion or inventing an actual philosopher’s position.

**M6-I, commentary gloss.** Verify typographic/editorial boundaries, attribution and exact primary locus. If the gloss is commentary, cite it as interpretation and check primary support; if it is primary text, retain attribution after verifying translation/context. The thesis may survive as a defended reading, need narrowing or fail. Fail treating inclusion within a printed book as authorship evidence. This is a substantial argument repair, not only a citation-format correction.

## DEF anchors

An acceptable answer identifies the challenged premise and explains what changes if it fails. Source rejection needs a reason such as different question, unread argument, inadequate primary support or a genuine textual counterconsideration. “It disagrees with me” and “it is older” alone are inadequate. A source/version decision must be traceable. A/C need no artificial translation debate; B needs the actual identified rendering or explicit translator note.

A default: an imitation mechanism can weaken the evidential connection without proving absence of experience; the learner must say which premise changes. A alternate: loss of self-report does not remove all possible evidence, and any necessity claim needs defense. B default: identical predictions can coexist with different accounts of dispositions, reasons, or standards; textual evidence rather than the stipulated outcome establishes the difference. B alternate: spontaneous success does not settle stable character, developmental origin or future need. C default: reduced harm may be a genuine improvement while a structural relation persists; the remaining wrong must be argued, not merely labeled. C alternate: comprehension and credibility/uptake are distinct; full source conditions still matter.

An unscripted answer may revise the thesis. Grade responsiveness and reasons, not whether the original thesis survives. If an answer exposes a central textual uncertainty, return to the source before resolving the row.

## T1 case-specific anchors

**A-F1:** equal conversational performance does not establish equal functional profiles or settle experience. R and W foreground different features; the learner must reconstruct the chosen theoretical conditions and identify what is unspecified. Counting one feature each assumes comparable evidential weights and completeness, neither stipulated. Strong work distinguishes theory-relative implications from a combined judgment under uncertainty. Accept a reasoned challenge to transferring either theory. Relevant locators: A1 §§1–2, A4 I/III, A5 §§1.2/2.1–2.2/2.5. Fail a numerical verdict with no weighting argument.

**A-F2:** identical reports do not by themselves settle the process or moral status. Q’s error feedback is not stipulated to be suffering. A2 raises the relation between program/process and understanding; it should not be cited as a complete moral theory. A4/A5 §4.1 invite distinguishing experience, valence and moral concern. Strong work can recommend a precaution conditionally while separating it from a proved attribution. Fail claiming lookup mechanism alone settles every possible metaphysical account or that a negative error signal is felt pain.

**B-F1:** a lapse under deprivation does not by itself establish absence of prior capacities or the irrelevance of cultivation. Distinguish developmental explanation from responsibility for a particular action. B2 6A1–10, including6A8, is relevant to conditions sustaining or damaging development; exact reading must be checked. B3 must be represented independently rather than treated as denying circumstances matter. B1 requires attention to particulars of threat, knowledge and alternatives. Strong work explains what a shared concern with circumstances does not settle about each account. Fail inferring universal blame or no prior disposition from the stipulated lapse. Irwin/Van Norden/Hutton sources remain essential for attribution.

**B-F2:** supervised conformity, unsupervised conduct, motive and stable character must be distinguished. The later change is evidence relevant to cultivation but repetition alone does not establish the claimed endorsement. Avoid a modern test imposed as every author’s criterion. Strong work explains different textual standards and identifies what further case facts matter. Fail treating all observable compliance as identical virtue, or declaring the development meaningless because training occurred. Exact B1–B3 source verification remains essential.

**C-F1:** the case stipulates belief and comprehension, so a simple disbelief or missing-vocabulary account is incomplete. Classification and control over policy uptake are central; a defensible framework comparison explains their epistemic significance and considers administrative alternatives. Compensation can reduce material harm while leaving a mechanism intact. A response must state why that remaining mechanism is unjust under a cited criterion, not assume every filter is illegitimate. Check full C1/C2/C3/C4/C5 claims used; do not require all five in 800 words.

**C-F2:** accuracy improves, but derivative credibility through a spokesperson may preserve exclusion of original speakers and control of categories. A bounded remedy could add direct submission/appeal and participatory category revision; its tradeoffs might include privacy, workload or representation, argued rather than invented as facts. Strong work distinguishes belief in content from equal standing as a knower. Fail claiming improvement proves completion or that any proxy arrangement is inherently wrong regardless of conditions.
