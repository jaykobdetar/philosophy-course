# Course 6 — examiner notes (withhold during independent work)

These are diagnostic anchors, not compulsory moral verdicts or scripts to provide during an attempt. Read the [learner conditions](assessment.md) and [shared rubric](../../current/assessment.md). Record exact source/edition, assistance/exposure, criterion, evidence and uncertainty. An adequate response may defend a conclusion different from the anchor's illustrative direction. Mark a criterion **source-dependent** when its text cannot be inspected. Do not substitute agreement among models for that inspection.

## Essential decisions

The independent sample must demonstrate all three rows: normative theories; cultivation/interpretation; metaethics/applied reasoning. Each fresh row has 30 minutes. The essay gives depth, but cannot replace a missing row. A source-open response remains independent only without live content help, prepared answers or solution exposure. The 20-minute exchange and 30-minute delayed task are inside M08. Schedule final delayed transfer roughly 4–6 weeks after course completion or after two later modules; the shorter module retrieval and addition checks do not replace it. If a task was coached, use its alternate; after exposure to both, set and verify another comparable case before claiming fresh evidence.

**Developing:** relevant vocabulary or verdict, but an important theory, attribution or inferential step is missing. **Competent:** faithful source reconstruction, explicit case-to-principle link, direct answer and appropriately limited conclusion. **Strong:** a consequential ambiguity/objection is handled with evidence, rival reasoning is tested fairly, and the remaining limit is clear. Excellent expression cannot cancel an attribution error. Fabricated quotations or unacknowledged borrowing require correction before assessment can proceed. Use [the annotated comparison](worked-comparison.md) for practice calibration only.

## Fresh Form A anchors

**F8A.1 normative:** Equal consequences need not establish identical character or moral worth. Kant distinguishes acting in accordance with duty from acting from duty; do not equate mere sympathy with Kantian moral worth automatically. Aristotle II.4 requires the agent’s knowledge, choice for the act’s own sake and settled state; one act does not establish a stable virtue. Mill distinguishes the morality of an action from the worth of the agent and the role of motive, while actual consequences may depend on dispositions over time. The stipulation limits immediate effects, not every counterfactual. Require two accurate accounts, explicit evaluative dimensions, an objection and a justified limit; disagreement between theories need not be forced.

**F8A.2 interpretation:** In 6A6 the assessor should check the opening report of rival views and the argument linking human capacities/responses to goodness. Distinguish initial resources from a completed character and identify the evidence for the claimed scope. The friend/stranger case tests extension and development; it does not by itself establish or disprove an innate tendency. Aristotle II.1 distinguishes capacities and acquired states. Require an argument, passage attribution and one limit of equivalence. **Provisional until the actual Van Norden passage is inspected**; do not grade the precise English wording from these general anchors.

**F8A.3 metaethics/application:** Voluntary undertaking and reliance add premises absent from the original nonconsensual violinist case; short wait and safe transfer matter separately. A response should not derive unlimited entitlement merely from personhood or unlimited permission merely from ownership of the body. Hume can be used to identify the bridge to an ought; Ayer to distinguish evaluative expression from factual assertion/report. Do not require a unique verdict on the exact promise case. Require the learner to explain what the added facts do to the inference and which question remains disputed.

## Alternate Form B anchors

**F8B.1:** Equal immediate benefits do not decide whether institutional partiality is permissible. An Aristotelian response must apply the assigned account of practical wisdom and character to relationship and role; do not require the unassigned Book V theory of justice; a Kantian response must specify the maxim and defend the alleged universal-law or humanity implication rather than simply declare favoritism contradictory; a Millian response can consider confidence, future practices and impartial consideration, while marking any unstipulated effects as assumptions. Private assistance and public selection have different roles and claims, but that distinction requires argument. Accept a defended verdict for either policy; require two theory-to-case paths and one tested objection. Neither the lottery nor sibling preference is the compulsory answer.

**F8B.2:** Check 6A7's specific comparisons and their role in explaining common capacities and different development. The pupil example may illustrate environmental explanation, but a claim about those pupils' actual psychology needs evidence. Aristotle II.4's distinction between doing a kind of action and having the corresponding state provides a bounded comparison. Translation-specific and detailed analogy judgments remain **provisional until source inspection**. Strong answers can question whether a shared capacity establishes the normative conclusion rather than accepting the analogy automatically.

**F8B.3:** The immediate independent survival removes the need to equate withdrawal with causing death. Thomson's final qualification distinguishes detachment from a guarantee of death; a permission for the former does not entail the latter. The evaluative-expression claim is metaethical and does not itself produce permission. Hume concerns the unexplained inferential transition, not the same semantic theory. A candidate can defend an alternative practical verdict only by supplying its additional argument.

## Delayed anchors

**D1:** The new reliable evidence changes the present choice; a benevolent aim does not automatically establish practical wisdom, duty or beneficial consequences. Require two source-grounded analyses, distinguishing sincerity from justified persistence and a particular judgment from a claim about settled character. **D1B:** Actual effects, justified deliberation, blame and stable character are different evaluative dimensions. The stipulated unforeseeability prevents inventing negligence; a defensible theory may still treat actual effects as significant. Require explicit reasons for which judgments vary and which need not, not the slogan that outcomes never matter.

**D2:** Inspect 6A9 in the actual translation before assessing the attention/instruction analogy; it should not be treated as a universal empirical proof about capacity. Compare one specific II.1 claim. **D2B:** check who advances the internal/external account of righteousness in 6A5 and how the dialogue tests it; do not infer the contents from the prompt. Its comparison with habituation may expose a limit rather than a positive equivalence. Both are source-dependent until inspection.

**D3:** A right to life does not, without another premise, establish entitlement to this particular helper when safe substitution exists. The positive permission inference from an attitude theory needs its own normative support. Ayer's evaluative use differs from reporting one's feelings; Hume's challenge differs from both. **D3B:** an undertaking may matter but does not settle its scope or whether an adequate substitute fulfills it. Ask what was promised and what right is claimed.

## Added packet anchors

**A27/B27/D27:** Distinguish a proposed definition, an error claim, and attitude expression; do not mark them as interchangeable skepticism. Moore's challenge concerns an analysis rather than a direct verdict. For Mackie require the contextual distinction, an actual argument and objection; mere disagreement is insufficient. A positive permission or tolerance rule requires additional practical support. Mark Mackie reconstruction source-dependent if the specified §§1–3, 7–9, 12 are inaccessible. The correct diagnosis is about inference, not mandatory allegiance to realism or anti-realism.

**A33:** Both an occupational title that allegedly decides everything and the inference from underdetermination to no reasons require examination. A strong response preserves real constraints, gives an argued responsibility link, and compares method with Kant. **B33:** uncertainty about alternative funding differs from inability to choose; do not assume guaranteed success or infer that the role removes responsibility. **D33:** endorsement/advice can inform a choice without automatically erasing agency; actual coercion or lack of knowledge may limit the objection. A merely repeated slogan about freedom is insufficient. Check the cited lecture paragraphs and translator. Do not award Macomber quotation credit for Mairet wording.

## Essay and exchange

For E7 inspect whether the four-to-six-entry dossier supports the actual thesis, whether the objection targets its strongest vulnerable step, and whether revision changes reasoning. In the two-theory case, a theory's label must be followed by an inferential route to the recommendation. In the Thomson prompt, failure of a universal prohibition is not proof of universal permission. In Mill, distinguish the three proof stages. In Kant, examine candid maxim selection and the alleged kind of contradiction.

Ask one of these after the written sample: “Which premise would you revise if the reliance assumption were false?”; “Which words in the passage support your comparison rather than a rival reading?”; “Does your metaethical answer entail your practical verdict, and why?” Select the question matching the actual response. Permit a written exchange. Give no hint until the independent answer is saved. A repair then uses the allocated practice block and a different fresh item; the later delayed item is another occasion.

Keep a record: criterion → submitted evidence → verified source or exact unavailable range → decision → next task. No learner pilot, expert endorsement, legal rights clearance, visual/accessibility QA, or actual model calibration is established by this document.
