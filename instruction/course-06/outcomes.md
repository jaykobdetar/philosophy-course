Three independent tasks of **30 minutes** each preserve the grid's 90-minute written allocation. The 20-minute exchange and 30-minute delayed check are already inside Module 8's eight hours. No row is inferred from a total score or from essay polish.

For each row record source actually inspected, artifact ID, excerpt/location in the learner's answer, assistance, prior exposure, criterion met or gap, repair task, and date for new independent evidence. Use **demonstrated**, **needs repair**, or **source-dependent**. Ayer's assigned chapter and Van Norden's passages require source access before definitive textual judgments. A public summary is not a substitute. Modern-edition wording for Kant/Aristotle also requires the actual translation when that wording matters.

Completion of the baseline is separate from completion of either addition. An unresolved Mackie task does not erase successfully demonstrated Thomson work, and the addition cannot substitute for missing Thomson evidence. The historical baseline is 120 hours; PC-27 and PC-33 each add eight. The four-hour later Course 12C credit concerns only the retained Sartre encounter/reconstruction, subject to the translation bridge and new work rule.
