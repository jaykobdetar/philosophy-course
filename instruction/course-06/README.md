# Course 6 — Ethics

**Complete authored sequence; not learner-piloted or externally validated.** Prerequisite: Course 1. The [accepted curriculum](../../current/courses/06.md) remains the 120-hour baseline. This directory supplies eight teaching modules, original practice cases, independent forms, and examiner guidance. Source access and verification limits are recorded in [the source audit](source-audit.md); a complete teaching packet does not establish that the learner possesses every reading.

The course asks how reasons about character, duty, welfare, cultivation, and bodily claims support a moral judgment. It also asks what a moral judgment is doing. Agreement with an author or tutor is never a criterion. A competent argument makes its premises and limits explicit, uses the source fairly, and meets a serious objection.
