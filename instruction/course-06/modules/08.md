# C06-M08 — Assessment and delayed check

**8 hours within the baseline.** Guiding question: can you independently transfer your reasoning beyond the essay's case? Prerequisites: saved P1–P6 and E7; addition artifacts if claiming the augmented course. Outcomes: demonstrate all three essential assessment rows; explain a source-based judgment under challenge; repair a weakness and show delayed transfer.

## Assigned work

No new book or commentator. Use the exact assigned passages already identified in Modules 2–6, particularly Mengzi 6A6/6A7 and Aristotle II.1/II.4 for the fresh passage tasks. Read [assessment.md](../assessment.md) for the learner rules. The examiner selects unseen Form A or B and reserves Form D for later; do not read examiner notes until attempts are saved. If an item was rehearsed, record exposure and replace it.

Assessment is evidence collection, not a contest over moral allegiance. A conclusion becomes assessable when the theory's principle, relevant case facts and linking inference are visible. The same case verdict can result from distinct reasons; a theory label cannot stand in for those reasons. A passage paraphrase should preserve the speaker, claim, qualification and analogy. The final question concerns the kind of support an answer earns, not whether it sounds confident.

## Worked evidence audit

Invented learner record: a polished Mill essay demonstrates detailed welfare reasoning, but the independent response merely says “Kant would disagree” and the Mengzi response has no passage citation. The record has depth but unresolved normative-comparison and interpretation rows. The appropriate repair is a maxim reconstruction and a new source-open passage explanation; awarding a high average for prose would leave those outcomes untested. Conversely, a learner who reaches a different library verdict with accurate theory, explicit premises and a serious objection may meet the criteria.

Make a grid with one row for normative theories, cultivation/interpretation, and metaethics/applied reasoning. For each, record a named practice artifact, independent item, source checked, observation, assistance/exposure, and status: **demonstrated / needs repair / source-dependent**. A missing artifact or inaccessible assigned passage is unresolved, not a zero to average away. If Ayer or Van Norden is unavailable, retain provisional evidence and name the exact source needed; do not certify those diagnoses from a summary.

The roughly 4–6-week interval (or two later modules) is calendar spacing, not additional billed study. The delayed 30 minutes is reserved here and is not added to the baseline. Module-local delayed retrieval blocks remain their own distinct short exercises. Do not relabel the same response as both fresh and delayed evidence.

The twenty-minute exchange asks the learner to name the strongest objection to one answer, identify its target, and revise or defend the response with a specific passage. Provide the question in writing and permit a written answer when appropriate; do not grade accent, speed, or confidence. If examiner content help is supplied, record it and arrange another independent form.

## Repair and completion

For a normative failure, state the theory's relevant principle, the new fact, and the inference in three separate lines, then test the changed version. For an interpretation failure, annotate the exact passage and give two rival readings before choosing. For a metaethical failure, separate a claim about the meaning/status of moral claims from a proposed practical rule. For a Thomson failure, write the claimed conclusion with its quantifier and name the additional premise required to generalize it.

Submit the grid, untouched forms, exchange record, revision/repair record, delayed response and a 200-word next-step memo. Completion requires credible independent evidence in each row; it does not require agreement with the examiner. Persistent gaps route back to the relevant module with an exact task. After baseline completion, proceed to Course 7 under the curriculum route; retain the PC-33 transfer dossier if planning Course 12C. Source audit, learner pilot, human expert review, tutor calibration and rendered accessibility review are distinct statuses; this authored package claims none of those experiential validations.
