## Working method and notation

Read a short section; attempt the example yourself; compare the explanation; complete practice; obtain feedback; revise; close the answer guidance before the independent task. Record date, task ID, source revision, attempt, error type, time, assistance and exposure. Use [outcomes](outcomes.md) to track each family separately. Reserve [assessment](assessment.md) until Module 8 and keep [examiner notes](examiner-notes.md) closed during all independent tasks. If an answer has been seen, mark the item exposed and use the specified alternate form.

Use `¬ ∧ ∨ → ↔ ∀ ∃ =`, names `a` through `r`, variables `s` through `z`, predicates such as `F(x)` and `R(x,y)`, and explicit brackets. `⊥` is contradiction; `⊢` is derivability; `⊨` is semantic entailment. `a ≠ b` abbreviates `¬a=b`. In printed proofs, `PR` marks a premise, `AS` an assumption, and vertical bars show open subproofs. Rule labels match the book: `R, ∧I, ∧E, ∨I, ∨E, →I, →E, ↔I, ↔E, ¬I, ¬E, IP, X`, and later `DS, MT, DNE, LEM, DeM, ∀I, ∀E, ∃I, ∃E, CQ, =I, =E`. See chapter 19 for exact additional-rule schemes. Rules apply to whole formulas, not arbitrarily inside them. Paper proofs are fully supported. **Carnap is not a dependency and has not been tested for these packets.**

For listening, speak each symbol and bracket: “for every x, open bracket F of x implies G of x, close bracket.” Read a proof with line number, active assumptions, formula, rule and cited lines. Pause to reconstruct the written formula. Every audio activity has the same written prompt and permits a written answer or transcript; diagrams also have explicit domain/tuple lists. Listening does not replace checking quantifier scope, citations or proof indentation against accessible text. No audiobook, TTS rendering or assistive technology has been validated here.

Finish when every essential outcome has both practice and fresh independent evidence, with a delayed sample of the least secure family. A high total cannot compensate for a recurring scope, witness-name or assumption-discharge error.
