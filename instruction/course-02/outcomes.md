The four essential rows in the current assessment grid map as follows: **symbolization and scope → O2, A1/B1 (30 min); semantics → O3, A2/B2 (30 min); proof → O4 and O5, A3/B3 (60 min); modeling limits → O1 and O5, A4/B4 (20 min)**. Total: **140 minutes**, inside Module 8's 16 hours. All rows have module practice and an independent fresh task. Formal correctness and faithful English symbolization are assessed separately.

Create a record with columns: outcome; module artifact and date; fresh form/task and date; assistance/exposure; observed success; error and repair; delayed result. Use competent/developing/unresolved with a sentence of evidence, not an aggregate pass percentage. Competent means a new varied case completed independently with relevant reasoning. Strong adds justified transfer between representations. A missing sample is unresolved. Distinguish a transcription slip corrected without help from a recurring conceptual error. Source locators and help declarations are necessary evidence, not decoration.

Intentional exclusions: modal logic chapters 42–44; optional metatheory chapters 45–48; automated theorem-prover training; mathematical completeness proofs; probability. Course 2's reading of chapters 22 and 41 teaches what soundness/completeness assert without claiming to prove the metatheorems.
