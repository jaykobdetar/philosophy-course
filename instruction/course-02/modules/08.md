# C02-M08 — What has the formal result established?

**Prerequisites:** M1–M7 practice with independent competence in syntax, semantics and proof. **Outcomes:** integrate O1–O5, defend a formalization, identify limits, demonstrate fresh and delayed transfer. **Reading:** review the pinned textbook chapters 13, 22, 28–30, 36, 39 and 41 as needed; no new required chapters beyond Parts I–VII. Access in [README](../README.md).

**16 hours:** paper reading/model planning 2; paper drafting and formal work 4; independent assessment 2 hours 20 minutes; explanation exchange 40 minutes; feedback 2; revision 2; delayed check 1; retrieval including M7-D and evidence audit 2. Total exactly 16. The delayed hour includes a 30-minute attempt and 30 minutes of feedback/logging. All are inside the historical 150 hours.

## Modeling is an argued decision

Formal validity is conditional on the model and premises. To model an argument: (1) identify its asserted reasons and conclusion; (2) fix a domain and a key with stable predicates; (3) expose a substantive ambiguity before selecting a reading; (4) translate each claim separately; (5) test the inference by proof or countermodel; (6) translate the result back and state what remains unsettled. If a hidden premise repairs an invalid form, defend that premise separately. Validity does not make a false empirical generalization, a disputed ethical principle or an unfaithful paraphrase true.

Invented worked mini-case: “Every policy that treats like cases alike is fair. Policy a treats like cases alike. So a is fair.” Domain policies, `L(x)` x treats like cases alike, `F(x)` x is fair. From `∀x(L(x)→F(x))` and `L(a)`, instantiate `L(a)→F(a)` then use →E to obtain `F(a)`. That proof establishes the formal consequence. Whether likeness has been specified properly and whether like treatment suffices for fairness are substantive objections to the premises or key. They are not defects in →E.

## Paper: 800–1,000 words

Use this original philosophical teaching argument (not attributed to a historical author): “Every action that respects autonomy is permissible. Some well-intentioned action respects no one's autonomy. Therefore some well-intentioned action is not permissible.” Interpret “respects no one's autonomy” as the negation of the one-place property “respects autonomy” for the initial TFL/FOL model, and explicitly discuss what that abstraction loses. Domain actions; `A(x)` respects autonomy; `P(x)` permissible; `W(x)` well-intentioned. Do not assume the first premise is a biconditional.

Write a paper with: question and key (100–150 words); faithful reconstruction and alternative reading (200–250); countermodel or proof and prose explanation (200–250); one proposed premise repair and objection to it (200–250); conclusion and limitations (100). The sections are planning guides; final prose must total 800–1,000 words. Include your finite model as a small table outside the word count. Cite relevant textbook chapter/section for formal methods, and identify the argument as invented. If choosing a real passage instead, obtain a short authorized passage with exact source/edition/locator and preserve the same outputs; the assessor must inspect it before judging fidelity.

The decision procedure is bounded: first test the given inference; next add **one** sufficient repair premise; then explain whether that premise is a defensible philosophical principle. Do not rewrite the original first premise silently or claim that a countermodel establishes a real action's permissibility. An alternative philosophical position is acceptable if reconstruction and inferential diagnosis are sound.

Time is a planning allocation rather than a speed criterion; accessible breaks and adjusted attempt duration are logged. A polished paper cannot replace a missing quantified proof. Reassessment uses an unexposed form; an item already solved with help is practice evidence only.

Select its specified tasks with the least secure family receiving priority; if more than one family remains unresolved, use the one-hour allocation for a broader attempt/feedback split and record further needed time explicitly. **Next:** close the outcome grid when each family has practice and fresh evidence and the delayed sample is stable; otherwise return to the named module's repair task.
