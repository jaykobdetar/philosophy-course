# Analytic essay rubric

Used for every paper, from the first Introduction essay to the thesis. It stays the same across the whole program, so rising scores reflect real development rather than changing criteria.

| Criterion | Points | What excellent work demonstrates |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem and thesis | 15 | A precise, contestable answer to a clearly defined philosophical problem |
| Reconstruction and charity | 20 | The views discussed are represented accurately, explicitly, and in their strongest reasonable form |
| Reasoning | 25 | Premises support conclusions; key assumptions are identified; distinctions do real argumentative work |
| Objection and reply | 15 | A serious objection rather than a straw man, followed by a reasoned response |
| Textual and scholarly evidence | 15 | Claims about philosophers are supported; secondary literature is relevant and accurately used |
| Structure and prose | 10 | Clear signposting, disciplined paragraphs, precise terms, economical prose |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## What scores mean

- **Around 70:** a recognizable philosophical argument with substantial weaknesses.
- **80s:** generally accurate reconstruction and competent reasoning. **80 is the gate standard.**
- **Above 90:** genuine control: a precise thesis, charitable handling of alternatives, serious objections, strong replies, and clean organization.

Don't award a high score because the prose sounds sophisticated. The thesis is scored on the same criteria, with higher expectations for originality and engagement with the literature (see [Research & Thesis](../curriculum/units/research-thesis.md)).
