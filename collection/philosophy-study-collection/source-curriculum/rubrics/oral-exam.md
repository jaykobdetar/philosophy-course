# Oral exam rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 4 after the exam, citing evidence from the learner's answers.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Misstates the material | Frequent errors | Mostly accurate, some errors | Accurate | Accurate and precise, including fine distinctions |
| Reasoning | No argument | Assertions with weak support | Basic reasons | Well-supported arguments | Arguments that anticipate objections |
| Responsiveness | Doesn't answer the question asked | Answers drift | Generally answers the question | Answers directly and completely | Answers directly and sees what the question is really after |
| Conceptual precision | Key terms confused | Terms used loosely | Terms mostly right | Terms used precisely | Draws and uses new distinctions under pressure |
| Revision under pressure | Can't respond to challenges | Repeats the original claim | Adjusts, but awkwardly | Revises appropriately and explains why | Turns a challenge into a stronger position |

Pass standards for each stage are in [gates.md](gates.md).
