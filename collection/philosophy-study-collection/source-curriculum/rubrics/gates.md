# Gate standards

Every unit ends with a gate. Passing it, then passing a confirmation check about four weeks later, makes a unit **mastered**, as long as its prerequisites are mastered too. Anyone can take a gate at any time, including to test out of a unit they already know.

These thresholds are defaults. If you change them, record the change in [docs/decisions.md](../docs/decisions.md).

## Standard gate

Used for every unit except Logic I and Research & Thesis.

| Component | Foundations | Core | Advanced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Written exam, no AI help | 60 minutes; 75% overall | 90 minutes; 75% overall | 90 minutes; 75% overall |
| Unfamiliar-passage question (part of the exam) | At least half marks | At least half marks | At least half marks |
| Oral exam, by voice | 20 minutes; no dimension below 2; average 2.5 or more | 30 minutes; no dimension below 2; average 3 or more | 30 minutes; no dimension below 2; average 3 or more |
| Paper, revised after feedback | 1,000–1,500 words; 80 or more | 2,000–3,000 words; 80 or more | 3,500–5,000 words; 80 or more |
| Dashboard | Reading, argumentation, and writing at 2 or more | Reading, argumentation, and writing at 3 or more (history units: historical understanding at 3 or more too) | As for Core, plus research at 3 or more |

Exam format: [templates/written-exam.md](../templates/written-exam.md). Oral scoring: [oral-exam.md](oral-exam.md). Paper scoring: [analytic-essay.md](analytic-essay.md).

## Logic I gate

- **Problem sets:** at least 80% of the unit's problem sets completed, with hint use recorded.
- **Written exam, no AI help (90 minutes):** symbolization, truth tables, natural deduction proofs in TFL and FOL, and an interpretation that shows an argument invalid; 75% overall.
- **Oral exam, by voice (15 minutes):** explain the strategy behind a proof and what an invalidating interpretation shows; no dimension below 2, average 2.5 or more.
- **Paper (1,000–1,500 words):** formalize an argument from a philosophical text and explain what its formal validity does and doesn't establish; 80 or more.
- **Dashboard:** logic at 3 or more.

## Research & Thesis gate

The capstone is gated at each milestone; see [the unit](../curriculum/units/research-thesis.md). The final standards: a thesis of 8,000–12,000 words scoring 80 or more on the essay rubric; a presentation (20–30 minutes) and oral defense (30–45 minutes) with no oral dimension below 3; and research and oral defense at 3 or more on the dashboard.

## Confirmation check

About four weeks after a gate is passed:

- **Retrieval quiz, no AI help (20 minutes):** mixed items from the unit; 70% or more.
- **Spoken explanation (5 minutes):** reconstruct one central argument chosen by the Examiner, clearly enough that the Examiner could write it down in standard form.

If the check fails, the Director sets targeted review items and the check is repeated in about two weeks. The gate isn't repeated.

## Retakes

Retake only the component that failed, after targeted work. Every attempt is recorded, and there's no penalty.
