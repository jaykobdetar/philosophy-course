# Elective: a contemporary area

**Stage:** Advanced
**Requires:** [Introduction to Philosophy](introduction.md) (each option may add more)
**Status:** outline

## What this unit is for

Working in a live area of current philosophy: mapping a debate, reading recent work, and taking a position in it.

## Scope benchmark

Pending, for each option: compare with 2–3 current syllabi and record them here.

## Options

Choose one. Before starting, the chosen option gets a full outline with its own anchor readings, and those readings get catalog entries.

- **Philosophy of science:** Hempel, Popper, Kuhn, van Fraassen. Also requires [Logic I](logic-1.md).
- **Philosophy of AI and technology:** machine minds, machine ethics, and alignment. Builds on [Philosophy of Mind](philosophy-of-mind.md).
- **Feminist philosophy:** de Beauvoir, Haslanger, Langton.
- **Philosophy of race:** Du Bois, Appiah, Mills, Haslanger.
- **Philosophy of law:** Hart, Dworkin.
- **Environmental ethics.**
- **Logic II:** modal logic and metatheory, using the later parts of *forall x: Calgary*. Also requires Logic I.

**Extra electives.** A full medieval unit (going beyond [Ancient & Medieval Philosophy](ancient-medieval.md)) can be taken in addition to the two required electives, but it doesn't fill this slot, which is for a contemporary area.

## Mastery criteria

1. Map the main positions in a current debate in the area, with the strongest argument for each.
2. Reconstruct and assess at least two recent contributions to the debate.
3. Find and evaluate the scholarly literature independently.
4. Take and defend a position in a 3,500–5,000-word paper.
