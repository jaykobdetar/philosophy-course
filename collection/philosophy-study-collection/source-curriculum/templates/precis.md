# Précis format

300–500 words, written by the learner without AI help and before any secondary reading.

**Text:** author, title, and the part read (with standard reference numbers where they exist)

1. **The question.** What problem is the text trying to answer? (1–2 sentences)
2. **The thesis.** What's the author's answer? (1 sentence)
3. **The argument.** How does the author get there? Numbered steps, in your own words.
4. **Key terms.** Any technical terms, and how the author uses them.
5. **Your question.** One thing you don't understand or don't believe, stated as precisely as you can.
