# Written exam format

Generated by the Examiner when the learner is ready to start, and taken without AI help or notes.

**Unit:**

## Section A: distinctions and concepts (about 25%)

Short answers: define, distinguish, give an example.

## Section B: an assigned text (about 40%)

Reconstruct an argument from the unit's readings and assess it.

## Section C: unfamiliar passage (about 35%)

A short passage from a text not studied in the unit. Identify the conclusion, reconstruct the argument, and assess its strongest and weakest points. This section needs at least half marks to pass the gate.

---

**Grading** (by the Examiner): marks for each question with evidence, the total, pass or not yet, and how the grade was produced (for example, "separate session, memory off").
