# Review queue

Maintained by the Director. Intervals: 3 days → 1 week → 3 weeks → 2 months → 6 months → 1 year, then yearly. A pass moves an item to the next interval; a miss sends it back to 3 days.

| Item | Unit | Kind | Interval | Next due | Last result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Kind:** explain (a distinction or concept), reconstruct (an argument, from memory), apply (a view to a new case), identify (a passage's author and claim), or confirmation (a unit's confirmation check).
