# Introduction and Logic I readings

Downloaded and checked on 30 September 2026. These are actual reading files, not link placeholders. Plato's three Introduction dialogues are stored in the separate public-domain text collection.

## Required: Introduction

- **Descartes: Meditations I–II.** Open `required/descartes_veitch_1912_scan.pdf`, PDF pages **113–128** (printed pp. 79–94). The entire volume is included; all six Meditations occupy PDF pages **113–177**. Meditation VI, needed later, is PDF pages **161–177**. John Veitch translation, Everyman/Dent/Dutton, 1912. [Internet Archive source](https://archive.org/details/discourseonmetho1912desc)
  - The accompanying `_fulltext.txt` is the Archive's searchable OCR. It has transcription errors; check quotations against the scan
  - This edition has no AT pagination in the reading text. For precise AT citations, use a scholarly edition such as the curriculum's recommended Cress translation. The introductory target remains AT VII 17–34
  - This public-domain alternative does not include the Elisabeth correspondence
- **Gettier: “Is Justified True Belief Knowledge?”** Open `required/gettier_bilingual_disputatio_2013.pdf`, PDF pages **2–4**. This is the complete English article, followed by a Spanish translation. The English is re-typeset in an author-authorized 2013 edition; its printed pages 186–188 differ from the original *Analysis* 23(6), 1963, pp. 121–123. [Journal record](https://disputatio.usal.es/vols/vol-2-no-3/gettier-conocimiento/)
- **Singer: “Famine, Affluence, and Morality.”** Open `required/singer_famine_affluence_morality_1972.pdf`, PDF pages **2–16**, preserving the original *Philosophy & Public Affairs* 1(3), 1972, pp. 229–243. PDF page 1 is the JSTOR cover. [University of Colorado Denver source](https://clas.ucdenver.edu/hbsc/sites/default/files/attached-files/singer_famine_affluence_and_morality_2.pdf)

## Required: Logic I

Open `required/forallx_calgary_textbook.pdf`. Use **Parts I–VII**, printed pp. **1–335**, for the prescribed Logic I material. Modal logic and metatheory, also present in the full book, are for the later elective.

The local book is *forall x: Calgary*, Fall 2025, revision **c5cb415 (2026-09-27)**, by P. D. Magnus, Tim Button, Robert Trueman, and Richard Zach, with contributions by J. Robert Loftis and Aaron Thomas-Bolduc. [Project site](https://forallx.openlogicproject.org/)

## Solutions: open after attempting the problems

`solutions/forallx_calgary_solutions.pdf` is the complete official **Solutions to Selected Exercises** booklet, Fall 2025, revision **80a85da (2026-09-15)**. It intentionally does not answer every problem.

The book and solutions have different build revisions, but the [upstream comparison](https://github.com/rzach/forallx-yyc/compare/80a85da...c5cb415) shows only accessibility/build-style changes, with no chapter or exercise changes. Use these saved copies together rather than independently replacing one from the moving download links.

## Optional

The prose is available offline; some links and styling refer to online resources. [Author's page](https://www.jimpryor.net/teaching/guidelines/writing.html)
