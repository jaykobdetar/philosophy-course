Classical-source private study packet

Use the selected Aristotle and Anselm TXT files first; complete Aristotle works are included for context. Hegel texts preserve paragraph numbers. Leibniz PDF is the 1902 Montgomery volume; its OCR is supplied for searching and may have errors.

IMPORTANT EDITION NOTES
- Elisabeth/Descartes is Jonathan Bennett's modernized reading aid, not the Lisa Shapiro edition. Do not quote it in assessed work.
- Hume is a 1757 Wikisource transcription, not the curriculum's Hume Texts Online edition. Original spelling is retained.
- Thomson is a copyrighted university-hosted teaching copy for private study only, not a redistribution license.
- De Anima's J. A. Smith translation is not US public domain until 2027, but MIT explicitly permits personal downloading.
- Marx's Estranged Labour remains online-only: https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1844/manuscripts/labour.htm
