# Stopping and continuation milestones

These are study-planning checkpoints, not credit, a degree or certification. Hours below are **September 30 source baselines** and exclude any later adopted work, selected bridges or learner-specific remediation. Keep a record of independently demonstrated outcome groups and the edition read. On returning, continue from the next unmet outcome rather than repeating credited work.

| Checkpoint | Source-baseline hours | Evidence to retain | What remains |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Course 1 methods | 120 | Interpretation, reconstruction, counterexample, philosophical writing and a fresh response, using the [Course 1 grid](assessment.md#course-1). | Formal logic, history and later subject courses. The opening four sessions alone do not complete this checkpoint. |
| Courses 1–2 methods and logic | 270 | Course 1 work and the independent symbolization, semantics, proof and modeling-limits samples in the [Course 2 grid](assessment.md#course-2). | The historical and topical curriculum. A first logic packet alone does not complete Course 2. |
| Courses 1–6 methods, history and ethics | 730 | The independently sampled groups in the [Courses 3–6 grids](assessment.md#course-3) as well as Courses 1–2. | Knowledge/reality, political philosophy, Kant, science and selected depth. |
| Courses 1–10 common core | 1,270 | All course grids through [Course 10](assessment.md#course-10), including Kant and science. | One Course 11 tradition option, one Course 12 area, one Course 13 seminar and the capstone. Introductory language coverage is incomplete on routes without 12A until the PC-28 bridge is authored and completed; its proposed 20 hours are not inside this baseline. |
| Selected fourteen-course route | 1,890 for default 11A/12A/13A | The selected option grids, seminar evidence and all eight capstone milestones. | Later adopted additions and any optional bridge must be added to the individual route ledger. No revised total has been verified. |

The exact [route and prerequisites](../data/course.json) determine which alternative option can follow. The needs-based Course 10 probability bridge is proposed as six **additional** hours only if taken; it is not yet an authored packet or a prerequisite to start Course 1. A learner who takes 12A will eventually credit its language foundation once, not also take and bill a duplicate bridge. A route that omits 12A cannot yet claim that proposed introductory language outcome.

The [outcome evidence grids](assessment.md#outcome-evidence-grids) specify the existing course-level practice and independent samples. Only the [opening Course 1 packets](../instruction/course-01/README.md) currently have session-level materials; later checkpoints describe what completion would mean once readings and packets are verified.
