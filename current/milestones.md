# Stopping and continuation milestones

These are study-planning checkpoints, not credit, a degree or certification. Hours below are **September 30 source baselines** and exclude any later adopted work, selected bridges or learner-specific remediation. Keep a record of independently demonstrated outcome groups and the edition read. On returning, continue from the next unmet outcome rather than repeating credited work.

| Checkpoint | Source-baseline hours | Evidence to retain | What remains |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Course 1 methods | 120 | Interpretation, reconstruction, counterexample, philosophical writing and a fresh response, using the [Course 1 grid](assessment.md#course-1). | Formal logic, history and later subject courses. The opening four sessions alone do not complete this checkpoint. |
| Courses 1–2 methods and logic | 270 | Course 1 work and the independent symbolization, semantics, proof and modeling-limits samples in the [Course 2 grid](assessment.md#course-2). | The historical and topical curriculum. A first logic packet alone does not complete Course 2. |
| Courses 1–6 methods, history and ethics | 730 | The independently sampled groups in the [Courses 3–6 grids](assessment.md#course-3) as well as Courses 1–2. | Knowledge/reality, political philosophy, Kant, science and selected depth. |
| Courses 1–10 common core | 1,270 | All course grids through [Course 10](assessment.md#course-10), including Kant and science. | One Course 11 tradition option, one Course 12 area, one Course 13 seminar and the capstone. Introductory language coverage is incomplete on routes without 12A until the [PC-28 foundation and Grice packets](../instruction/course-12/additions/pc28-foundation.md) are completed; their combined 24 hours are not inside this baseline. |
| Selected fourteen-course route | 1,890 for default 11A/12A/13A | The selected option grids, seminar evidence and all eight capstone milestones. | Use the [authored route ledger](../instruction/workload.md) for additions, optional support and earned overlap credit; default 1,970 planned hours. |

The exact [route and prerequisites](../data/course.json) determine which alternative option can follow. The [authored milestone ledger](../instruction/workload.md#stopping-milestones) gives 120 / 270 / 756 / 1,336 planned hours through Courses 1 / 2 / 6 / 10. Introductory language work at the Course 10 stop adds 24 hours, giving 1,360; on continuation, credit the same foundation and Grice work once. C12A's foundation is already inside its baseline; other C12 routes use the shared bridge. The [probability bridge](../instruction/course-10/probability-bridge.md) adds six hours only when needed.

The [outcome grids](assessment.md#outcome-evidence-grids) preserve the accepted course outcomes. [All teaching packets and detailed maps](../instruction/README.md) are authored, with [source dependencies](../instruction/source-status.md) explicit. A planned hour total does not establish actual learner completion, measured effort or external certification.
