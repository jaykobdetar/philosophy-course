# Course 11 Sustained study of a global tradition

Choose one complete option. The recommended route is Classical Chinese Philosophy because it develops a conversation already begun in ethics and offers contrasting methods of philosophical inquiry. Africana Philosophy is an equally legitimate alternative, especially after political philosophy. Neither option claims to represent every tradition outside Europe. Indian thought is encountered in Course 7 and Islamic/Jewish thought in Course 4; further sustained study remains an honest extension opportunity.
