## Start here

Begin with one rested ninety-minute session. Use your existing collection and the linked free marked reading copies and guide. Save your own first attempt; it is a starting point, not a graded test.

If you need longer, extend the session or split it. These ninety minutes count inside Course 1; do not repeat the diagnostic when you reach its module list. Ask for feedback on one or two important issues rather than a rewritten paragraph.

## The route and commitment

Fourteen courses: methods and logic; eight common historical and topical courses; Chinese or Africana philosophy; one selected area; an advanced seminar; and a research capstone. The default depth route is Chinese philosophy, Mind and Language, then AI Language and Consciousness. Later choices have a compatibility map; no choice is needed now.

The default planning estimate is 1,890 focused hours. At five to seven hours a week and forty-six study weeks a year, that is about 5.9–8.2 years. There is no deadline. Actual pace will be calibrated; optional work audio does not replace focused reading and writing.

This is a university-informed philosophy education with inspectable work. It awards no credit and does not certify equality with a university degree. The first useful milestone is the initial reading, reasoning, and writing course, not the distant capstone.
