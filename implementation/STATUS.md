## Meaning of completion

**Authored and content-reviewed** means the teaching work exists, its stated scope has been independently reviewed by AI contexts, and findings have been repaired and rechecked. It does not mean all assigned editions were inspected or every reading is locally available. Use the per-course progress and review record for the precise accepted inventory.

**Ready for a particular reading-dependent task** requires lawful access to the assigned text and usable passage boundaries. The course audit identifies what was actually inspected. Missing access blocks the affected task, not unrelated authored work.

**Learner completion** requires the learner's own independent evidence for each essential outcome, repairs where needed and delayed transfer. No real learner pilot, human specialist certification, rights clearance or rendered accessibility result is claimed. The planned default route is 1,970 hours, with six optional probability hours only if taken; this is not measured effort.
